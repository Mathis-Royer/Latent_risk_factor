## Five questions

1. **Purpose and original intent.** Replace a hand-chosen factor count with a model-determined one: "K is a **capacity ceiling**, not the number of factors the model will use" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:404`), because "A VAE with K = 200 on data supporting 80 factors converges to AU ≈ 80 with ~120 inactive dimensions" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:406`); overhead should be read off as active units (AU), "Dimensions with marginal KL > 0.01 nats" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:404`). The normative version is strongest in `strategy_philosophy.md:75` ("The number of risk factors is not chosen in advance") and `strategy_philosophy.md:82` ("the strategy does not require a human decision about how many risk factors exist in the market"). K is also given three secondary functions — exploration space, noise buffer, diagnostic (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:420`) — and the downstream covariance imposes a fourth, statistical one (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:422-436`). The unit is therefore simultaneously an architecture choice, an estimator, a diagnostic and a capacity guard.

2. **State of the art for this objective.** The target is a *model-based estimate of effective dimensionality with a null distribution*, not a thresholded count. (a) A posterior over the number of active dimensions: stick-breaking VAE priors let K be an upper bound and return a distribution over the used dimensions (Nalisnick & Smyth, 2017, *Stick-Breaking Variational Autoencoders*, ICLR — [unverified]). (b) A KL decomposition that separates *usage* from *redundancy*: β-TCVAE splits the aggregated KL into index-code MI, total correlation and dimension-wise KL (Chen, Li, Grosse & Duvenaud, 2018, *Isolating Sources of Disentanglement in VAEs*, NeurIPS — [unverified]); a count built on dimension-wise KL alone conflates the two. (c) A gauge-free effective dimension: effective rank = exp of the entropy of normalised singular values (Roy & Vetterli, 2007, *The effective rank: a measure of effective dimensionality*, EUSIPCO — [unverified]), i.e. participation ratio rather than a per-coordinate cut. (d) An identifiable latent basis: iVAE identifiability up to permutation given an auxiliary/time-conditioned prior (Khemakhem, Kingma, Monti & Hyvärinen, 2020, *Variational Autoencoders and Nonlinear ICA*, AISTATS — [unverified]) — the only route to "factor k means the same thing across folds". (e) External count checks: Bai & Ng (2002) IC₂ ([unverified], formula correctly quoted at `literature_review_strategy.md:224`) and eigenvalue-difference tests (Onatski, 2010, [unverified]). The classical ARD target (Bishop, 1999, *Bayesian PCA*, [unverified]) is the *benchmark object*, not the method: a principled deep model should produce a comparable posterior over dimensionality.

3. **What prevents it (from my role, several obstacles are only apparent).** Apparent: (i) "the p(p+1)/2 parameter budget forbids large AU" — shrinkage is precisely designed for p/n not small, so the budget is a chosen stance, not a theorem (see U06-ADV-04); (ii) "K ≥ 1.5–2× intrinsic dimension is required" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:420`) — asserted, not derived, and not needed if the count is estimated gauge-free. Real: the statistic is coordinate-dependent (U06-ADV-02), the count has no null model and no calibration against N, K or D (U06-ADV-05), and K is ultimately selected by portfolio OOS score (U06-ADV-06), which reinstates exactly the human decision the unit claims to remove. The unit is therefore conceptually ahead of its own statistic: the idea is right, the estimator is a threshold on a non-invariant quantity.

4. **Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: quadratic growth of Σ_z free parameters against a bounded N_obs (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:422-426`); non-identifiability of the latent basis, which the DVT acknowledges and gauges by rotation to principal factors (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:692`); amortisation of encoder parameters across ~10³ stocks; on non-CRSP vendor data, AU_max_stat lands near 60–85 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:430-433`). Self-imposed: the hard K ≤ 200 / range 50–300 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:999`) while simultaneously demanding K be "generous"; the constant 0.01 nats; the K grid itself; ranking truncation by marginal KL rather than by risk contribution; the count floor AU ≥ AU_PCA.

5. **What to remove, merge or move.** *Remove* the AU ≥ AU_PCA count floor (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:473`) — the explanatory-power floor (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:475`) is the sound test of the same thing; remove the Phase-2 sweep K ∈ {AU, 1.5AU, 2AU} selected by OOS score (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:418`); remove the "noise absorption buffer" as a justification (keep at most as hypothesis, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:420`); remove the inoperative estimation penalty term (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:935`). *Merge* activity, conditioning and rank into one basis-free effective-rank criterion on B_𝔸, which already has its instrument written down (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:759`). *Move* factor characterization from the entropy objective to the Σ_z layer, and delete the persistence-weighting of entropy (`literature_review_strategy.md:267`). *Keep* the H/ln(AU) normalization, the distance-based stability metric, and the EP floor.

## Core strengths to preserve
1. **K decoupled from the factor count** (U06-ADV-01). This is the unit's real contribution and the reason the strategy is not a fixed-rank linear model: capacity is an architectural allowance, the effective count is a posterior property. Any redesign must keep a K ceiling *and* a model-derived effective dimension — the defect is the estimator, not the architecture.
2. **The statistical guard as the binding constraint, not K** (U06-ADV-04, -11). The insight that the cost of factors lives in Σ_z's quadratic parameter count, evaluated on N_obs, is correct and rare; keep the guard object, fix its closed form, and keep it basis-free.
3. **Normalised entropy for cross-AU comparison** (U06-ADV-07), applied symmetrically to the VAE and PCA benchmark (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:172`). Without it the selection score is structurally biased by factor count; this is a fairness device, not a cosmetic one.
4. **Distance-based latent stability** (U06-ADV-10). The correct gauge-invariant comparison across retrainings; it should be the template for how the factor-count statistic itself is built.
5. **The explanatory-power floor EP ≥ EP_PCA + 0.10** (U06-ADV-09). The only criterion in the unit that can falsify the value of nonlinearity against a linear baseline — which is exactly the test the user's constraint demands.

## Verdict
This unit gets the architecture right and the statistic wrong, in a way that is precise and repairable. The central move — K as a capacity ceiling, the effective count read from the trained model — is theoretically legitimate (per-coordinate KL elimination), it is correctly motivated (a fixed rank would be a linear-model assumption in disguise), and it is correctly located economically (the binding cost is AU²/2 in Σ_z, not K in the encoder). Against that, the estimator is a fixed 0.01-nat cut on a quantity that is not gauge-invariant (a 45° rotation of the same fitted model changes AU from 2 to 1), with no null distribution, an activity threshold 50× looser than the prose claims, and an internal subsidy toward splitting variance across coordinates; it is then reconciled into the protocol by sweeping K on the OOS portfolio score, which reinstates the human factor-count decision the philosophy claims to abolish. The supporting documents make this worse: the half-life formula has a sign error, the proposed stability metric is one the DVT itself rules out, and entropy-weighting by persistence would break the equalization identity. None of these defects requires abandoning the deep-learning formulation — they require replacing a thresholded coordinate count with a gauge-free effective dimension (participation ratio of the marginal-KL profile, or effective rank of B), validated by a null model, and deleting the K sweep. What is sound — capacity ceiling, statistical guard, normalised entropy, distance-based stability, EP floor — is worth keeping intact and is enough to build the redesigned layer on.

Score: 6/10
