## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** The spec's objective is "minimize risk through optimal diversification" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:25`), declared a "two-level hierarchy: risk minimization is the primary goal, factorial diversification is the means" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:765`). The measure is deliberately not exposure-based: it "equalizes the contribution of each factor to total portfolio risk" (`strategy_philosophy.md:88`), which yields `|β'_k| ∝ λ_k^{-1/2}` (`strategy_philosophy.md:110-116`). The intent is to stop the portfolio from being concentrated on one latent factor that "cuts across" traditional categories (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:33`), including dormant crisis factors (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:78`), while never forecasting returns (`strategy_philosophy.md:154-168`).

**2. State of the art for this objective.** A SOTA version would (i) define diversification on the same risk decomposition that is penalized, so factor and residual risk are priced consistently; (ii) express the target as a budget vector with a convex risk-budgeting program rather than a non-concave entropy maximization; (iii) treat eigenvalue estimation error explicitly; (iv) if downside risk is wanted, fix the conditioning event ex ante and keep the problem convex; (v) make the latent factor structure identifiable. Literature: Meucci (2009), *Managing Diversification*, Risk 22(5), 74–79 — effective number of bets = exp(entropy of principal-portfolio risk contributions); mean–diversification frontier [unverified] (corpus's own description at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1654`). Roncalli (2013), *Introduction to Risk Parity and Budgeting*, Ch. 7-8 — general budgets `b`, and the log-barrier program `min ½w'Σw − Σ b_k ln w_k` whose solution has risk contributions proportional to `b` [unverified]. Spinu (2013), *An Algorithm for Computing Risk Parity Weights* — convex ERC reformulation [unverified] (cited as [38] at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:822`). Roncalli & Weisang (2016), *Risk Parity Portfolios with Risk Factors*, Quantitative Finance 16(3) — factor risk budgeting, multiple solutions, two-stage factor→asset mapping [unverified] (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1650`). Tasche (2008), *Capital Allocation to Business Units and Sub-Portfolios: the Euler Principle* — the Euler decomposition behind `c_k` [unverified]. Rockafellar & Uryasev (2000), *Optimization of Conditional Value-at-Risk*, Journal of Risk 2(3) — CVaR as a convex program [unverified]. Khemakhem et al. (2020), *Variational Autoencoders and Nonlinear ICA*, AISTATS — plain VAE latents are not identifiable without auxiliary variables [unverified].

**3. What prevents the unit from reaching it.** The factor entropy is normalized by systematic variance `C`, not total variance `σ²` (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1835`), so it is blind to the factor/idio split; the repair exists only in a proposal document and its weight is not derived (U12-ADV-06, U12-ADV-10). The `λ^{-1/2}` target loads most on the least reliably estimated directions (U12-ADV-05). The uniqueness claim needs a spectral gap (U12-ADV-03). The downside case rests on a false PSD argument plus an untested asymmetry hypothesis (U12-ADV-09). The Sortino alternative smuggles a return forecast into a supposedly return-free objective (U12-ADV-08). The claimed "hierarchy" is not what a scalarized sum implements (U12-ADV-11). *Apparent* obstacles only: negative risk contributions (fixed by diagonalization, U12-ADV-01); non-concavity (SCA/Armijo at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:818-830`; convex log-barrier alternatives exist); rotational indeterminacy (resolved, U12-ADV-03); "entropy ignores idiosyncratic risk" (a normalization issue, not a reason to abandon the measure).

**4. Real constraints vs self-imposed choices.** *Real*: Σ_z symmetric PSD; `C>0`; finite AU; sampling error in eigenvalues and eigenvectors; exact uniform contributions are generically outside the long-only simplex `{w≥0, 1'w=1, w≤5%}`; H is genuinely non-concave. *Self-imposed*: Shannon entropy as *the* measure (HHI, DR, Rao's quadratic entropy are alternatives, `literature_review_strategy.md:126-130`); uniform budgets; the fixed `w_idio = 0.20`; normalization by `C` rather than `σ²`; symmetric variance in the penalty; the "elbow" as the only calibration of `α`; requiring the diversification target to be a function of Σ_z and B alone.

**5. Remove, merge, move.** *Remove*: the two-layer formula as a decomposition claim and the fixed `w_idio = 0.20` (U12-ADV-06); the claim that KL budgets prevent zero risk (U12-ADV-07); the unconditional uniqueness claim (U12-ADV-03). *Move*: Sortino budgets into the directional layer with an explicit `μ` (U12-ADV-08); the downside question from training-time VIX weighting to an explicitly defined ex-ante conditioning event (U12-ADV-09). *Merge*: the three competing objectives (factor-only, two-layer, KL-budget) into one family parameterized by `(b, w, risk term)`, with the primary OOS metric matching the chosen objective (U12-ADV-10). *Keep*: the principal-basis Euler construction, the `α/λ` homotopy, and entropy-aware cardinality pruning.

## Core strengths to preserve
- **The principal-basis Euler construction** (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:684-692`). It is the only way to make a factor-level diversification measure well-defined with signed contributions, and it is exactly covariance-preserving. Any redesign must keep this object, whatever replaces Shannon entropy.
- **The return-free default and the removable return layer** (`strategy_philosophy.md:154-168`). This is what makes the risk model independently falsifiable (`strategy_philosophy.md:166`); U12-ADV-08 exists because the Sortino proposal would destroy it.
- **The `λ^{-1/2}` law, stated honestly** (`strategy_philosophy.md:110-124`). It is a precise and testable description of the portfolio's factor bets, including the uncomfortable part. Keep the honesty and add the estimation-error caveat (U12-ADV-05).
- **The one-parameter homotopy** between minimum variance and maximum diversification, with only `α/λ` mattering (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:769-774`). The endpoints are meaningful benchmarks, and the interpolation is interpretable.
- **`exp(H)` as the effective number of bets, and using the same measure for construction and cardinality pruning** (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:837-838`; `strategy_philosophy.md:146`). The measure is used consistently, not merely reported.
- **The idiosyncratic-block diagnosis** (`literature_review_idiosyncratic_risk.md:244`): the flat `AU+n` entropy does degenerate toward 1/N (`p = n/(AU+n)` at the flat maximum). The insight is right even though the fixed weight is not derived.
- **The honest disadvantage table for Strategy A** (`strategy_comparison.md:54-61`). It is unusually good self-criticism and should survive as the redesign checklist.

## Verdict
The unit's core mathematics is sound. The diagonalization argument fixes a real defect, the gradient is correctly derived, the ERC/ENB reading and the `λ^{-1/2}` law are correct, and the basis-invariance property is a genuine strength that a redesign should retain. The weaknesses are architectural rather than algebraic: the measure is normalized within the systematic block only; the two-layer repair is presented as a decomposition when it is a tuned reweighting; the downside-risk dismissal rests on a false PSD claim plus an untested asymmetry hypothesis; the Sortino alternative breaks the return-free modularity that the philosophy calls a principle; and the "hierarchy" is a scalarized trade-off, not a hierarchy. None of these requires abandoning the deep-learning formulation, and none is fixed by adding terms — they are fixed by choosing one coherent risk decomposition, one explicitly budgeted objective, and one estimation-error policy.

Score: 8/10
