## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** The unit is the operational form of the corpus's epistemic rule: "la complexité du VAE n'est justifiée que si elle produit un bénéfice OOS mesurable et statistiquement significatif par rapport à des alternatives plus simples, sur les mêmes données, avec les mêmes contraintes, évaluées par les mêmes métriques" (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:5`). DVT §4.8 frames it identically: "Without it, all metrics are suspect… the strategy must also be evaluated against benchmark models to determine whether its complexity is justified" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:862`). Primary metrics are chosen to mirror the declared objective (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:168`), and the benchmark suite is an explicit component-attribution design (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:71`, `:324`). Intent is threefold: a decision procedure (Scenarios A–E, `Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:287-311`), an attribution procedure (which stage earns its cost), and a pre-commitment device (`strategy_philosophy.md:183-185`).

**2. What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** (i) One pre-registered primary endpoint per hypothesis family, the rest explicitly secondary. (ii) A Model Confidence Set instead of 24 pairwise tests — it returns the set of models not distinguishable at a given confidence, which is exactly the "PCA ≈ VAE" question without accepting the null (Hansen, Lunde & Nason 2011, *The Model Confidence Set*, Econometrica 79(2), 453–497) [unverified]. (iii) Inference on score differentials robust to serial dependence (Diebold & Mariano 1995, *Comparing Predictive Accuracy*, JBES 13(3); Giacomini & White 2006, *Tests of Conditional Predictive Ability*, Econometrica 74(6)) [unverified]. (iv) Data-snooping control over the full trial set (White 2000, *A Reality Check for Data Snooping*, Econometrica 68(5); Hansen 2005, *A Test for Superior Predictive Ability*, JBES 23(4); Romano & Wolf 2005, *Stepwise Multiple Testing*, Econometrica 73(4)) [unverified]. (v) A selection-bias correction for the number of configurations tried (Bailey & López de Prado 2014, *The Deflated Sharpe Ratio*, JPM 40(5); Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado & Zhu 2017, *The Probability of Backtest Overfitting*, J. Computational Finance 20(4)) [unverified]. (vi) Explicit equivalence bounds and TOST for "≈" (Schuirmann 1987) [unverified]. (vii) Proper scoring rules for covariance forecasts (Patton 2011, *Volatility forecast comparison using imperfect volatility proxies*, J. Econometrics 160(1)) [unverified]. The corpus cites none of (ii)–(vii): its reference list (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:328-338`) contains Ledoit-Wolf, Spinu, Bai-Ng, Clarke et al., Choueifaty-Coignard, Feng-Palomar, Almgren-Chriss and DeMiguel et al., but no backtest-inference or multiple-testing reference.

**3. What prevents the unit from reaching it — apparent vs real obstacles.** Apparent: (a) "24 tests require heavy correction" — the multiplicity is manufactured by the four-metric co-primary block (ADV-03); one endpoint plus an MCS removes it. (b) "Entropy must be primary" — it is a valid within-model utilization rate but not a cross-model yardstick (ADV-06). (c) "Without CRSP the protocol cannot run" — the inferential core needs only an aligned survivorship-bias-free panel (ADV-10). (d) "34 folds are too few to test anything" — 34 paired folds detect a consistent effect at 24/34 signs (ADV-02); the real limit is the crisis subsample (ADV-08). Real: non-exchangeability across regimes (contiguous OOS windows, volatility clustering), the small number of independent crisis episodes, and the fact that a single 3-year holdout is one path (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:283`). Selection-on-test is **not** an obstacle at all — it is a self-imposed choice (ADV-05), since a nested validation subset already exists (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:874`).

**4. Real constraints vs self-imposed choices.** Real: ~34 non-overlapping 6-month windows over 30 years (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:59`; `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:882`); crisis episodes are few and clustered (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:228`); daily factor returns are serially dependent, so the effective sample for second moments is below nominal (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:752`); a one-shot holdout yields one path, no distribution (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:880`). Self-imposed: four co-primary metrics (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:166-175`); a free-weight composite score (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:933`, `:1063`); HP selection on the OOS folds (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:894`, `:929`); pairwise testing rather than an MCS (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:224`); "≈" = non-rejection (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:299-301`); entropy normalized by each model's own capacity (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:172`); the regime split outside the declared family (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:275`); the 30-year/1,000-stock/CRSP universe as the only specified data (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:15-32`).

**5. What should be removed, merged or moved.** Remove the composite score from the role of test statistic; keep it as a reported utility, with the ranking shown across its own declared λ_pen range 1–10 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1063`) — if the winner changes, it is not a decision criterion (ADV-07). Remove the four-metric co-primary block; one risk endpoint primary, entropy demoted to within-model diagnostic (where `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:937` shows it is valid), return/Sharpe to sanity checks (ADV-03, ADV-06). Remove Scenarios C and D as admissible *actions*; keep them as diagnostic states that trigger a different deep formulation and a new test (ADV-04). Remove the holdout from the vocabulary of "tests" (ADV-10). Merge Benchmark §3.3 and DVT Layer 2 into one gate with one threshold set — explanatory power currently has three values (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:198`; `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:910`, `:955`) (ADV-01). Move the regime decomposition into the pre-registered hypothesis set with its own alpha and a daily crisis-conditional metric (ADV-08). Move comparison from 24 pairwise tests to a model confidence set — this deletes the multiplicity problem rather than correcting it.

## Core strengths to preserve

1. **Three-layer metric architecture with explicit primary/diagnostic status (ADV-01).** Keep the separation of "the model achieves its own objective" from "the objective is economically meaningful". Any redesign must retain a layer that scores the learned object independently of the portfolio it produces.
2. **Paired-by-fold inference with effect size and bootstrap CI (ADV-02).** Pairing is the correct variance-reduction device and the CI prevents significance-only decisions. Keep both, with a block bootstrap or HAC correction.
3. **Conjunctive benchmark requirement (ADV-03).** "Beat all six" is an intersection-union structure whose size is controlled without extra correction. Keep the conjunction; fix the family accounting.
4. **Ablation-by-substitution suite (ADV-09).** It is the only design in the corpus that can attribute value to a specific stage, and it gives the redesign a diagnostic vocabulary (which layer fails, not just "the model loses").
5. **Pre-registered crisis/calm invalidation clause (ADV-08).** Keep the directional prediction and its stated falsification condition; it is the corpus's clearest falsifiable hypothesis.
6. **Point-in-time regime labelling and untouched holdout (A9, ADV-10).** Keep the expanding VIX percentile and the one-shot holdout, with the holdout used only as a structural check.
7. **Refusal to re-tune penalty parameters per model (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:48`).** Keep the principle and extend it to every model hyperparameter, not just portfolio penalties.
8. **Dimensional discipline of the score (ADV-07).** Keep the normalization and explicit units; move the score out of the inferential role.
9. **Risk-model calibration metrics (ADV-01).** Keep the variance ratio, explanatory power and correlation-rank diagnostics, with a single threshold set.

## Verdict

The unit is a genuine inferential architecture, not a checklist: paired folds, nonparametric tests, effect sizes, FWER control, an ablation suite and pre-registered consequences. As an instrument for *refuting* the theory it is credible — selection-on-OOS biases in the VAE's favour, so a negative result is a fortiori informative, and the crisis/calm clause is a real prediction. As an instrument for *confirming* it, it fails on three separable choices: selection on the test folds (ADV-05), equivalence inferred from non-rejection (ADV-04), and a model-specific primary metric (ADV-06). The accounting and arithmetic errors (ADV-03, ADV-07, ADV-11) are symptoms of one cause: the unit was specified as a reporting pipeline rather than as a pre-registered decision procedure. The skeleton is worth keeping; the decision layer must be rebuilt around one endpoint and a model confidence set.

Score: 6/10
