## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** U19 has no owner document; its intent is stated by the corpus's own cross-references — the DVT claims a benchmark specification "is specified in `Latent_risk_factor_benchmark.md`" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:862`) while the file is actually `Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md`, and the philosophy claims to be "Synthesized from DVT v4.1" (`strategy_philosophy.md:3`). The unit's purpose is therefore *subordination*: every derived document must be a faithful, non-divergent restatement of the DVT, and the DVT's bibliography must actually support the claims it decorates.

**2. State of the art.** (a) A **normative core + derived views** architecture with a single definition per quantity (ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010 style architecture-description discipline) [unverified]; (b) **pre-registered decision rules** with two-sided admissible outcomes (Nosek et al. 2018, "The preregistration revolution", *PNAS* — pre-specification removes the analyst's freedom to choose which failure branch counts) [unverified]; (c) for factor counting, formal **model-selection criteria with known asymptotics** rather than heuristic thresholds (Bai & Ng 2002, already cited at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1630`; Ahn & Horenstein 2013 eigenvalue-ratio estimators) [unverified]; (d) for VAE latent-count claims, the **identifiability result** of Lucas et al. 2019, which the corpus itself cites (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1602`) but does not apply to its own AU argument.

**3. What prevents it.** The corpus is not one document with appended references; it is six documents each of which re-derives and re-numbers the same constants, with no normative hierarchy. Concretely: one statistical constant has three mutually inconsistent definitions (Finding U19-CRI-01), the benchmark set has two different cardinalities (U19-CRI-05), the decision score has two different formulas (U19-CRI-06), the Σ_z temporal-weighting justification misreads the DVT section it cites (U19-CRI-07), and three supporting documents silently propose objectives the DVT does not contain (U19-CRI-07, -08).

**4. Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: closed corpus, one author, no CRSP, no results yet. Self-imposed: (i) declaring K a "capacity ceiling" so that the factor count need not be chosen (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:404-406`); (ii) the 0.01-nat activity threshold, which the corpus's own review calls heuristic (`literature_review_strategy.md:236`); (iii) a *single* terminal comparison against PCA as the only admitted failure branch (`strategy_philosophy.md:183`); (iv) treating supporting documents as authoritative without a promotion protocol; (v) putting the risk-model rescaling derivation in one section while the α/λ calibration that consumes it sits in another, unlinked.

**5. What should be removed, merged or moved.** *Remove*: the auto-pruning "the model decides the number of factors" claim as a *mechanism* (§4.3, philosophy §6) — replace by an explicitly penalized elbow rule; the λ_est term from the selection score or apply it to *all* models; the ERC post-hoc projection from the benchmark. *Merge*: the three Σ_z documents (DVT §4.7, §6.3, `sigma_z_ewma_justification.md`) into one; the four/five bibliographies into one shared list; `strategy_comparison.md`'s "current DVT" description into the DVT's objective section; `literature_review_idiosyncratic_risk.md`'s objective into the DVT as a *named variant* if it is to exist at all. *Move*: the benchmark decision matrix out of the benchmark document and into the DVT's stopping-criteria section, so that the terminal rule and the terminal outcome live in the same normative place; the reference list to an appendix that is machine-checked against in-text citations.

## Root-cause hypotheses

**RC1 — No normative core; every document re-derives the same constants (explains CRI-01, -05, -06, -07, -09, -12).** The corpus is organised as prose documents that *quote* each other rather than as one normative specification with derived summaries. Any constant restated twice drifts. The symptom set is diagnostic: a formula that disagrees with its own table, a benchmark set with two cardinalities, a score with two definitions, three years for one Meucci paper, a reference to a section that does not exist.
*Alternatives.* (a) **Single normative registry**: every symbol defined once with a value, a formula and a source; all other documents reference rather than restate. Trade-off: heavy authoring, and prose loses the rhetorical force of derivation-in-place. (b) **One normative document, others demoted to summaries that may not contain formulas or numbers**. Trade-off: readable, but summaries invite drift exactly where they are most consulted. (c) **Generated documents with invariant checks** (each number computed from the registry, each in-text citation checked against the list). Trade-off: needs tooling outside the corpus's current mode, but is the only option that makes consistency mechanical rather than aspirational.

**RC2 — Mechanisms are asserted at a level of certainty their evidence cannot carry, and the hedge is exiled to a secondary document (explains CRI-02, -01).** The DVT states "the VAE decides" as fact and demonstrates it with a round number that equals the statistical guard; the review that admits the threshold is heuristic is never cited where the claim is made. This is not an error of scholarship but a structural one: the corpus has no rule that a load-bearing mechanism must carry its own falsification test at the point of use.
*Alternatives.* (a) **Synthetic calibration layer**: generate panels with a known factor count and report the AU recovery rate as a function of spectrum flatness — turning the mechanism claim into a measured operating characteristic. Trade-off: adds a whole experimental layer the theory currently does not have. (b) **Replace the discrete AU decision by a continuous penalised criterion inside the downstream objective** — the network's latent spectrum enters the risk model with an explicit complexity penalty rather than a 0.01-nat gate. Trade-off: loses the "no human choice" narrative, which is one of the strategy's stated selling points. (c) **Demote AU to hyperparameter, selected by an information criterion with known asymptotics (Bai-Ng IC₂ on a linear-VAE spectrum)**, keeping the network as the extractor. Trade-off: makes the factor count a tuned quantity, i.e. a degree of freedom the corpus tried to eliminate.

**RC3 — One-sided decision architecture: the documents are written to justify a possible retreat, not to specify a search (explains CRI-04, -05, -06).** Because the terminal state is pre-committed to "PCA if the VAE fails", the benchmark set is built to adjudicate that one comparison, and every other metric is demoted to "diagnostic" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:916-923`). A layered redesign cannot grow out of a protocol whose only output is a binary verdict on the network.
*Alternatives.* (a) **Layer-wise stopping rules**: risk-model accuracy, factor-number stability, objective behaviour and allocation each get their own baseline and their own stop, so failure is localised rather than terminal. Trade-off: many more pre-registered tests, and the multiple-testing burden the benchmark already mishandles (CRI-12) grows. (b) **Two-sided decision table with an "inconclusive" branch** that keeps the deep model under narrowed scope. Trade-off: risks never resolving. (c) **Sequential testing hierarchy** (risk-model first; portfolio metrics only if the risk model passes), which makes each layer's claim separable. Trade-off: a failing upstream layer never gets evaluated downstream, so some questions stay unanswered by design.

**RC4 — Supporting documents can propose new objectives without amending the normative one (explains CRI-07, -08).** `sigma_z_ewma_justification.md` reverses a DVT choice using a misread section; `literature_review_idiosyncratic_risk.md` introduces a second entropy term; `strategy_comparison.md` describes the "current DVT" with a $\mu'w$ tilt the DVT excludes (`strategy_comparison.md:15` vs `implementation_decisions.md:342`). In three cases the proposal is defensible in isolation; none is marked as a proposal.
*Alternatives.* (a) **Objective registry** with exactly one canonical objective and named variants that must be implemented as benchmarks, never as descriptions of the baseline. Trade-off: forecloses exploratory prose. (b) **Two-tier documents**: normative (may state the objective) and hypothesis notes (may not; must cite a discriminating experiment). Trade-off: authoring discipline, but it is the cheapest of the four. (c) **Promotion protocol**: a variant enters the DVT only after a pre-registered test against the canonical objective, with the test specified at proposal time. Trade-off: slows every idea, which for a theory-stage project may be the point.

## Verdict

As devil's advocate on cross-document consistency, I cannot give this corpus a pass. It reads as one voice and is in fact six voices, and the divergence is not in prose style but in the numbers: the statistical guard that the DVT itself calls "the binding constraint in practice" has a printed formula that produces 59 where its own table and the ISD produce 85, and a third parameter count appears in the selection score (U19-CRI-01); the central claim that the network, not the analyst, decides how many risk factors exist is asserted as convergence, contradicted by the corpus's own review, back-filled from the administrative cap, and supported by a citation that does not contain the result attributed to it (U19-CRI-02); the rescaling section's "stable unit across time" is the exact inverse of what its own algebra does, and the α/λ calibration that consumes it is therefore on a moving scale (U19-CRI-03). The remaining findings are lower-severity but the same disease: a benchmark set that cannot detect the ablation its own logic requires, a decision score that can only ever penalise the model under test, a Σ_z memo that cites the wrong section of the document it claims to defend, a review that proposes a second objective while the philosophy says the problem is handled by constraints, and a bibliography where the load-bearing empirical anchors (Barra stability, 25-month half-lives, Epps) have no entry and several entries have no use. Every terminal rule in the corpus agrees — and all of them agree on abandoning the network, which is the one outcome the project has excluded; a corpus whose falsification architecture has a single admissible branch is not yet falsifiable in the sense the campaign needs. The reference lists are the most visible symptom: three divergent bibliographies, one impossible date, one absent key source, and a misannotated IWAE paper carrying a quantitative rule it never states. The deep-learning commitment is not what is at risk here; what is at risk is that the theory's constants, objectives and stopping criteria are not one theory but a family of drifting restatements, and any redesign built on them will inherit the drift.

Score: 3/10
