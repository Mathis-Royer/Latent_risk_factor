## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** §2 is a requirements-elicitation chapter written *before* the model is chosen: "Before designing the model, we need to clarify what it must capture... to establish the requirements that the architecture must satisfy" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:45`). It emits three requirements: continuous multi-membership (`:64`), memory of dormant factors (`:78`), and sensitivity to tail co-movements (`:95`). Its intent is therefore to *constrain* the model choice — the unit is a filter on architectures, not a description of one.

**2. What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** Not a factor-model catalogue, but a single *state-conditional* risk object: a covariance whose loadings are functions of (own history × latent market state), with the state inferred rather than assumed, and with identifiability of the state made a falsifiable claim. The relevant literature is the conditional/autoencoder factor-model line: Kelly, Pruitt & Su (2019), "Characteristics are covariances", *JFE* 134(3) — latent factors with time-varying loadings [unverified]; Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2021), "Autoencoder asset pricing models", *Journal of Econometrics* 222(1) — a conditional autoencoder where the nonlinearity that earns its place is in the *loading function* [unverified]; Ferson & Harvey (1991), "The Variation of Economic Risk Premiums", *JPE* 99(2) — conditional betas [unverified]; Ang & Timmermann (2012), "Regime Changes and Financial Markets", *ARFE* 4 — state dependence in return distributions [unverified]; Locatello et al. (2019) on the impossibility of bias-free disentanglement [unverified], which the corpus already invokes. The SOTA unit would state persistence as an estimable parameter (the corpus has the estimator at `literature_review_strategy.md:261`) and make "the state matters" a testable null.

**3. What prevents it.** The requirements are stated at the level of the *representation* and then carried by an architecture whose only cross-sectional term is scheduled to zero (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:508,512`) and whose only date-varying quantity is a scalar volatility ratio (`:730`). So the temporal requirement has no carrier (findings 05–07). **Obstacles that are only apparent:** the "infinity of sources" looks like it forces large K, but the binding limit is AU_max_stat ≈ 85 (`:424-426`); the "graph" looks like it forces a GNN, but a weighted incidence matrix satisfies it (`:672`); universe flexibility looks like it forbids peer-dependent encoders, but that is a consequence of the static profile (finding 09); "non-linear dependencies" look like they need to be discovered after the scaffold is removed, but nothing in the Phase-3 objective rewards them (finding 08).

**4. Real constraints vs self-imposed choices.** *Real:* no look-ahead (`:238`), the parameter/sample budget on Σ_z (`:422-426`), the fact that a variance objective needs second moments, and price-only identification (`:136-137`). *Self-imposed:* one static composite profile per stock (`:644-656`); a translation-invariant encoder that deliberately discards "when" (`:311`); equal weighting of the full history (`:752`, `sigma_z_ewma_justification.md:41`); λ_co→0 (`:512`); AU chosen by a generative statistic rather than a risk criterion (`:714,718`).

**5. What should be removed, merged or moved.** (a) **Remove** the composite profile as the primitive: replace the static μ̄ by a date-indexed exposure path — the pipeline already computes B̃_{A,i,t} (`:728-730`), so the static object is a special case doing less work than it appears. This also dissolves the peer-independence constraint (finding 09) and lets the encoder see the cross-section, which is the only way the co-movement requirement survives inference (finding 08). (b) **Remove** the tail/asymmetry bullet from §2.1 (`:57`) or change the objective — under w'Σw it is decoration (finding 01). (c) **Merge** §2.1–2.4 into one sentence: *the risk object is a state-conditional return covariance with low-rank-plus-diagonal structure; the state is continuous, time-indexed, and peer-independent at inference.* (d) **Move** the anti-cyclical guarantee from the aggregation step to the covariance estimator, where a weighting is explicit, declared and testable instead of emergent (findings 05–06). (e) **Merge** the three verbal temporal classes into the single persistence statistic the corpus already defines. Nothing above loses a strength: the invariance discipline (10), the regime-conditioned evaluation (11) and the no-a-priori-categories principle (02) are untouched.

## Core strengths to preserve

1. **The reduction to a single identifiable risk object** (finding 01). Any redesign that keeps a covariance-based objective must keep this discipline: it is what stops the theory from chasing unmeasurable "risk sources" and what makes PCA a fair baseline rather than a category error.
2. **Requirement statements at the representation level** (finding 02). Continuous multi-membership in a real-valued n×K matrix is architecture-neutral; it is the one part of §2 that automatically survives a redesign and should be carried forward verbatim, minus the claim that it selects a VAE.
3. **Explicit treatment of representational indeterminacy** (finding 10). Rotation/sign/dimension invariance, in both the risk model and the stability diagnostic, is rare and hard-won. A redesign that introduces a new latent object must re-establish its invariance group and its invariant diagnostics before any comparison across retrainings is meaningful.
4. **Regime-conditioned evaluation** (finding 11). The only mechanism in the corpus that can adjudicate the temporality claim; keep it and extend it from portfolio metrics to covariance-forecast metrics.
5. **Honest self-limitation** (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:496`, `:1163`). The corpus states where its mechanism is not guaranteed and what would falsify the architecture. That is the right register for the redesign to inherit.

## Verdict

As requirements elicitation, §2 is disciplined and unusually honest: it names an open-ended risk space, narrows it deliberately to price co-movement, and derives a representation (continuous multi-membership) that is genuinely architecture-agnostic and correctly handled for indeterminacy. As a constraint on model choice, however, it under-delivers in three linked ways. It constrains the representation, not the function class, so nothing in §2 distinguishes a VAE from a linear factor model except the tail/non-linearity claim — and the risk object is a second-moment object that cannot represent tails (01). Its temporal taxonomy is descriptive, with no parameter, no estimator and no unsupported quantitative anchor (04). And its central mechanism, "memory of dormant factors", is delivered by two frequency-weighted averages — a mean over windows (05) and an equal-weighted full-history covariance (06) — that encode dormancy as attenuation and, through the inverse-√λ entropy rule, convert it into *crisis-period concentration* (07). The cross-sectional channel that would make the graph requirement survive inference exists only in a scaffold that is annealed to zero (08), while the peer-independence constraint that supposedly rules out stronger alternatives turns out to be derivative of the static profile (09). These are architectural, not parametric: the fix is to make the exposure object date- and state-indexed and to declare the regime weighting explicitly, which the corpus already half-built (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:728-730`) and then froze.
