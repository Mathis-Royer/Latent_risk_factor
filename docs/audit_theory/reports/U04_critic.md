## Five questions

1. **Purpose and original intent.** The unit must turn raw prices into a representation that is (i) stationary and comparable across stocks and time, (ii) informative about co-movement, and (iii) sensitive to stress. The spec is explicit: daily log returns (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:206-208`), per-window z-scoring because it is the only no-look-ahead normalization that removes level and scale (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:226-240`), a 21-day realized-vol channel to restore what z-scoring erased (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:244-252`), T=504 as "long enough for several market conditions … short enough for a coherent risk regime" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:301`), and VIX-based crisis weights (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:544-558`). The stated intent: per-window normalization "forces the model to learn structural patterns … rather than absolute return levels — which is precisely what a risk factor model requires" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:240`). The unstated intent is to make a 30-year, 1,000-stock panel trainable. The tension is that a factor risk model needs both shape (β) and scale (σ); the unit erases scale in the encoder and delegates it to a separate linear rescaling (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:724-742`).

2. **What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** A modern deep risk-feature pipeline would: (a) keep a scale-aware representation — raw returns under a heavy-tailed or scale-mixture likelihood, or returns normalized by a causal, cross-sectionally comparable scale estimate, rather than a per-window z-score that erases the level (Andersen, Bollerslev, Diebold & Labys, 2003, "Modeling and Forecasting Realized Volatility", *Econometrica* 71(2):579-625, who show realized variance from intraday returns is more efficient than rolling daily-return standard deviations and exhibits long memory [unverified]; Engle, 2002, "Dynamic Conditional Correlation", *JBES* 20(3):339-350, who separates volatilities and correlations and allows time-varying correlations [unverified]); (b) learn from the cross-section, not only per-stock reconstruction — panel ML or contrastive objectives (Gu, Kelly & Xiu, 2020, "Empirical Asset Pricing via Machine Learning", *RFS* 33(5):2223-2273, who show nonlinear panel models on firm characteristics improve out-of-sample prediction over linear benchmarks using cross-sectional normalization [unverified]; Chen, Kornblith, Norouzi & Hinton, 2020, "A Simple Framework for Contrastive Learning of Visual Representations", ICML, whose InfoNCE pulls positive pairs together and pushes negatives apart [unverified]); (c) validate with purged/embargoed splits whose embargo is at least the label/window horizon (Lopez de Prado, 2018, *Advances in Financial Machine Learning*, who shows purging and embargoing prevent leakage from overlapping labels [unverified]); (d) handle missing data with a mask/likelihood rather than forward-fill (Little & Rubin, 2019, *Statistical Analysis with Missing Data*, who show likelihood-based methods under MAR are preferable to ad hoc imputation [unverified]); (e) label stress stock-specifically or with a regime-switching model, not a single market-wide VIX percentile (Hamilton, 1989, "A New Approach to the Economic Analysis of Nonstationary Time Series and the Business Cycle", *Econometrica* 57(2):357-384, who identifies latent regimes by Markov switching [unverified]). The current unit does (a) partially (it keeps shape, not scale), (b) only through a scaffold it removes, (c) not at the window level, (d) not, (e) not.

3. **What prevents it.** The load-bearing failures are CRI-01 (the vol channel cannot restore scale), CRI-02 (the capacity constraint counts overlapping windows as independent), CRI-03 (the crisis label is internally inconsistent), CRI-10 (validation windows overlap training), and CRI-12 (the final training phase has no cross-sectional objective). The rest are material but secondary.

   *Steelman.* Per-window z-scoring is the only normalization that is both point-in-time and regime-local (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:234-238`); the vol channel, though deterministic, may still act as an inductive bias that makes heteroskedasticity easier to optimize; T=504 is a reasonable compromise; stride-1 windows are standard in time-series deep learning; VIX is the best available point-in-time stress indicator; mean aggregation is robust and parameter-free.

   *Rebuttal.* Comparability across stocks is exactly what per-window z-scoring destroys at the level of scale, and the downstream σ-rescaling is an ad hoc patch, not a substitute (CRI-01, CRI-05). The inductive-bias argument supports a weaker claim than the one made ("restores" scale) and does not justify the F=2 benchmark as a test of richer data. T=504 being reasonable does not justify declaring it "resolved" while it also sets the validation block length (CRI-09, CRI-10). Stride 1 being standard does not make 7,057 windows independent (CRI-02). VIX being point-in-time does not make it cross-sectional (CRI-03). Mean aggregation being robust is precisely why it averages away the episodic exposures the theory claims to preserve (CRI-04).

4. **Real vs self-imposed constraints.** *Real:* price-only data (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:3.4`); VIX history from 1990 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:558`); delisted stocks are required for survivorship-free training (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:263-265`), but a non-CRSP vendor may only have them from 2000 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:291`); log returns are additive; z-scoring needs a finite window; the compute cost of ~7M forward passes (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:648`). *Self-imposed:* per-window time-series z-scoring rather than cross-sectional standardization (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:236-240`); z-scoring the vol channel (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:248`); T=504 fixed (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:301, 998`); the 21-day vol window (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:244`); P80 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:556`); γ=3 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:571`); the 20% zero-return exclusion (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:242`); mean aggregation (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:656`); the 252-day trailing vol (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:734`); the top-n market-cap cap (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:279`); the 2-year validation block (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:874`); the 21-day embargo (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:876`). *Self-imposed presented as real:* "T is … an architectural constraint, not a choice about how much history to consider" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:297`) — T is a choice, and it also sets the normalization window and the minimum listing history. "F=2 baseline" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:303`) is a choice, not a datum. The no-CRSP constraint is real, but the 30-year, 1,000-stock, survivorship-free requirement is a design choice that may not be feasible on EODHD (delisted only since 2000, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:291`).

5. **What should be removed, merged or moved.**
   - **Remove** the per-window z-scoring of the vol channel. Merge scale into one explicit scale model; do not erase it in the encoder and re-inject it linearly at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:724-742` (CRI-01, CRI-05).
   - **Remove** the vol channel as an "information" feature, or replace it with a genuinely orthogonal signal (overnight gap, intraday range, volume, option-implied vol). It is a deterministic function of the return window (CRI-01).
   - **Remove** T=504 from the resolved list. Separate the encoder receptive field, the normalization window, the regime-coherence window, the minimum listing history and the validation block (CRI-09).
   - **Remove** mean aggregation. Replace it with a distribution-aware or quantile/attention aggregation that can retain episodic exposures and posterior uncertainty (CRI-04, CRI-06).
   - **Move** crisis labeling out of the reconstruction loss and into the sampling geometry, or remove it and use a stock-specific stress label (CRI-03, CRI-11).
   - **Remove** the 20% zero-return hard exclusion and the forward-fill-as-zero rule; use a mask.
   - **Remove** the delisting imputation from the feature pipeline; treat delisting as a censoring event (CRI-08).
   - **Move** validation to window-level block CV with an embargo at least as long as T (CRI-10).
   - **Merge** the universe cap into the risk model as a size exposure rather than an a priori exclusion (U03).

## Root-cause hypotheses

**RC1 — Scale is treated as a separable scalar that can be erased and re-injected.** Explains CRI-01, -04, -05, -07, -08. The theory uses three incompatible scale conventions (per-window z-score, per-window z-scored vol, per-date σ ratio) and never states the decomposition. Structural alternatives: (a) one explicit scale-shape factorization with a learned scale head; trade-off: identifies the decomposition but adds a latent scale variable and a likelihood. ## Verdict

The unit is conventional in its choices but unsound in its load-bearing justifications. The two critical defects are mathematical: the realized-volatility channel is invariant to the very scale it is said to restore (CRI-01), and the capacity-data argument counts overlapping windows as independent observations while the document elsewhere says there are only ~15 independent windows per stock (CRI-02). The crisis labeling is internally inconsistent (CRI-03), the mean aggregation cannot preserve the episodic exposures the theory is built on (CRI-04), and the validation block is contaminated by the same 504-day window that defines the model (CRI-10). The unit is not beyond repair — log returns, per-window shape normalization, a 2-year window and a vol channel are all defensible choices — but the theory must state the weaker claims it can support, separate the roles conflated into T, and replace the point-estimate pipeline with one that carries scale and uncertainty explicitly.
