## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent of this unit.** U19 is the corpus's own validity condition, not an editorial afterthought. The benchmark procedure states it explicitly: every model must share "exactement la même infrastructure. Toute divergence invalide la comparaison" (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:11`). The corpus assigns itself a division of labour — normative synthesis (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:3`), operational instantiation (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:3`), derived principles (`strategy_philosophy.md:3`), decision log for places where "the ISD/DVT specifications were insufficient or ambiguous" (`implementation_decisions.md:3`). U19's intent is therefore to check that one theory is actually being stated: one definition per symbol, one derivation per number, one gate per decision.

**2. What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** A corpus with (i) a single normative symbol registry and no number asserted in a summary that is not derived in the normative document; (ii) an evaluation protocol that pre-registers the selection rule and separates selection from reporting — the selection-bias literature is explicit that reporting the maximum of many trials as OOS performance is biased (Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado & Zhu, 2014, "Pseudo-Mathematics and Financial Charlatanism", *Notices of the AMS* 61(5); Bailey & López de Prado, 2014, "The Deflated Sharpe Ratio", *JPM* 40(5) — both `[unverified]`); (iii) a factor-count rule with a null distribution rather than a threshold plus a degrees-of-freedom guard (Bai & Ng, 2002; Onatski, 2010, both already in the corpus); (iv) confirmatory/exploratory separation as in pre-registration practice (Nosek, Ebersole, DeHaven & Mellor, 2018, "The preregistration revolution", *PNAS* 115(11) — `[unverified]`); (v) citation annotations that contain only what the cited work contains.

**3. What prevents the current unit from reaching it.** The obstacles are distributed, not concentrated: U19-ADV-01, -03, -04, -05, -09. Two obstacles are only apparent. (a) The French language of the benchmark procedure is not the problem: that document is the most internally consistent and most complete statement of the gate in the corpus, and the DVT's summary is the thing that diverges from it (U19-ADV-04). (b) "The documents describe different versions" explains little: U19-ADV-01, -04 and -03 are contradictions *inside* a single document or between a document and its own table. Version drift is a governance failure, not the root cause. The real obstacle is that numbers are asserted where they should be computed, so each document re-derives locally and none is authoritative.

**4. Which constraints are real and which are self-imposed.** Real: the data constraint. Without CRSP the history is 25 years (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:578`), which lowers the observation count to ~6,049 and the factor ceiling to 77 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:431`); the non-concavity of H is real and does force SCA (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:818`); an AU≈85 covariance on ~7,300 dates genuinely sits at an observations/parameter ratio near 2 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:752`), so shrinkage is not optional. Self-imposed: K=200, the 5% position cap, the 0.01-nat threshold, the 3-year holdout, the 30% turnover cap, the scalar σ², r_min=2, the 1.5–2× K rule, the decision to select on the OOS fold, and the rule that benchmarks inherit the VAE's penalties.

**5. What should be removed, merged or moved.** Remove: the duplicate benchmark inventory in the DVT (U19-ADV-04); the legacy "Mode 1/Mode 2" labels (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:773`, `:1051-1052`); one of the two K rules, since they conflict (U19-ADV-01); the idiosyncratic-entropy term, which duplicates the constraint layer (U19-ADV-11). Merge: the three AU_max_stat forms into one corrected closed form (U19-ADV-01); the four explanatory-power thresholds into one decision rule (U19-ADV-04); the 30-year and 25-year schedules into one schedule parameterized by available history (U19-ADV-10). Move: the benchmark procedure into the DVT as the normative annex, or make it the sole normative source and delete the DVT's summary; the selection step inside the fold, or the reported metric to the holdout (U19-ADV-03). Nothing in this list removes the learned-σ² derivation (U19-ADV-06), the principal-factor entropy construction (U19-ADV-02), or the shared-infrastructure controls (U19-ADV-07).

## Core strengths to preserve
- **The learned-σ² VAE objective and its dimensional accounting** (U19-ADV-06). Any redesign that changes the architecture or the loss must keep the exact Gaussian NLL and the D/(2σ²) coefficient, because it is the corpus's only parameter-free recon/KL balancer and it has a closed-form optimum. Removing it re-opens posterior collapse by construction.
- **The principal-factor entropy construction** (U19-ADV-02). Diagonalising Σ_z=VΛVᵀ makes contributions non-negative and removes the VAE's rotational indeterminacy; the gradient (−2/C)B′φ with φ_k=λ_kβ′_k(ln ĉ′_k+H) is correct and vanishes at uniformity. Keep the construction even if the objective changes.
- **Normalised entropy Ĥ=H/ln(AU)** (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:935-939`). The arithmetic is right and it fixes a real cross-configuration comparability problem; any redesign should keep normalisation but calibrate it against the maximum attainable under the position cap.
- **The AU guard as a principle** (U19-ADV-01). Counting AU(AU+1)/2 free parameters and requiring a minimum observations/parameter ratio is the right way to let a statistical constraint, rather than taste, bound the factor count.
- **The benchmark fairness architecture** (U19-ADV-07). Shared universe, constraints, folds, fixed penalties, multiplicity correction, regime split and holdout are the corpus's external-validity backbone.
- **The explicit risk definition → objective link** (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:89` → `:765-776`): risk is what materialises in price co-movements, hence symmetric variance and μ=0. The definition is stated once and the objective follows; this is what makes the μ=0 choice principled rather than arbitrary, and it should survive any change of learned object.

## Verdict
The corpus is mathematically serious and, in places, better than most production risk-model documentation: the VAE likelihood derivation, the principal-factor entropy gradient, the crisis-weight calibration table, the degrees-of-freedom logic of the AU guard and the benchmark's fairness controls are all correct and mutually reinforcing. What fails is not the mathematics but the *governance of definitions*: the factor count is specified by four rules with an empty intersection, the equalisation principle is a limit case presented as the operating point, the OOS folds are used both to select and to report, the gate exists in two inventories and four thresholds, and the bibliography carries quantitative rules its sources do not contain. These are architectural because they mean the theory's central quantities — how many factors, how much diversification, how good the model is — do not have unique values. The defects are diagnosable and mostly repairable by deletion and re-derivation rather than by adding machinery; the underlying objects are sound. That is why this is a 6 rather than a 3: the theory is inconsistent, but it is not confused about what it is trying to measure.

Score: 6/10
