## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** The unit exists to convert a risk-only factor covariance into weights without importing a return forecast: `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:680` ("pure risk model with no return views"), with return views "injected at the optimizer level as a separate, removable layer". The declared objective is a hierarchy, not a single preference: "risk minimization is the primary goal, factorial diversification is the means" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:765`), operationalized as `max_w -λ w'Σw + αH(w)` with μ=0 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:769`). The intent is coherent: Σ supplies the risk level, H supplies the composition of systematic risk, and α prices one against the other.

**2. What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** A modern version would (i) keep a risk-based objective but express the diversification target in a scale-free, eigenbasis-stable way — Meucci (2009), *Managing Diversification*, *Risk* 22(5), the Effective Number of Bets as the entropy of principal-portfolio risk contributions (quoted in the corpus at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1654`) [unverified for the paper's exact construction]; (ii) treat the risk/diversification trade-off with an explicitly normalized scalarization rather than a raw α, and check that both terms are of the same order (standard multi-objective practice; Das & Dennis 1997 on weighted-sum scalarization and its supported-point limitation [unverified]); (iii) solve the nonconvex problem with SCA/majorization and a proven stationarity guarantee — Feng & Palomar (2015), *SCRIP*, IEEE TSP 63(19), "convergence to KKT point guaranteed" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1648`) — or with Spinu (2013) convex reformulation for the ERC special case (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1652`); (iv) handle cardinality with a convex relaxation/MISOCP or an explicit optimality gap, since the semi-continuous problem is NP-hard (Bienstock 1996 [unverified]; corpus statement at `literature_review_strategy.md:111-113`); (v) internalize trading costs in a dynamic program (Garleanu & Pedersen 2013, *Dynamic Trading with Predictable Returns and Transaction Costs*, *Journal of Finance* [unverified]) rather than a static penalty attributed to Almgren & Chriss (2000) [unverified]; (vi) select the operating point by out-of-sample forecast quality, not by the maximand itself (DeMiguel, Garlappi & Uppal 2009 on estimation error dominating optimization, quoted at `literature_review_strategy.md:104-107`).

**3. What prevents the current unit from reaching it.** Evidenced obstacles: the α scale is not normalized to the variance, so the grid is effectively saturated (U13-ADV-03); the eigenbasis is unstable under Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage, which makes H itself data-fragile (U13-ADV-02); the entropy is non-differentiable where a principal-factor contribution vanishes, so the KKT claim is not established on the whole feasible set (U13-ADV-07); the rebalancing cadence contradicts the validation hold (U13-ADV-10); and the primary metric is the objective (U13-ADV-11). Only apparent: the non-convexity (the SCA subproblem is a concave QP and the Armijo step is well-posed — U13-ADV-07), the μ=0/directional dichotomy (one formulation, μ a removable term — U13-ADV-06), and cardinality NP-hardness as such (the greedy has a termination guarantee; the defect is its post-hoc placement, not the NP-hardness — U13-ADV-08).

**4. Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: long-only, fully invested, 5% position cap, 30% turnover cap, minimum position size, cardinality, and the non-concavity of H (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:812`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:818`). Self-imposed: the seven-point α grid and the λ=1 normalization (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:788`), the elbow rule (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:814`), φ=25 with w̄=3% (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:794`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1044`), the 30% cap equal to the 30% "risk-model instability" diagnostic (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:922`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1050`), the six-month fixed holding (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:878`), the purity of μ=0, and the three "modes" nomenclature.

**5. What should be removed, merged or moved.** Remove: the exploratory mode as a separate mode (it is the λ=0 limit, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:784`); the λ_mkt calibration from the default mode (valid only for directional mean-variance, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:786`); the two-stage decomposition (it solves a different, ill-posed problem — U13-ADV-08); the legacy "Mode 1/Mode 2" and "return-entropy frontier" labels (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:773`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1052`). Merge: directional into the single objective (already stated as "the default mode is the special case μ=0", `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:782`); the α-elbow calibration and the Phase A score into one tuning layer (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:2261`, `ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:2443`). Move: turnover and rebalancing frequency into a single explicit cadence consistent with the OOS protocol; cardinality into the feasible set rather than a post-SCA loop; the 30% turnover diagnostic below the cap.

## Core strengths to preserve

1. **Risk/return modularity.** The risk model is return-free and the return term is a removable linear add-on (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:680`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:782`). This is the correct architecture for a risk mandate and makes independent testing possible; a redesign should keep the separation even if it rejects the "μ=0 is neutral" justification.
2. **The principal-factor rotation.** It is what makes the risk-contribution distribution nonnegative and Shannon entropy defined (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:684-692`). No redesign should compute entropy on signed contributions.
3. **H as log-ENB.** The diversification target is bounded, interpretable and scale-invariant (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:690`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1654`); it is a genuine diversification index, not an ad hoc penalty.
4. **Internalized soft penalties + hard caps as safety limits.** The design principle is sound (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:792`), the penalty terms are convex and conic-representable (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:844`), and the concentration/turnover penalties are correctly shaped.
5. **SCA + Armijo + multi-start.** For a nonconcave H, this is the right class of method: convex subproblems, a verifiable sufficient-decrease condition, and diversification of starts (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:820-831`). Preserve the method, fix the smoothness and the selection rule.
6. **Benchmark parity of the optimization layer.** Same objective, solver, constraints and α procedure across models (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:142-144`, `ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:2151-2158`) is the fairness backbone of the whole comparison; a redesign must not let the VAE and the baselines optimize different objectives.

## Verdict

The mathematical core of U13 holds up: the entropy of principal-factor risk contributions is a well-defined, scale-invariant diversification index, its gradient is correct (I verified the chain rule), the rotation is orthogonal and covariance-preserving, the constrained objective is convex in its penalties, and the SCA-with-Armijo scheme is a defensible way to handle a nonconcave reward. The unit's weaknesses are not in the algebra but in the calibration and protocol layers: α is not normalized to the variance, so the declared grid is almost entirely in the entropy-saturated regime; the eigenbasis on which H depends is unstable under the shrinkage that the risk model relies on; the KKT claim is applied to a non-smooth function and a non-majorizing surrogate; the multi-start selection rule is stated three ways; the rebalancing cadence contradicts the six-month hold and makes the 30% turnover diagnostic vacuous; and the primary metric is the objective itself. These are systemic, but they are repairable within a deep-learning architecture: normalize the trade-off, stabilize the basis, smooth the entropy, unify the cadence, and separate evaluation from the maximand. The modular risk/return separation and the controlled benchmark parity are worth keeping in any redesign.
