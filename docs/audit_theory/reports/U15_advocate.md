## Five questions

1. **Purpose and original intent.** The unit is a falsification harness, not a leaderboard: "la complexité du VAE n'est justifiée que si elle produit un bénéfice OOS mesurable et statistiquement significatif par rapport à des alternatives plus simples, sur les mêmes données, avec les mêmes contraintes, évaluées par les mêmes métriques" (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:5`). The DVT makes it a gate on the whole programme: "the strategy must also be evaluated against benchmark models to determine whether its complexity is justified" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:862`), with an explicit cost claim ("If the VAE strategy does not surpass these benchmarks, its additional complexity is not justified", `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1097`) and a prerequisite status — Phase 0 precedes every VAE iteration (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1088-1090`). The intent is therefore *attribution*: which stage of the deep pipeline earns its keep, and which does not.

2. **What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** Same design, plus: (i) a covariance-estimator ladder that estimates *every* arm with the same estimator family — Ledoit & Wolf (2004, *A well-conditioned estimator for large-dimensional covariance matrices*, JMVA 88(2)) and Ledoit & Wolf (2020, *Analytical nonlinear shrinkage*, AoS 48(5)), the latter applied to the PCA eigenvalues, not only as a min-var sensitivity [unverified]; (ii) evaluation of risk models by the out-of-sample variance of the minimum-variance portfolio they generate — Jagannathan & Ma (2003, *Risk Reduction in Large Portfolios*, JF 58(4)) [unverified]; (iii) the full diversification family, including the most-diversified portfolio of Choueifaty & Coignard (2008, JPM 35(1)) and HRP of López de Prado (2016) [unverified]; (iv) inference on the *search*, not only on the winner: White (2000, *A Reality Check for Data Snooping*, Econometrica 68(5)) or Hansen (2005, SPA, JBES 23(4)), and a deflated Sharpe (Bailey & López de Prado 2014) [unverified]; (v) nested model selection so hyperparameters are not chosen on the evaluation folds — Cawley & Talbot (2010, *On Over-fitting in Model Selection*, JMLR 11) [unverified]; (vi) the deep-vs-linear comparison as in Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2021, *Autoencoder Asset Pricing Models*, J. Econometrics) [unverified], i.e. the same conditional-factor object estimated both ways.

3. **What prevents it from reaching that.** Real obstacles: the PCA control's factor covariance is estimated differently from the VAE's (U15-ADV-04); the composite decision statistic is undefined for two benchmarks (U15-ADV-06); the attribution ladder has one non-nested step and one missing cell (U15-ADV-02); coverage of the strongest linear alternatives is asserted via an unproven nesting claim (U15-ADV-09); the VAE's Phase A selects configs on the same OOS folds used for the comparison (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:894`) while benchmarks get one fit per fold with no search (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:2383`) — an unequal tuning budget that runs *in the VAE's favour*. Only apparent: the exclusion of HRP/MDP looks like a gap but is coherent if the design is read as attributional (U15-ADV-02); the frozen penalty parameters look like a handicap to benchmarks but they are portfolio-level only and mostly parameter-free arms (U15-ADV-01); the 5% cap and 3% soft threshold bind, but identically for all models, so the "constraints compress differences" worry is already pre-registered as a diagnostic (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:305`), not a hidden confound.

4. **Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: a survivorship-free vendor with delisted tickers (user constraint; `Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:15,19,34`); 30 years of daily data, which fixes ~34 folds (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:17,59`); a 1000-name universe in which any covariance model faces T/n ≈ 2.5 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:880`). Self-imposed: exactly six benchmarks; one component per benchmark; the ban on per-benchmark tuning of portfolio penalties (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:48`, `ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1968`); a *single* shared objective (entropy of principal-factor risk contributions) as the yardstick for every model; α calibrated by an elbow rule; and — the deepest one — the decision to judge the deep model only inside the decision rule it was designed for. That last choice makes the test narrower than "is deep learning useful for risk" and wider than "does the encoder beat PCA": it is exactly "does the deep extractor beat the linear extractor inside this pipeline".

5. **What should be removed, merged or moved.** *Remove* the absolutism "exactement la même infrastructure … Toute divergence invalide la comparaison" (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:11`): it is already false at `Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:83` (1/N exempt from turnover) and it hides the asymmetries that actually matter. Removing the sentence and keeping the substance (same universe, folds, constraints, metrics, solver) loses nothing. *Merge* the representation-level gates (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:908,910,955`) with the portfolio-level decision matrix (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:291-309`) into one joint rule with stated precedence; today a model can pass one and fail the other with no arbitration. *Move* the seven diagnostic metrics (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:181-189`) out of the decision table — none of them enters any decision, and keeping them adjacent to the primaries invites post-hoc narrative. *Drop* the second, optional implementation of the PCA+vol control (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:150` offers two variants): pick one, because two versions of a single control double the multiplicity without doubling information. *Move* the Scenario C action out of the matrix per the user decision, keeping the trigger.

## Core strengths to preserve

1. **One universe, one fold calendar, one constraint set, one metric computation.** The substance of `Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:11` is what makes any difference attributable to the risk model rather than to the harness. Keep the substance, discard the absolutist wording.
2. **A linear counterpart that optimizes the identical functional with the identical solver and calibration** (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:142-144`, `ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:2151-2159`). This is the only design that makes "the non-linearity earns its keep" a falsifiable statement rather than an assertion. It must survive any redesign, including a redesign that changes the learned object.
3. **Both ends of the estimation-error spectrum as nulls** — 1/N for "no model" (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:77`) and LW min-variance for "regularized direct covariance" (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:91`). Together they bracket every proposed replacement.
4. **Pre-specified primary metrics, paired fold inference, FWER control** (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:166-175,207-224`). The decision is not a narrative reconstructed after the fact.
5. **A pre-registered outcome→action map with a regime-specific invalidation clause** (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:228,291-309`). The action set must be re-pointed under the user decision; the pre-commitment itself is the asset.
6. **Representation-level dominance gates against the linear baseline** (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:908,910,955`). Requiring the deep model to beat PCA where its value is supposed to be *created* — not only where luck can appear — is the most under-appreciated strength of the corpus.
7. **A survivorship-free panel enforced at three points** (point-in-time universe, delisted-inclusive training universe, Shumway delisting returns: `Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:19,23,34`). Without it the 1/N null is meaningless.

## Verdict

This is the best-engineered unit I have examined: it is a *diagnostic* apparatus rather than a leaderboard, and it is unusually honest about its own failure modes. The three load-bearing pieces are sound — the shared-infrastructure invariant, the component-attribution ladder, and above all the PCA control that reproduces the exact entropy functional with the same solver and the same α procedure, which I verified algebraically. Two things keep it short of state of the art: the control is not neutral at the factor-covariance stage (raw PCA eigenvalues against an LW-shrunk Σ_z), and the decision statistic is undefined for the two benchmarks the protocol itself calls most dangerous. Coverage of the strongest linear alternatives is claimed through an unproven nesting of the entropy objective into maximum diversification, while the corpus's own literature review recommends an arm (HRP) that never appears. The tuning budget is also unequal, but in the direction that weakens the VAE's case, not strengthens it. The architecture is worth keeping essentially intact; what needs redesign is the *claim it supports* — from "beats the strongest alternatives" to "each deep stage beats its own linear ablation" — and the symmetry of the estimation treatment across arms.

Score: 7.5/10
