## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** §4.5 exists to turn the §4.4 objective into a *reproducible estimator of §4.6's exposure matrix B* while refusing to make the training distribution a place where recency is encoded: "The encoder learns a broad vocabulary of risk patterns across **all** historical regimes, not weighted toward the present. Current relevance is handled at inference (Section 4.6), not training." (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:596`). The ISD states the operational intent as one loop with three coupled mechanisms — curriculum batching, early stopping on validation ELBO, ReduceLROnPlateau (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1466`). Three sub-intents are visible and distinct: (i) *batch composition* exists only because the co-movement term needs temporal synchronization (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:518`); (ii) *early stopping* exists to make the epoch count data-driven rather than tuned (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:616`); (iii) *retraining cadence* exists to let the dictionary of shapes track a drifting market without destroying B between refits (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:630-640`, `:676`).

**2. What a state-of-the-art version looks like.** (a) Keep the learned global observation variance and its log-MSE form (ADV-01) — this is the correct VAE training regime for unknown noise scale. (b) Replace L2-in-Adam by decoupled decay (AdamW), because with adaptive preconditioning an L2 term is not weight decay: Loshchilov & Hutter, 2019, *Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization*, ICLR — the claim that L2-with-Adam is equivalent to decoupled decay fails and AdamW converges to better solutions [unverified]. (c) Replace BatchNorm by LayerNorm in the encoder (Ba, Kiros & Hinton, 2016, *Layer Normalization* — per-sample normalization removes the dependence of one sample's encoding on its batch-mates) [unverified]; the corpus already z-scores inputs per window, so BatchNorm's stated purpose (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:334`) is already served. (d) Measure patience in *effective* samples, not nominal epochs (ADV-05). (e) Realize the co-movement constraint per *pair* rather than per *batch* (ADV-04), which is the standard multi-view trick (many independent blocks per batch) [hypothesis: with M blocks per batch, B_eff/M recovers effective batch size proportional to M]. (f) Use weight averaging for the deployment checkpoint (Izmailov et al., 2018, *Averaging Weights Leads to Wider Optima and Better Generalization*, UAI [unverified]) instead of a single restored epoch, which removes most of the E*-transfer problem in ADV-06.

**3. What prevents reaching it — and which obstacles are only apparent.** Real: the pair-level synchronization requirement (ADV-04); sliding-window redundancy, which caps independent information per stock at ≈ `T_hist`/T ≈ 15 (ADV-05); the absence of an operational regime-change trigger (ADV-07). Only apparent: (i) the *shared* validation set is treated as a bias to be argued away (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:620`) — the argument is actually sound, because config comparison happens OOS; the bias is genuinely second-order and correctly handled; (ii) "the training objective is pointwise while the downstream object is a cross-sectional geometry" — this is *not* a training-layer defect: the downstream object is a function of the *distribution* of μ, and the encoder needs no aggregate-level objective to supply it; (iii) "early stopping competes with the KL/auto-pruning" — apparent, since at the σ² optimum the KL and reconstruction are pinned at a fixed ratio (ADV-01).

**4. Real constraints vs self-imposed choices.** Real: point-in-time training cut-off (INV-005, `ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1566`); no validation set available in deployment; pairwise synchronization for Spearman; per-fold recomputation of strata to avoid look-ahead (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:528`). Self-imposed: **batch-wide** synchronization as the reading of a **pairwise** condition (ADV-04); patience expressed in epochs over a 7.06M-window dataset with ~15 effective windows per stock (ADV-05); truncating the learning-rate grid to the top decade, which removes the DVT's own baseline (ADV-09); putting every training hyperparameter inside every fold, which is what forces the compact grid (`implementation_decisions.md:193`); the "as detected" trigger (ADV-07).

**5. What to remove, merge or move.** *Remove*: the DVT:900(b) justification for E* (keep the protocol, ADV-06); the L2 term "on all weights" or its stated mechanism (ADV-08); the sentence "a lower learning rate reduces the risk of the encoder memorizing noise patterns" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:606`) and the bandwagon sentence "every comparable financial VAE in the literature uses it" (`:602`) — neither carries load (ADV-09, ADV-08); the `< 0.85` branch of the overfit diagnostic or its uncalibrated threshold (ADV-05). *Merge*: the κ-means stratification with the B_eff formula as a single named design constraint ("intra-batch decorrelation budget") rather than two separate procedural steps (ADV-04). *Move*: the recency decision out of §4.5's delegated claim into an explicit aggregation-time choice, since §4.6's default is a simple mean (ADV-11); the learning-rate baseline back into the Phase A grid (ADV-09). *Nothing here removes a strength*: ADV-01 through ADV-03, the restore-best-weights rule, the fixed-E* deployment run and the γ/λ_co exclusions all survive untouched.

## Core strengths to preserve

1. **Learned global σ² with β pinned at 1.** ADV-01 proves the recon:KL balance is D/2:1 at every step, independent of data and of γ. Any redesign must keep this property or reproduce it explicitly; it is the main reason the model cannot silently collapse or silently over-reconstruct, and it is what makes γ safe to use at all.
2. **The three-way exclusion rule for the validation signal** (γ out, λ_co out, σ² in) and the **freeze of the scheduler and early stopping during non-stationary phases** (ADV-03). This is the most formally careful part of the corpus and should be generalized into a stated principle: *the monitoring signal must be the deployment functional and must be stationary in composition*.
3. **Scale-free shape learning with analytic scale restoration** (ADV-02). It is what lets one encoder serve all stocks and all regimes and lets a single scalar σ² be well specified; it must not be traded for a richer but absolute-scale input without re-deriving Mode P.
4. **Restore-best-weights plus the fixed-E* deployment run** (ADV-06), including the point-in-time **expanding** median for E*. Keep the protocol; drop the epoch-count justification.
5. **Uniform training over all history paired with periodic full refits** (ADV-07): the cadence is quantitatively grounded and keeps B stable between refits, which is the precondition for the whole downstream pipeline.
6. **The B_eff formula as an explicit design diagnostic** (ADV-04). Very few risk-model papers write down intra-batch gradient correlation; keep the formula and promote it from a justification for stratification to a hard design constraint on batch construction.
7. **Point-in-time, representation-neutral stratification** (ADV-10): trailing-window clustering, recomputed per fold, never fed to the encoder.

The core mechanisms are not merely plausible, they are formally defensible: learned-σ² training pins the reconstruction/KL balance at D/2 independent of the data and of the crisis weight, which makes γ a provably redistribution-only device; the validation signal is specified with real care (γ and λ_co excluded for the right reasons, σ² retained because dropping the log term would make the criterion monotone in σ²); the warmup freeze is a correct response to a non-stationary monitoring signal; and the deployment protocol (fixed E*, expanding point-in-time median, restore-best-weights) is honest about what production can and cannot observe. The defects are of a different kind — unit-of-progress bookkeeping (nominal 7.06M windows versus ~15 effective windows per stock, so "patience in epochs" is a compute budget), a constraint misderivation that turns a pairwise synchronization requirement into a batch-wide one and thereby manufactures the gradient-correlation problem the corpus then only half-fixes, and optimizer/learning-rate settings imported from vision whose justifications do not survive inspection (L2-in-Adam, decay applied to BatchNorm affine parameters, a rationalized LR that the Phase A grid then excludes). None of these requires abandoning the training design; all are localized in the *arguments* rather than the mechanisms, which is exactly the profile an advocate wants: keep the machinery, delete three paragraphs and one invariant.
