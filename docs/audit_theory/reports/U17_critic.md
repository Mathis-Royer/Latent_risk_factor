## Five questions

1. **Purpose and original intent.** The unit is the theory's closing argument plus its fallback ladder: four headline properties (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:17`), a cost/priority-ordered repair list for a base system that "underperforms benchmarks or exhibits specific failure modes" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1086`), and a terminal decision rule (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1192`, restated as a "terminal principle" in `strategy_philosophy.md:183-185`). Its intent is to make the architecture's complexity defensible and to pre-commit to a response to failure. Structurally, the DVT has no conclusion section — headings run Executive Summary, §1–§6, Appendix A, References, TODO (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:7`, `:21`, `:43`, `:93`, `:148`, `:965`, `:1084`, `:1200`, `:1576`, `:1670`) — so the claims and the limitations are never confronted with each other in one place.

2. **What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** A conclusions/contingency layer for a risk model should be a pre-registered claims-and-falsifiers protocol: (i) every property expressed as a hypothesis with a metric, a threshold, a split and a test; (ii) the stability claim tested with proper scoring rules for covariance forecasts and a Diebold–Mariano-type comparison (Laurent, Rombouts & Violante 2013, *J. Econometrics*, "On loss functions and ranking forecasting performances of multivariate volatility models"; Patton 2011, *J. Econometrics*, robust loss functions — both [unverified]); (iii) model comparison across the whole variant set controlled for data snooping (White 2000, *Econometrica*, Reality Check; Hansen 2005, *JBES*, SPA; Hansen, Lunde & Nason 2011, *Econometrica*, Model Confidence Set — all [unverified]); (iv) a one-shot confirmatory test on the reserved holdout rather than repeated 5% tests on the tuning folds; (v) factor identification stated as a separate condition from factor count (Bai & Ng 2002 gives only the count; identification requires normalizations such as unit factor variances and residual orthogonality — Anderson & Rubin 1956 [unverified]); (vi) each claimed property labelled as a property of the *model*, of the *constraint set*, or of the *rebalancing calendar*.

3. **What prevents it from reaching that.** The central claims have no metric (U17-CRI-01), and the two strongest ones are contradicted by the design itself (U17-CRI-02, U17-CRI-03). The terminal test is statistically invalid (U17-CRI-05) and its outcome is inadmissible under the user's decision (U17-CRI-06). The supporting reviews and memos justify their recommendations with sources that do not support them (U17-CRI-07, -08, -09, -10). The roadmap's triggers are largely unoperationalized (U17-CRI-11).

4. **Real constraints vs self-imposed choices.** Real: no CRSP, no results, theory only, and the deep-learning requirement (user decision, not challengeable). Self-imposed: (a) the binary "VAE or PCA" framing of the terminal rule (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1192`); (b) the requirement that the risk model be a factor model `BΣ_zB'+D` with `B` frozen between retrainings (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:676`) — a choice, not a necessity; (c) the premise that "full history" is the only anti-cyclical memory horizon (`strategy_philosophy.md:56`); (d) `μ=0` as the only "pure" mode, which forces every return signal into an external layer; (e) the four-property framing itself.

5. **What should be removed, merged or moved.** Remove: §6.7 and the "terminal principle" *as decision rules* (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1159-1167`, `strategy_philosophy.md:183-185`); the four adjectives as properties rather than hypotheses; the "Validated Changes" label (`literature_review_strategy.md:275`); the `idio_weight` calibration table (`literature_review_idiosyncratic_risk.md:287-297`); the Σ_z memo as a standalone theory document. Merge: §6.2 and §6.3 into one memory-horizon estimator, since they are the same bias-variance knob applied to `B` and to `Σ_z` (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1101-1127`); §6.6 (InfoNCE) into the training objective rather than a post-hoc iteration. Move: turnover and stability from "key properties" to "constraints and diagnostics"; the VAE-vs-PCA test from a terminal rule to a nested loading-estimator test at matched AU; the terminal p-value to the holdout with pre-registration.

**Full derivations for the critical findings.**

*U17-CRI-02.* Take `AU = 2`, `B = I₂`, `Σ_z = λI₂`, `D = dI₂`. Then `Σ_assets = (λ+d)I₂`, and for every orthogonal `Q` the pair `(BQ, Q'Σ_zQ) = (Q, λI₂)` produces the same `Σ_assets`; every `Q` is an admissible principal basis because the eigenvalue is repeated. Write the objective as `max_w −w'Σw + H(w)` (i.e. `α/λ = 1`). Since `w'Σw = (λ+d)‖w‖² = (λ+d)` on the simplex, the risk term is constant and the argmax is argmax `H`. In the basis `B = I`, `H(w) = −Σ_k (w_k²/‖w‖²) ln(w_k²/‖w‖²)`, maximized at `w = (1/√2, 1/√2)` with `H = ln 2`, and equal to 0 at `w = (1,0)`. In the basis `B' = Q` with `Q = 2^{−1/2}[[1,−1],[1,1]]`, the same physical portfolios reverse: `w = (1,0)` has exposure `Q'w = (1/√2, −1/√2)`, hence equal contributions and `H = ln 2`; `w = (1/√2, 1/√2)` has exposure `(1,0)`, hence `H = 0`. Same risk model, same asset covariance, same objective, opposite optimal portfolios. The procedure is therefore undefined at exact degeneracy; near degeneracy gives the continuous version, since eigenvector perturbation is of order `‖ΔΣ̂‖/gap` (Davis–Kahan [unverified]), so with `AU ≈ 80` and a shrunk tail (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:752`) the tail basis rotates with estimation noise between retrainings, carrying `H` and `w*` with it.

*U17-CRI-03.* Let calm days have variance `σ_n²` and crisis days `σ_c²`, with fractions `1−p` and `p`. The full-history equal-weighted covariance has expectation `E[Σ̂] = (1−p)σ_n² + pσ_c²`, so the crisis share is `pσ_c² / ((1−p)σ_n² + pσ_c²)`. With `p = 0.10` and `σ_c = 4σ_n`: `0.1·16 / (0.9 + 0.1·16) = 1.6/2.5 = 64%`. Ten percent of days produce 64% of the estimate, so the effective sample size is far below the nominal 7,560 and is closer to the number of independent crisis episodes. This is the opposite of "maximizes N_eff, minimizes estimation error" (`sigma_z_ewma_justification.md:115`), and it makes the claimed stability depend on how many crises the sample happens to contain, not on its length.

*U17-CRI-05.* With five prerequisite iterations each admitted at a nominal 5% trigger under a global null, `P(at least one false admission) = 1 − 0.95^5 = 0.226`; adding the terminal test, `1 − 0.95^6 = 0.265`. Because the terminal variant is selected on the same folds, the null distribution of the terminal statistic is not the nominal one; the holdout at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:880` is the only clean sample and is not used by the rule.

## Root-cause hypotheses

**RC1 — Property-first framing without falsifiers** (generates U17-CRI-01, -03, -04, -11). The theory starts from four desirable adjectives and builds mechanisms consistent with them, but never defines the metric at which an adjective becomes false. Turnover, stability and bias are treated as design intentions rather than as estimable properties of `w*(B,Σ)`. Alternatives: (a) a claims register mapping each property to metric, threshold, split, test and owning layer — trade-off: forces the theory to name a loss function for the risk model, which the corpus lacks; (b) derive the properties from estimator theory (bias and variance of `Σ̂`, turnover of `w*` across retrainings) — trade-off: analytic work, and the derivation may refute the claims; (c) demote all four to hypotheses tested once on the holdout — trade-off: loses rhetorical force, gains falsifiability.

**RC2 — Unidentified latent factor basis** (generates U17-CRI-02, and feeds U17-CRI-04). The VAE latent space is identified only up to a nonlinear gauge; the principal rotation removes the linear part of `Σ_z` only when eigenvalues are separated. Every downstream object that depends on "the factors" — stability, turnover, interpretability, entropy — inherits the non-identification. Alternatives: (a) add an identification layer (unit-variance orthogonal factors, residual orthogonality) — trade-off: restricts the encoder's flexibility and may hurt reconstruction; (b) replace per-window reconstruction with a cross-sectional conditional likelihood, so factors are defined by their predictive role — trade-off: abandons the "complete historical profile" story and requires a time-varying `B_t`; (c) define the risk model on predicted covariances rather than on `B`, so the latent basis never enters the objective — trade-off: loses interpretability and the factor-entropy objective as written.

**RC3 — Cost-ordered sequential research protocol** (generates U17-CRI-05, -06). Iterations are ordered by cost, evaluated on the same folds, and then a 5% test is applied to the survivor. The protocol maximizes the number of cheap experiments but destroys the validity of the final comparison. Alternatives: (a) pre-register one confirmatory holdout test, with all iterations exploratory on a tuning sample — trade-off: only one confirmatory shot; (b) a Reality Check / model confidence set over the whole variant set — trade-off: requires a common loss and a bootstrap, and returns a set rather than a winner; (c) separate tuning and confirmation samples — trade-off: less data for tuning, valid inference.

**RC4 — Justificatory citation culture** (generates U17-CRI-07, -08, -09, -10). Supporting memos cite authorities for propositions those authorities do not establish, and cite other corpus documents as if they were evidence. Alternatives: (a) a citation contract stating the precise proposition needed, the source's exact result and a verified/unverified flag — trade-off: slower writing, many claims demoted; (b) no intra-corpus authority: no parameter may be justified by another corpus document's assertion — trade-off: several current justifications disappear; (c) a recommendation ledger requiring an evidence type (derivation, external result, experiment) for every item — trade-off: exposes how much is unsupported.

## Verdict

The strongest case for this unit is that it does what few strategy documents do: it front-loads benchmarks before the model (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1090-1097`), pre-commits to abandoning the architecture if it loses (`:1192`), lists its own limitations (`:17`), and separates training-time from inference-time mechanisms. That honesty is real, and §6 contains genuine engineering thought. But naming a failure mode is not testing it.
