## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** The unit must produce, per stock, an exposure vector $\beta_i$ to K latent risk factors, so that the downstream factor model $\Sigma = B\Sigma_z B^T + D_\varepsilon$ can be assembled (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:162`, `:174`, `:187-196`). The architecture is justified on three grounds: a distributional latent space makes inter-stock distance meaningful (`:164`), the bottleneck discards idiosyncratic noise (`:168`), and the KL auto-prunes to a data-determined factor count (`:404-406`, `strategy_philosophy.md:73-82`). The intended deliverable is therefore **a factor-loading matrix whose subspace and metric are risk-relevant**.

**2. What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** The object required is a conditional loading function $\beta_{i,t}=g_\theta(\text{history}_i)$, validated by its ability to explain the *cross-section of returns*, not by its ability to reconstruct a single stock's own window. Exemplars: Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2021), "Autoencoder Asset Pricing Models", *J. Econometrics* — the decoder's loading function is trained against the cross-section of returns [unverified]; Chen, Pelger & Zhu (2024), "Deep Learning in Asset Pricing" — conditional factor model with a GMM moment objective on pricing errors [unverified]; Kelly, Pruitt & Su (2019), IPCA — loadings instrumented by characteristics, identified through the panel likelihood [unverified]. Two structural ingredients are common: (i) the loss **contains the cross-section**, (ii) the latent state is *shared across assets* at each date. For the risk-only variant the analogous target is the residual correlation matrix of $\Sigma$, not the window reconstruction. On identification, the corpus itself quotes Locatello et al. (2019) that unsupervised disentanglement without inductive bias is impossible (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:496`); the literature-side claim that a β-VAE's KL term recovers the true number of generative factors in the identifiable regime requires a *linear, additive decoder with known noise* (Khemakhem et al., 2020 [unverified]) — not one that a CNN-decoder satisfies.

**3. What prevents reaching it.** CRI-01 (no cross-section in the loss, so μ is a shape characteristic, not a loading), CRI-02 (the model contributes only a subspace, with residuals forced diagonal), CRI-05 (the only test of added value is vacuous or arithmetically impossible), CRI-03 (AU is not identified and moves with T·F), CRI-06 (the objective depends on a latent basis the fit does not fix), CRI-08 (the "no a priori bias" claim is contradicted by its own scaffolding).

**4. Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: n ≈ 1000, ~30 years daily, low SNR, no CRSP but a vendor with delisted tickers (`:291`), universe flexibility (a fixed-universe cross-attention model is rejected on this ground, `:542`), point-in-time correctness. Self-imposed: K = 200 as a ceiling rather than a searched quantity; one window length T = 504; InceptionTime/ResNet defaults inherited wholesale (`:327-338`); the decision to make the *encoder* the exposure producer and discard the decoder (`:170`); the 0.01-nat AU threshold; per-window z-scoring of *both* channels (`:226-248`), which erases the scale the risk model needs; the "5 variable parameters" framing (`:321`); the demand EP > EP_PCA + 0.10 (`:475`); the capacity ratio as a feasibility gate (`:366-382`).

**5. What should be removed, merged or moved.**
- **Remove** the capacity-data ratio as a feasibility gate (denominator inflated by ~T, CRI-04); the claim that AU is the discovered factor count (CRI-03); the 0.01-nat threshold as the *definition* of the factor count; the claim in `:692` that the principal-factor rotation resolves the latent indeterminacy (CRI-06); the EP > EP_PCA + 0.10 criterion (CRI-05); the single-T design (CRI-10).
- **Merge** the four loss mechanisms (reconstruction, KL with learned σ², co-movement curriculum, γ crisis weighting; `:586`) into **one likelihood over the cross-section**, and merge the encoder output with the OLS factor recovery into a single estimated object — the model should emit the covariance, not a representation to which a covariance is later fitted.
- **Move** the exposure producer from the encoder to the **decoder**: in $r_t = B z_t + \varepsilon_t$ the loading is a map *from* the factor state *to* returns; the corpus inverts the roles and then discards the half that has the right parameterization (`:170`). Move the AU definition from training-time KL nats to the estimation stage where it can be tested. Move the volatility rescaling *into* the model (a scale-aware likelihood) instead of restoring it with a post-hoc diagonal ratio (`:724-742`).

## Root-cause hypotheses

**RC1 — The unit optimizes a proxy instead of the target object (generates CRI-01, -02, -04; aggravates -07).** Window reconstruction is a *within-stock* objective; the risk model needs a *cross-sectional* object. Because the two are never connected by a term, everything the VAE contributes collapses to a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$ (CRI-02), and the only cross-sectional validation available reduces to a bound PCA already attains (CRI-04). Three structural alternatives:
- *(a) Invert the generative direction.* Make the factor state $z_t$ shared across assets and the decoder the loading map: $r_t \mid z_t \sim \mathcal N(B_\theta(\text{history})z_t, D_\varepsilon)$. The loss becomes the panel likelihood — exactly the object the risk model needs. Trade-off: amortized inference over the whole panel, cross-sectional batches, and the loss is dominated by n and T rather than by n×windows.
- *(b) Keep the per-stock encoder but make the objective the covariance.* Differentiate through $\hat z_t=(B^TB)^{-1}B^Tr_t$ and add a differentiable residual-correlation penalty to the loss. Trade-off: high-variance gradients, strong temptation for the model to shrink $\Sigma$ toward diagonal, and it needs the OLS inside the training loop.
- *(c) Two-stage with an explicit low-rank-plus-diagonal likelihood.* Train the network as a feature map, then fit a factor-analysis MLE (not a projection) with a nonlinear loading map, so that off-diagonal residual structure is *modelled* rather than assumed away. Trade-off: loses the "one deep model" claim and reintroduces a linear estimation stage — but that stage is where the risk model already lives.

**RC2 — Identification and invariance are assumed, never established (generates CRI-03, -06; explains the AU truncation).** The corpus builds an elaborate identification narrative (AU, KL elbow, principal-factor rotation) on quantities that are either metric-dependent (nats) or symmetry-restricted (orthogonal group). Three alternatives:
- *(a) Construct the invariance rather than assert it:* whiten the latent code exactly by construction, and define AU as the count of directions with non-negligible contribution to the *systematic covariance*, not to the KL. Trade-off: gives up the auto-pruning story but yields a count with a sampling interpretation.
- *(b) Select the factor count by a penalized criterion on the asset covariance* (an information criterion on the factor model's residual correlation, in the spirit of Bai–Ng but evaluated on the object of interest). Trade-off: reintroduces a threshold — one that has a sampling theory.
- *(c) Redefine the diversification objective in a basis-invariant way:* entropy over the risk contributions of the eigenvectors of the *asset* covariance restricted to the systematic subspace. Trade-off: an $n\times n$ eigen-decomposition and a loss of the AU-dimensional efficiency, but $H$ becomes invariant by construction.

**RC3 — Rule-driven specification substituting for ablation (generates CRI-05, -09, -10).** The architecture is fixed by inherited constants and reverse-engineered sizing rules, then "verified" by a table whose entries are computed from the same rules. Alternatives:
- *(a) Replace the cascade with one explicit budget* (effective sample size × model capacity) and spend the saved degrees of freedom on the nested CV already used for λ_co, γ and the learning rate. Trade-off: the effective sample size is itself an assumption, so it must be reported as such.
- *(b) Freeze one axis (width) and let the search cover the others.* Trade-off: less expressive nonlinearity, but every remaining constant is then justified by an ablation rather than by a citation.
- *(c) Derive T from the target rather than from a compromise:* the factor half-lives the strategy claims to capture (structural stability > 0.90, style half-life > 25 months, `strategy_philosophy.md:48-49`) imply multi-year memory, which forces a hierarchical/multi-scale representation instead of one window length.

**RC4 — "Assertion + citation + closing table" as the argumentative mode (generates CRI-05, -07, -08; sustains the unfalsifiability).** Statements such as "A VAE with K=200 … converges to AU ≈ 80" (`:406`), "distances are meaningful" (`:164`) and "r is conservative vs ImageNet" (`:372`) are written as settled facts with a reference attached, and each terminates the question instead of opening a test. Alternatives: (a) convert every architectural assertion into a pre-registered A/B with a stated effect size and stopping rule before the design is frozen; (b) report the selected architecture's OOS score with the selection cost accounted for; (c) reduce the model to the smallest one that can express the claimed nonlinearity and run the nonlinear-vs-linear ablation *at equal factor count* — the one experiment the corpus currently lacks (CRI-04).

## Verdict

**Steelman.** The downstream machinery is genuinely good: the factor model is standard, the principal-factor rotation is correctly derived, the LW shrinkage target is sensible, and — decisively for fairness — the benchmark document explicitly pits $\Sigma=B\Sigma_zB^T+D_\varepsilon$ against a direct Ledoit–Wolf covariance, declaring the VAE's value added to be exactly the question (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:99`, `:91`). The system as a whole therefore has a falsification path, and the philosophy's rejection of human-defined categories is a defensible premise. **Rebuttal.** That path tests the *pipeline*, not the *unit*, and a failure would be unattributable. Within the unit, the central bridge — "the encoder's mean vector *is* the exposure vector" (`:174`) — is asserted without any term in the objective that could make it true: the likelihood is per-stock and per-window (`:446`, `:496`), the one cross-sectional term is a linear-correlation scaffolding removed for the last 40% of training (`:512`), the decoder that would carry a loading parameterization is discarded (`:170`), the posterior variance is dropped at inference (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1035`), and the fit is invariant under any invertible reparameterization while the objective the optimizer maximizes is not (CRI-02, CRI-06). The auto-pruning story that carries §6 of the philosophy is arithmetically a function of $T\cdot F$ (CRI-03), and the single quantitative test of added value is either vacuous or forbidden by the Ky Fan bound (CRI-04). What survives is a defensible *heuristic*: a nonlinear, scale-free shape descriptor of each stock's own history, used as an instrumented loading, with volatility and covariance estimated entirely by classical means downstream. That is not what the documents claim, and the gap is architectural rather than editorial. Fixing it means moving the exposure producer to the decoder side and putting the cross-section into the loss — not tuning K, T, or the KL threshold.

Score: 4/10
