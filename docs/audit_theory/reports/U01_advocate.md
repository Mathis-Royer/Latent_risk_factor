## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** The unit fixes what the strategy is *for*: "not to maximize returns through a predictive algorithm, but to **minimize risk through optimal diversification**" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:25`), on the epistemological claim that risk modelling requires being "right about the present structure" rather than the future (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:29`), that human categories under-describe co-movement (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:33`), and that factors should be discovered rather than named (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:37-39`), with `μ = 0` as default and return views as a removable layer (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:15`, `strategy_philosophy.md:13`). Intent: make co-movement the learned object and the portfolio a deterministic projection of it.

**2. What a state-of-the-art version of this unit would look like.** It would state the premise as an *estimability* statement with effective sample sizes (Merton 1980, "On estimating the expected return on the market", JFE — variances are estimable from short samples while expected returns are not [unverified]; Lo 2002, "The Statistics of Sharpe Ratios", FAJ — s.e. of annualised Sharpe ≈ √((1+SR²/2)/T) [unverified]); it would treat the loading matrix as *conditional* on state or characteristics rather than a single full-history object (Kelly, Pruitt & Su 2019, "Characteristics are covariances", JFE, IPCA [unverified]; Gu, Kelly & Xiu 2021, "Autoencoder asset pricing models", J. Econometrics [unverified]); it would model time-varying second moments explicitly (Engle 2002, "Dynamic Conditional Correlation", JBES [unverified]) and tail dependence as a separate object (Longin & Solnik 2001, "Extreme correlation of international equity markets", JF — correlation rises in the tail [unverified]); and it would be judged on covariance forecasts with a robust loss (Patton 2011, "Volatility forecast comparison using imperfect volatility proxies", J. Econometrics — only QLIKE/MSE rank consistently under noisy proxies [unverified]) plus economic value (Fleming, Kirby & Ostdiek 2001, "The economic value of volatility timing", JF [unverified]). In short: *risk is estimable, returns are not, and what is estimable is conditional*.

**3. What prevents it.** Only-apparent obstacles: the absence of a return forecast (a correct statistical position, not a limitation); latent non-identification (invariance, not a defect — ADV-04); price-only input (Σ is *defined* by returns, so nothing is missing for this objective); "a VAE is not a factor model" (the cross-sectional OLS at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:748` makes it one — the network only proposes `B`). Real obstacles: "true sources" is never operationalised (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:9`); a static full-history `B` contradicts the corpus's own regime language (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:29`, `:1109`); the episodic-memory mechanism is self-referential (ADV-07); the capacity guard contradicts the effective-sample statement (ADV-03); and the one advantage over PCA — tail/conditional co-movement — is precisely the object with the smallest effective sample.

**4. Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: non-CRSP end-of-day data with delistings; ~30 years of daily history; n≈1000; AU/n ratio; non-concavity of `H(w)` (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:818`); rotation symmetry; return signal-to-noise. Self-imposed: "VAE" as the fixed architecture (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:11`); K≤200; F=2 (`:182`); per-window z-scoring that erases the volatility level later re-injected by rescaling (`:240`); equal-weight full-history Σ_z presented as a principle (`strategy_philosophy.md:56`) while the same corpus calls it a limitation (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:17`, `:1123`); `μ = 0` as a *default* rather than a derived optimum; "no a priori bias" as an absolute.

**5. What to remove, merge or move.** *Remove*: the Renaissance win-rate figure (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:27`) — an unverifiable result doing rhetorical work that the estimability argument does better; "no a priori bias" (`:17`, `:37`); "Distances between stocks are therefore meaningful and comparable" (`:164`); the MaxEnt/Jaynes gloss (`strategy_comparison.md:50`) — keep only the exact ERC equivalence; "low turnover" as a vision property (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:17`), which follows from the turnover penalty (`:798-802`), not from factor discovery. *Merge*: the graph metaphor (`:60-64`, `:672`) into "continuous exposure matrix" — no graph object is ever used, and the analogy invites ADV-05's inference. *Move*: §3 fundamentals (`:93-143`) out of the justification chain (the pipeline consumes prices only, so the section defends a negative); the VaR/CVaR taxonomy (`:84-88`) out of the premise chain; "anti-cyclical by construction" downgraded from guarantee to "no forced forgetting". None of these removals touches a strength listed below.

## Core strengths to preserve

1. **Decision-level invariance to latent identification** (ADV-04). A redesign may replace the VAE freely as long as only `span(B)` and Σ_z feed the decision; this removes the need for disentanglement, naming, or axis alignment, and is the licence for any architecture change.
2. **Structure-based parsimony** (ADV-02). The `BΣ_zBᵀ + D_ε` parameterisation, with diagonal D_ε and the explicit statement that all inter-stock correlation passes through the factors (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:761`), is a testable, well-posed prior. Keep it; count `B`'s degrees of freedom honestly.
3. **`μ = 0` as a derived statistical position, not an omission** (ADV-01). Preserve the conclusion; derive it from the estimability arithmetic rather than asserting "neutrality".
4. **Second-moment sufficiency as a design discipline** (ADV-09). It keeps the learned object aligned with the objective and prevents scope creep into tail claims the data cannot support.
5. **Separation of risk model and return views, with independent risk-model validation** (ADV-10). This is what makes a deep-learning redesign falsifiable layer by layer instead of only end-to-end.
6. **The falsifiability commitment itself** (ADV-11) — keep the commitment, re-aim the trigger.

## Verdict

The unit's core chain is logically tight and, in three places, provably correct: the tractability premise is true as a statement about second-moment estimability (and the arithmetic that supports it also demolishes any expectation of resolving small Sharpe differences); the low-rank-plus-diagonal risk structure is a genuine and quantifiable parsimony prior; and the decision depends only on the span of `B` and Σ_z, so the VAE's non-identifiability costs nothing. The refutation of a priori categories is valid as far as it goes. What does not hold as written is the *strength* of three claims: "no a priori bias" (three contradicting inductive priors are declared inside the same document), "anti-cyclical by construction" (a ~1/15 echo plus a dormant-regime λ_k is memory, not protection), and the implicit claim that `α/λ` is a portable diversification price (it is variance-scaled, and its elbow is read off an unnormalised frontier). The corpus's capacity arithmetic also contradicts its own effective-sample statement by two orders of magnitude. None of these is fatal to the vision; all of them are removable without losing a single strength — which is the strongest thing an advocate can say about a premise layer.
