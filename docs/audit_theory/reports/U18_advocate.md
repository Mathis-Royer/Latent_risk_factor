## Five questions
1. **Purpose and original intent.** `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:967` states that §5 “consolidates all parameters that define a concrete instance of the strategy” and that they are “the axes of analysis during implementation and backtesting”; the diamond marker distinguishes alternatives from priorities (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:971`). The ISD adds the binding layer: conventions (L41–54) and invariants (L56–174) that a valid parameter combination must not violate. The intent is therefore threefold: reproducibility of a concrete instance, an explicit and bounded search space, and protection of the model’s mathematical identities (D-scaling, scalar σ², AU truncation, dual rescaling, mode exclusivity).

2. **What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** A modern parameter-governance layer would have: (i) one registry with provenance — derived by formula, frozen by design decision, or tuned on data; (ii) a joint-feasibility filter so ranges are not presented as a Cartesian product when the constraints couple them; (iii) nested walk-forward selection for *all* free parameters, with a final untouched holdout; (iv) random or Bayesian search rather than coarse grids when only a few parameters matter (Bergstra & Bengio, 2012, *Random Search for Hyper-Parameter Optimization*, JMLR: random search dominates grid search when the effective dimensionality is low [unverified]); (v) selection-bias accounting (Cawley & Talbot, 2010, *On Over-fitting in Model Selection and Subsequent Selection Bias in Performance Evaluation*, JMLR: selection on the same resampling used for reporting is optimistically biased [unverified]; López de Prado, 2018, *Advances in Financial Machine Learning*: purged/embargoed CV and combinatorial purged CV [unverified]); (vi) multiple-testing-adjusted inference (Bailey & López de Prado, 2014, *The Deflated Sharpe Ratio*, JPM [unverified]); (vii) VAE-specific identifiability: one of β and learned σ² at a time (Higgins et al., 2017, *beta-VAE*, ICLR [unverified]; Dai & Wipf, 2019, *Diagnosing and Enhancing VAE Models*, ICLR [unverified]; Lucas et al., 2019, *Don’t Blame the ELBO!*, NeurIPS [unverified]); (viii) a measured, not assumed, active-unit count (Burda, Grosse & Salakhutdinov, 2016, *Importance Weighted Autoencoders*, ICLR: inactive units collapse to the prior [unverified]); (ix) a factor-count floor from Bai & Ng (2002), *Determining the Number of Factors in Approximate Factor Models*, Econometrica [unverified], and Ledoit & Wolf (2004) shrinkage [unverified] — as floors, not replacements.

3. **What prevents the current unit from reaching it.** The obstacles are governance gaps, not wrong individual values: the inventory is not closed (U18-ADV-09), the effective-sample-size correction is not propagated to the capacity ratio (U18-ADV-02), the ranges are not jointly feasible (U18-ADV-03), the portfolio penalties escape the nested protocol (U18-ADV-08), and two invariants are false as written (U18-ADV-04, U18-ADV-06). *Only apparent*: the r-ratio is explicitly a nominal budget, and the document says overfitting protection comes from KL/dropout/weight decay/OOS, not window count (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:368`); the DVT’s AU formula slip is corrected by the ISD’s form (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:82, 222`); the OOS fold scores are selection-influenced but the final holdout is clean (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:880`; `strategy_philosophy.md:180`); the 0.01 nats AU threshold is openly called heuristic (`literature_review_strategy.md:236`); the PCA baseline is a floor, not a fallback (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:416`).

4. **Real constraints vs. self-imposed choices.** *Real*: the free-parameter count AU(AU+1)/2 and finite N_obs; the overlap of sliding windows; the non-concavity of H(w) (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:818`); the scale invariance of the encoder; point-in-time; the n² pair cost; the user’s deep-learning requirement. *Self-imposed*: K = 200, T = 504, r_max = 5, the 0.01 nats threshold, the 30/30/40 curriculum, the 21-day embargo, λ = 1.0, γ = 3.0, w̄ = 3%, w_max = 5%, w_min = 0.10%, φ = 25, κ1 = 0.1, κ2 = 7.5, δ̄ = 1%, τ_max = 30%, the [P5, P95] winsorization, r_min = 2, MDD_threshold = 0.20, λ_pen = 5, λ_est = 2. The “resolved” label conflates *derived by formula* (L, C_L, listing history = T, N_train), *frozen by decision* (T, architecture, s_inf), and *selected from data* (K, γ, λ_co, η0).

5. **What should be removed, merged or moved.** *Remove*: λ_est (dominated by the AU guard — U18-ADV-07), σ²₀ as a tunable (it is an initialization), the weekly-frequency alternative unless every day-denominated parameter is converted (U18-ADV-11), the “only α/λ matters” claim (U18-ADV-06), and the duplicate “structural hyperparameters K, T, architecture” list (T and architecture are already resolved). *Merge*: DVT §5 and the ISD glossary/invariants into one registry with a provenance column; AU threshold and AU_max_stat into one effective-factor module; curriculum and early stopping into one stopping-rule module. *Move*: portfolio-penalty calibration into the nested Phase A protocol; sensitivity analysis from §5.2 into §4.8. These removals cost no strength: the sizing rules, K/AU separation, dual rescaling, σ² = MSE parameterization, mode exclusivity, final holdout and normalized entropy score all remain.

## Core strengths to preserve
- **The closed-form sizing map (T, K) → (L, C_L, channel progression).** It makes the architecture a deterministic function of two interpretable structural parameters, so any redesign can preserve reproducibility and compare architectures on a fixed footing (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:340–354`). Condition: keep the fixed constants explicit; they are conventions, not laws.
- **The K-ceiling / AU-effective-count / AU_max_stat separation.** It lets the learned representation be richer than the downstream covariance estimate, and it uses the model’s own KL signal instead of an external factor-count rule (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:404–436, 714–720`). This is the right division between learning and estimation; a redesign that collapses K to AU loses it.
- **The two-scale exposure restoration with cross-sectional winsorization.** It is the correct bridge between a scale-free encoder and raw returns, and it prevents a single outlier from dominating the factor regression (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:724–744`; `ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:89–97, 133–141`).
- **The learned scalar σ² with the D/(2σ²) + D/2 ln σ² parameterization.** It makes the reconstruction/KL balance data-driven and removes β from the primary mode; the σ² = MSE identity is exact (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:458–468`).
- **The mutual exclusivity of loss modes.** One balancing mechanism at a time is the minimum discipline for interpretability; keep it even if the loss is redesigned (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:111–120`).
- **The nested validation/OOS split plus the final untouched holdout, and the normalized entropy score H/ln(AU).** The holdout is the only uncontaminated evaluation (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:880`; `strategy_philosophy.md:180`); the normalization removes the mechanical AU advantage and gives a dimensionless selection criterion (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:933–951`). Keep both, and pair the normalization with an AU floor.
- **The baseline/range/reference format of §5.** It makes the search space explicit and auditable; a redesign should keep the format but add a provenance column (derived / frozen / tuned) and a joint-feasibility check.

## Verdict
This unit is strong as a specification and weak as a governance system. The individual values are mostly defensible: the sizing rules are correct, the K/AU separation is a genuine architectural asset, the dual rescaling is arithmetically sound, and the σ² = MSE parameterization is exact. The weaknesses are structural, not local: the inventory is not closed (U18-ADV-09), the effective-sample-size correction is not propagated to the capacity ratio (U18-ADV-02), the parameter ranges are not jointly feasible (U18-ADV-03), the portfolio penalties sit outside the nested protocol (U18-ADV-08), and two displayed invariants are false as written (U18-ADV-04, U18-ADV-06). These are removable or mergeable defects, not reasons to abandon the parameterization; fixing them would make the unit a genuine contract between the methodological and operational documents.
