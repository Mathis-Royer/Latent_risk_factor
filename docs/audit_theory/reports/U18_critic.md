## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** §5 states its own remit: it "consolidates all parameters that define a concrete instance of the strategy" and constitutes "the axes of analysis during implementation and backtesting" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:967`). So the unit is answerable for three things: completeness, a design space that is actually usable, and reproducibility of one configuration. Appendix A's declared remit runs the other way — derive the whole architecture from five parameters and "verify the capacity-data constraint" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1202`, `:1224-1233`) — i.e. it is the unit's derivation engine. Both remits fail below, and they fail for different reasons: the inventory because it has no provenance or hierarchy, the derivation engine because it has no data term.

**2. State of the art for this objective.** A defensible parameter system for a deep factor-risk model has four features the current one lacks: (i) an explicit partition of parameters into *derived* (closed-form from other parameters), *structural* (a small set chosen a priori), and *tuned* (with a declared tuning sample disjoint from the reporting sample), i.e. nested selection — Arlot & Celisse (2010), *A survey of cross-validation procedures for model selection* [unverified]; (ii) a capacity constraint expressed on **effective** (non-overlapping) information, not raw sample counts; (iii) a learned object whose free parameters do not scale as n×K — the characteristic-conditioned autoencoder of Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2020), *Autoencoder asset pricing models* [unverified], makes the loading a learned function shared across stocks instead of a free n×K matrix; (iv) operating-point selection that is scale-free and pre-registered, and performance claims adjusted for the number of configurations tried — White (2000) *A Reality Check for Data Snooping*, Bailey & López de Prado (2014) *The Deflated Sharpe Ratio*, Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado & Zhu (2014) *Pseudo-mathematics and financial charlatanism* [all unverified]. The corpus instead uses a fixed threshold inherited from a diagnostic (Burda, Grosse & Salakhutdinov 2016, active units [unverified]) as a factor-count rule, and an undefined "elbow" as a weight.

**3. What prevents it.** Four mechanisms, each evidenced: the capacity guard is evaluated on a sample count the document itself declares invalid (U18-CRI-01); selection and reporting share the same OOS period, so every reported number is a selected maximum (U18-CRI-02); the guard's own algebra is wrong and exists in three mutually inconsistent forms (U18-CRI-03); the σ²/ELBO guard is inverted so the primary failure detector cannot fire (U18-CRI-04). Secondary blockers: α is a derived-from-the-fit quantity masquerading as a free weight (U18-CRI-05), the capacity diagnostics are structurally unfalsifiable (U18-CRI-06), the inventory is incomplete and carries stale rows (U18-CRI-07), part of the selection criterion is identically zero (U18-CRI-08).

**4. Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: the ~34 semi-annual folds, the n≈1000 cross-section, the AU(AU+1)/2 parameters of Σ_z, the effective independence of overlapping windows, and a non-CRSP vendor history of unknown (probably <30 y) length. Self-imposed: K=200 as a capacity ceiling with its "1.5–2×" rule; the three loss modes; the 0.01-nats switch; the σ² clamp; five bang-bang portfolio parameters (`w̄`, `w_max`, `w_min`, `κ1`, `κ2`) tuned against one another; α defined by an elbow; β treated as a free weight when the ELBO fixes it at 1; the choice to compare 32 configurations on the reporting OOS; and the "resolved"/"◆" labels that convert untested choices into closed questions. The last is the most consequential: it converts an assumption into an axiom.

**5. What should be removed, merged or moved.** *Remove*: `λ_est` (identically zero, U18-CRI-08); the `r = P_total/N` ratio computed at s=1 (U18-CRI-01); the upper σ² clamp and the σ²-based collapse detector (U18-CRI-04); Mode A, which reproduces exactly the interaction INV-006 forbids; the "◆" and "resolved" epistemic categories; the 0.01-nats hard switch; and the three-row split of one degree of freedom (`λ`, `α`, "only α/λ matters"). *Merge*: `λ`, `α`, the elbow threshold and its fallback `α=0.1` into a single scale-free operating point (a pre-registered target normalized entropy Ĥ*, with α solved for); `MDD_threshold`/`λ_pen` into a hard drawdown constraint rather than a buying-back of diversification; `T_année`/min-training/OOS length/holdout into one calendar identity with one free parameter (U18-CRI-10); the ~40-row inventory into three tiers with provenance and one declared tuning sample. *Move*: configuration selection out of the OOS fold into an inner embargoed window (U18-CRI-02); the factor-count decision out of the KL threshold into a test on an explicitly estimated loading matrix (U18-CRI-06).

## Root-cause hypotheses

**RC-A — Correctness by invariant rather than by derivation.** The unit's authority rests on an invariant table and a capacity ratio, both of which assert instead of derive. This single cause generates U18-CRI-01 (guard on the wrong N), -03 (guard algebra wrong), -04 (detector inverted), -06 (threshold as switch), -09 (constants as citations). *Alternatives*: (a) require every derived parameter to be produced by an equation that is numerically re-derived from its inputs in the document itself (a one-page "derivation sheet"), with the trade-off that it fixes the algebra but not the modeling choices; (b) replace invariant tests by generative validation — simulate panels with a known number of factors and require the pipeline to recover it, which tests the guard instead of restating it, at the cost of a simulation design; (c) demote the invariant table to a conventions table with a provenance column (derived / prior / tuned), which is cheap but abandons the claim of mathematical necessity.

**RC-B — One-layer evaluation: selection and reporting share the same sample.** U18-CRI-02, -08, and the holdout arithmetic in -10. The framework treats "not used for gradient descent" as "not used for fitting", which is why 32 configurations can be compared per fold on the fold's own score. *Alternatives*: (a) nested walk-forward — an inner embargoed selection window inside each training period, outer OOS untouched — which is the only design that makes the reported distribution interpretable, at the cost of shortening the effective OOS span (the calendar is already saturated, U18-CRI-10); (b) pre-register one configuration from theory and select nothing on OOS, which restores interpretability and removes 31/32 of the search but forfeits adaptivity; (c) report the full configuration distribution with a reality-check / deflated-Sharpe adjustment instead of the selected maximum, which keeps the search and prices it honestly.

**RC-C — No accounting of effective information in dependent data.** Overlapping windows and cross-sectional correlation are acknowledged once (`DVT…:1076`) and then ignored whenever a sample size is needed. Generates U18-CRI-01, -06, -10. *Alternatives*: (a) put an effective-sample term into every sizing rule (N_eff = n·T_hist/T) so the architecture must shrink with the data, at the cost of a much smaller network; (b) replace stride-1 overlapping windows with a fixed number of non-overlapping blocks per stock, making the composite profile a designed estimator with a computable variance, at the cost of fewer training examples; (c) keep overlapping windows but treat B as an estimated matrix and propagate its error covariance into Σ_z (errors-in-variables), which is the statistically honest route but requires an explicit loading-error model that a KL threshold cannot supply.

**RC-D — Parameter provenance by changelog.** Values move and old rows survive: K 38→150→200, AU_max_stat 19→79→85/59, α fallback 0.1, batch 128 vs 256. Generates U18-CRI-03, -06, -07. *Alternatives*: (a) a single machine-checked parameter registry in which each value carries a provenance tag and a supersession date, at the cost of bookkeeping discipline; (b) make K and AU_max_stat *outputs* — report them per fold, never set them — which removes two parameters and makes the factor count a result; (c) freeze the parameter vector at a stated version and forbid post-hoc edits without a new version, which buys auditability and loses the ability to repair mistakes silently.

## Verdict

As a checklist, §5 is more thorough than most specifications: it names ~40 parameters, gives each a baseline and a range, and cross-references the section that uses it. As a *parameter system* it does not work. It has no partition into derived, structural and tuned quantities; the derived ones are computed three different ways (AU_max_stat = 59/79/85), one of them from an algebraically wrong equation; the capacity constraint that is supposed to protect the whole design is evaluated on a sample count the document itself declares invalid, and is invariant to the stride actually used; the largest "free" weight (α) is in fact derived from the fitted covariance by an undefined elbow with a fallback constant, in units the corpus never states; a quarter of the selection criterion is identically zero; and every reported OOS number is the maximum of 4 or 32 configurations selected on the same window that is then reported, so the "distribution across 34 folds" is a distribution of selected maxima of a strategy that changes identity each fold. The steelman — that these are engineering defaults, cheap to revise — fails because the parameters are load-bearing for the strategy's claims: they determine the factor count, the diversification price, and the capacity to which the estimator is entitled. This is not a list of local errors; it is the absence of a parameter architecture. Score reflects that the list is complete enough to be audited at all, which is worth something.

Score: 3/10
