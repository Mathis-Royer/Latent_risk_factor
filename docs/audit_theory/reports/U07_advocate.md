## Five questions

1. **Purpose and original intent.** The loss is specified to do three jobs at once: reconstruct each per-window normalized return/volatility path (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:444-448`); regularize the latent geometry toward `N(0,1)` so that unused dimensions deactivate and inter-stock distances remain meaningful (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:452-456`); and inject information reconstruction cannot supply — relational structure through the co-movement term (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:494-504`) and regime priority through crisis weighting (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:544-552`). The ISD calls this module "the mathematical core of the VAE — any error here silently invalidates training" (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1293`). The intent is therefore not likelihood estimation for its own sake, but shaping a representation whose geometry is usable as a risk model.

2. **What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** For this objective, the primary loss would be a cross-sectional factor-model likelihood rather than a per-stock autoencoder: `r_t | B_t ~ N(0, B_t Σ_z B_t^T + D_ε)`, or a Student-t/copula version for tails, with `B_t` and `Σ_z` learned jointly. Identifiability would be addressed with auxiliary variables, as in Khemakhem et al. (2020), "Variational Autoencoders and Nonlinear ICA: A Unifying Framework", AISTATS, which gives identifiability up to permutation and linear transform under auxiliary variables [unverified]; temporal auxiliary variables in the same spirit are Hyvärinen & Morioka (2016), "Unsupervised Feature Extraction by Time-Contrastive Learning and Nonlinear ICA" [unverified]. The closest deep-finance precedent is Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2021), "Autoencoder Asset Pricing Models", Journal of Econometrics, which uses reconstruction but evaluates the learned factors in a pricing model [unverified]. A modern version would keep a reconstruction auxiliary term, make the cross-sectional covariance the primary term, use heteroscedastic per-feature noise, and replace the three-phase curriculum with a persistent contrastive or covariance-matching term — the direction the corpus itself proposes in §6.6 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1149-1157`). Selection would use held-out predictive log-likelihood, not only the training ELBO.

3. **What prevents the current unit from reaching it; apparent obstacles.** Real: the final Phase-3 objective contains no term that depends on the cross-sectional covariance or on the composite `μ̄_i` (ADV-11); the Gaussian scalar-noise likelihood is fragile to fat tails and to different residual scales across the two channels (ADV-01); the active-unit count is a coordinate-dependent heuristic (ADV-12). Apparent: rotational indeterminacy — the risk model is invariant to any invertible reparameterization of `B` (ADV-03); the alleged `σ²`/`β` double-counting — `σ² = MSE` regardless of `β`, so `β` is not absorbed (ADV-02); the global level of `γ` — only its profile is identified, which the `η_eff` normalization already measures (ADV-09); per-window z-scoring — scale is restored downstream (ADV-10); the shared validation set — disclosed, and hyperparameter comparison uses the OOS score (ADV-07). The unit is therefore closer to alignment than a first reading suggests; the genuine gap is the removal of the relational term and the never-optimized composite.

4. **Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: per-window z-scoring to avoid look-ahead (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:232-240`); the `K ≪ T` bottleneck; the isotropic prior needed for a tractable KL; the Gaussian likelihood for tractability; excluding `γ` and `λ_co` from the validation ELBO (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:622`). Self-imposed: `β` fixed at 1 plus the three-mode taxonomy, although the mathematics gives one binary switch (ADV-02); a single scalar `σ²` shared by both features (could be per-feature); Gaussian rather than heavy-tailed noise; the fixed 30/30/40 curriculum (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:510-512`); `λ_co^max = 0.5` (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:514`); cosine distance (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:504`); the 0.01-nat AU threshold (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:714`); and the claim of "no hyperparameter" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:484`), which ignores the clamps `[10^-4, 10]` (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:468`) and the remaining `γ`/`λ_co`. The clamps are real constraints, but their values are design choices.

5. **What should be removed, merged or moved.** Remove Mode A as a separate mode and delete the incorrect "`σ²` absorbs `β`" warning (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:492`); merge it into a single binary: `σ²` learned (profile) or frozen (`β`/annealing). Remove the per-dimension KL threshold as the factor-counting device; keep the KL as a regularizer and read the count from a coordinate-invariant object (rank/eigenvalues of `Σ_z`, or the spectrum of `B`). Remove the three-phase curriculum and move the co-movement term from a temporary scaffold to a permanent, low-weight part of the objective — the term already exists, so this is a move, not an addition. Remove the "no hyperparameter" claim. Keep the D-scaled Gaussian likelihood, the learned `σ²`, the KL, the ELBO discipline, the shape/scale division of labor, and the relational term. The unit's algebra is sound, but its mode taxonomy, its AU threshold and its temporary-scaffold design are removable without losing any verified strength.

## Core strengths to preserve
- **The D-scaled Gaussian likelihood with a learned scalar `σ²`.** It yields a parameter-free recon/KL balance and the correct `σ² = MSE` interpretation (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:448-464`). Any redesign must keep the per-element-mean convention and the `D` factor, or the balance changes by `T×F`.
- **The KL as a geometry-pinning regularizer.** It is the only term that prevents an arbitrary latent metric (ADV-04) and it prunes unused capacity (`strategy_philosophy.md:78`). Keep the regularizer; do not over-interpret the per-dimension KL as a factor count.
- **The span-based risk model.** Because `Σ_assets` depends only on `span(B)` (ADV-03), the loss need not identify factors. This is a powerful separation of representation learning from factor identification and should be made explicit in any redesign.
- **The ELBO discipline.** Excluding `γ` and `λ_co` from validation while including `σ²` is exactly right (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:622`). Keep the likelihood-based checkpoint criterion.
- **The shape/scale division of labor.** Per-window z-scoring, shape-only `B`, and downstream volatility rescaling form a coherent chain (ADV-10). Keep it; it lets the loss avoid modeling absolute scale.
- **The explicit relational term.** The document correctly diagnoses that reconstruction does not guarantee correlation preservation (ADV-05) and constructs a well-typed remedy (ADV-06). Keep the term; move it from a temporary scaffold to a permanent, low-weight part of the objective.
- **The crisis-weighting normalization.** `η_eff` is the right way to measure the effective crisis contribution (ADV-09). Keep it as a diagnostic, while recognizing that only the profile of `γ` is identified.

## Verdict
The unit is mathematically careful and honest about its central limitation. The Gaussian/`σ²` derivation, the `D` scaling, the ELBO discipline, the co-movement construction, and the span invariance are genuine strengths, and the document does not claim the guarantee it lacks. But the final objective is a per-stock reconstruction with no cross-sectional term, the only relational term is annealed to zero, the composite exposure is never optimized, and the AU count is a coordinate-dependent heuristic. The loss is aligned with the *local shape* of each window, not with the *cross-sectional covariance* the portfolio actually uses. The algebra is sound; the alignment is partial.

Score: 7/10
