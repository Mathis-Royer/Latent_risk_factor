## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** §4.6 exists to convert a set of *local* window encodings into one *global* exposure vector per stock, which the rest of the pipeline requires: "The stock's **composite risk profile** aggregates all local vectors across its full history" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:644-646`), producing "the exposure matrix B (n x K)" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:660-672`). The stated purpose is not statistical: it is the carrier of the strategy's founding differentiation — "a complete historical risk profile … A stock that was exposed to interbank contagion in 2008 retains that exposure in its composite profile" (`strategy_philosophy.md:54`). So the unit's intent is to estimate a *static* loading vector whose construction *also* encodes a *dynamic* claim about episodic risk. Those two intentions are in direct conflict (see CRI-01, CRI-03).

**2. What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** For a deep-learning conditional factor model on a non-CRSP EOD panel with delisted tickers, the object to estimate is not a lifetime average but a loading *function*: β_{i,t} = f(window representation, history context). Two reference designs: (i) Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2021), "Autoencoder asset pricing models", *Journal of Econometrics* 222(1) — a conditional autoencoder in which betas are outputs of a network applied to firm characteristics, i.e. loadings vary cross-sectionally and over time rather than being stock constants [unverified as to exact specification and results]; (ii) Krishnan, Shalit & Sontag (2017), "Structured Inference Networks for Nonlinear State Space Models", AAAI — a deep Kalman/structured inference network that infers a latent *state path* with a transition prior instead of independent per-window encodings [unverified]. For the aggregation step specifically, the correct machinery is permutation-invariant pooling over a set of window latents: Zaheer et al. (2017), "Deep Sets", NeurIPS (sum-decomposition pooling is universal for permutation-invariant functions) [unverified], and Ilse, Tomczak & Welling (2018), "Attention-based Deep Multiple Instance Learning", ICML (learned gated attention pooling over instances) [unverified]. The point that a *mean* is only a sufficient statistic for a constant parameter, and that a path requires a path-valued summary, is not a literature claim — it is the definition of sufficiency.

**3. What prevents it.** Three structural blockers, all evidenced below: (a) μ is *declared* to be β while the training objective is per-window reconstruction and the only cross-sectional term is annealed out (CRI-01); (b) the active-dimension gate measures encoder informativeness, not cross-sectional loading quality, and can certify a null column (CRI-02); (c) the theory needs a single date-invariant B, so every dynamic statement is converted into a static object and then asserted rather than established (CRI-03, CRI-04, CRI-08).

**4. Real constraints vs. self-imposed choices.** Real: the data lacks CRSP-grade survivorship handling, so history depth per stock is heterogeneous; inference must run per-stock without a fixed universe at inference time (the rejection of a cross-attention encoder is legitimate, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:542`); T = 504 is an architectural constraint (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:297`). Self-imposed: B must be one vector per stock; the aggregation must be a fixed scalar-weight average; AU must be `KL > 0.01`; the composite must be a lifetime statistic rather than an expanding/rolling one; the encoder's posterior variance is discarded at inference although it is used to select dimensions (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1642`); the "confidence weight" for short histories is a sentence, not a mechanism (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:674`).

**5. What should be removed, merged or moved.** *Removed:* the mean as the *definition* of exposure; §6.8 (learned attention) entirely; the claim that B "realizes the graph structure" and "memory of dormant exposures" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:672`); the sentence inventing a "confidence weight" (`:674`); stride-1 as a statistical justification (§4.6 points 1-2). *Merged:* §4.6, §6.2 and §6.8 into one decision — *is the exposure static or time-indexed?* — rather than three knobs on the same collapsed object. *Moved:* the AU gate from "filter the columns of B" to "constrain the factor covariance / rank of the loading", where its statistical meaning is defined; the dormant-risk claim from the exposure matrix to an explicit, separately tested estimand (regime-conditional covariance), where it could actually be true.

## Root-cause hypotheses

**RC-A — Static-attribute collapse (one lifetime vector per stock, because the downstream factor model demands a fixed B).** The OLS (`:748`), the covariance assembly (`:757`) and the entropy objective (`:769`) all require a date-invariant loading, so every time-indexed property is projected onto one vector. This single constraint generates CRI-01, CRI-03, CRI-04, CRI-05 and the futility of §6.2/§6.8: all three "aggregation alternatives" move weights around *within* the same collapsed object and cannot recover what the collapse discards.
- *A1. Deep state-space loadings.* The encoder becomes a structured inference network q(β_{i,1:T} | windows) with a persistence/regime prior; Σ_z is estimated from time-varying loadings; the composite disappears as an object. Trade-off: identification of a loading path from price alone is weak and needs an explicit prior (smoothness or regime persistence), the effective parameter count rises by T_hist/T, and the validation target changes from "portfolio on one vector" to "covariance forecast along a path".
- *A2. Regime-conditional static loadings.* B becomes R loadings per stock with an inferred regime index; the exposure at t is the loading of the active regime. Trade-off: R×AU parameters, regime identification is a known weak point, and the objective must be redefined over a mixture (or a worst-case) of regime covariances — a decision the theory currently avoids by choosing R = 1 implicitly.
- *A3. Keep B static, change what it is allowed to depend on.* Estimate loadings conditional on observable state variables (the Gu-Kelly-Xiu conditional-beta route) so that β_{i,t} is a deterministic function of information at t. Trade-off: reintroduces "a priori" conditioning variables the philosophy rejects (`strategy_philosophy.md:65`), but restores point-in-time validity at zero extra latent dimensions.

**RC-B — Proxy estimand (a cheap statistic from the VAE is used as if it estimated the object B needs).** The mean is chosen because it is the "default" of an aggregation function, and KL > 0.01 because it is already computed. Neither estimates what B requires: a rank-revealing, cross-sectionally informative, precision-homogeneous loading. Generates CRI-02, CRI-05, and the "confidence weight" gesture.
- *B1. Activity defined on the composite, not the encoder.* Column k is active if its cross-sectional dispersion of β̄_k exceeds its own bootstrap sampling variance by a stated margin. Trade-off: one more estimated quantity, but it measures the thing the OLS consumes.
- *B2. Conjunction gate.* Require both local KL (the dimension is used) and cross-sectional dispersion (it separates stocks). Trade-off: two thresholds, but the conjunction is far harder to satisfy spuriously than either alone.
- *B3. Drop the gate; put the rank constraint where it belongs.* An explicit low-rank or group-sparse prior on B_A with the rank chosen OOS. Trade-off: contradicts the "the model decides the number of factors" narrative (`strategy_philosophy.md:75-80`), but replaces an unvalidated heuristic with a selectable, testable one.

**RC-C — Property by assertion (narrative properties are attached to objects that cannot carry them, and validation only ever measures the portfolio).** "Memory of dormant exposures" (`:672`), "consistent meaning" (`:676`), "realizes the graph structure" (`:672`), "confidence weight" (`:674`) are claims about B with no estimand and no metric. Because every test is downstream (`:906-923`), no claim is isolable, so none can fail. Generates CRI-01's unfalsifiability, CRI-08, CRI-09.
- *C1. Property → estimand → threshold, before the portfolio layer.* Each claimed property gets a measurement (exposure recovery on synthetic data, regime-conditional exposure test, cross-retraining basis alignment) and an acceptance criterion that can fail.
- *C2. Delete the unmeasurable properties.* Restate the strategy as "a nonlinear static loading model with full-history factor covariance" — still deep, no longer claiming an anti-cyclical property it does not implement. Trade-off: loses the differentiator against PCA on paper, which is honest and forces the differentiator to be earned empirically.
- *C3. Split the pipeline into two gated layers* (representation quality, then risk-model quality) with a hard pass/fail between them. Trade-off: kills the end-to-end narrative and forbids compensating failures, but makes each layer's contribution identifiable.

## Verdict

The inference unit is where the strategy's central claim is supposed to become an object, and it does not. The composite is defined as a lifetime mean of local means (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:644-656`) and then credited with a property — memory of dormant exposures — that a mean cannot carry: it is sufficient only for a constant loading, and the document's own reasoning about volatility non-stationarity (`:724`) condemns it. The gate that certifies which columns of B are usable measures encoder informativeness rather than cross-sectional loading quality and, by its own algebra, can certify a null column and force the ridge the document explicitly rejects (`:714`, `:720`, `:759`). Nothing in the training objective makes μ a factor loading at all after the co-movement scaffolding is released (`:512`, `:540`), and no test in the protocol could detect the difference. The three "aggregation alternatives" (§6.2, §6.8) are weightings of the same collapsed object, ordered by implementation cost rather than by diagnosis, and one of them abandons the founding principle while being labelled an iteration. None of this argues for abandoning deep learning; it argues that the deep object being inferred should be the loading *path* or a *conditional* loading, with the static vector as a by-product rather than the estimand. As formalised, the unit is a narrative in search of a measurement.
