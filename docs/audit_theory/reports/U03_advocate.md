## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** The unit decides what the latent factors are *identified from*: `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:95` frames the question as "what data should the model consume?" and resolves it at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:136-137` as "**Price data is used for latent factor discovery.** / **Fundamental data is used only for *a posteriori* interpretation.**" The second half of the unit builds the precondition under which that discovery is valid: `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:261-265` ("An incorrectly constructed universe silently invalidates the backtest through **survivorship bias**"), the point-in-time principle at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:265`, and the separation of investment from training universe at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:281`. The intent is not "use prices"; it is "identify structure from the object that actually carries the risk, without injecting structure and without conditioning on survival."

**2. State of the art for this objective.** (a) *Identification*: the risk object is Cov(w'r), so the loadings must be identified from the joint law of r — a definitional requirement, not an empirical one. (b) *Conditioning without injection*: the modern answer to "fundamentals for interpretation" is to make characteristics a **conditioning variable on the loading network**, not an input channel — Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2021), "Autoencoder Asset Pricing Models", *Journal of Econometrics* 222(1), where β_t(x_i) is a neural function of characteristics and factors remain latent `[unverified]`; Kelly, Pruitt & Su (2019), "Characteristics are covariances", *JFE* 134(3), instruments loadings on characteristics in a linear model `[unverified]`; Lettau & Pelger (2020), "Factors That Fit the Time Series and Cross-Section", *RFS* `[unverified]`. (c) *Delisting*: correction conditional on delisting type — Beaver, McNichols & Price (2007), "Delisting returns and their effect on accounting-based market anomalies", *JAE* 43, `[unverified]`; Shumway (1997) as quoted at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1620`. (d) *Non-synchronous trading*: Lo & MacKinlay (1990), "An econometric analysis of nonsynchronous trading", *J. Econometrics* 45 `[unverified]`; Epps (1979), *JASA* 74 `[unverified]`. (e) *Universe*: index-style float/ADV/listing filters with hysteresis, as the corpus already cites (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:267`). The theory already reaches (e) and the principle of (c); it stops short of (b) and mis-specifies (d).

**3. What prevents reaching it — and which obstacles are only apparent.** Real obstacles: no CRSP; vendor coverage from ~2000 (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:577`); vendors restate corporate actions retroactively; delisting returns are generally unobservable outside CRSP. Only-apparent obstacle: the *frequency* objection to fundamentals (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:126`, "only 80 quarterly observations") is valid only against fundamentals as a **time-series input channel**; as a cross-sectional vector of ~1,000 stocks × 80 quarters it is a perfectly ordinary conditioning panel. The load-bearing objection is the *bias* one (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:130`), and that objection disappears if characteristics condition the network rather than being reconstructed. Second only-apparent obstacle: the claim that prices have "no publication delay or revision issues" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:104`) — adjusted prices *are* revised, see U03-ADV-04.

**4. Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: vendor depth, absence of as-of float, absence of delisting codes, closed-end universe of ~1,000 liquid names, no intraday history. Self-imposed: prices-only as *input* (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:136-137`); training universe defined as the union of *investment* universes (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:281`); the 504-day listing gate applied to training (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:273`); uniform Shumway constants with the conditioning variable deliberately deleted (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:605-606`); the cash sleeve on delisting (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:284`); 30-year depth as the stated baseline (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:979`) when the protocol needs 10 training + 3 holdout.

**5. Remove / merge / move.** *Remove*: the market-efficiency appeal (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:99`) — the identification argument does not need it and it is the weakest sentence in §3; the "no a priori bias" phrasing (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:17`, `:140`) — the price pipeline injects its own priors (504-day windows, translation invariance, a Spearman co-movement loss), so the correct claim is "no injected *economic* structure". *Move*: the 504-day listing gate from the training path to the investment path (it is a requirement on B, not on windows); fundamentals from the unwritten "interpretation" annex (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:137`) to a conditioning input, or delete the annex as unspecified. *Merge*: the three delisting code paths (return matrix imputation, `handle_delisting`, cash sleeve) into one return-adjustment object; the benchmark's data section with the DVT's, since they disagree on history length.

## Core strengths to preserve
1. **Prices as the identification object** (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:136-137`). Any redesign that feeds fundamentals into the encoder has changed the object being estimated; characteristics may condition loadings, never replace the return-identified factor.
2. **Point-in-time universe incl. delisted names, and training universe = union over the training period** (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:265`, `:281`). This is the single commitment that makes the anti-cyclical premise falsifiable rather than rhetorical. Keep the union; keep the "pre-delisting history" requirement.
3. **Infrastructure columns vs feature columns** (`implementation_decisions.md:106-112`). The distinction that resolved the volume dependency is conceptually right: what the pipeline needs to *select* and what the model needs to *see* are different objects and must be declared separately. A redesign will need more of this, not less.
4. **Causal hygiene of the feature pipeline** — per-window μ,σ with an explicit look-ahead comparison table (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:234-238`), the 21-day warm-up extension (`:252`), and the suspension/σ_min guards (`:242`). These are correct and cheap; they should survive any re-architecture verbatim.
5. **Data depth tied to parameter count** (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:422-436`). The idea that the factor count is bounded by the observations-per-parameter ratio — not by a modelling preference — is the correct form of a data requirement and should be generalised, whatever replaces the VAE.
6. **One shared data pipeline for strategy and benchmarks** (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:11`, `:21-34`). Without it the comparison measures pipelines, not risk models. It is currently violated only on the depth dimension (U03-ADV-10), not in principle.

## Verdict
This unit is the most disciplined part of the corpus. The identification argument is definitionally correct, the point-in-time discipline is unusually rigorous for a specification, and the decision to train on delisted names is a genuine structural insight rather than a checklist item — it is what allows the crisis-weighting premise to be tested at all. The defects are not in the principle but in the passage from principle to data object: the capacity guard's printed algebra is wrong by a factor of two while its table is right, the guard is evaluated at the wrong fold depth, the market-cap filter is built from a retroactively adjusted price and therefore violates the very point-in-time rule the unit is built on, and the delisting correction deletes the condition (delisting type) that makes Shumway's constants valid — in a large-cap universe where most disappearances are benign. The Epps justification is a bias/variance confusion, and the cash sleeve quietly breaks the fully-invested constraint during the periods that matter most. None of these requires abandoning a deep formulation; all are repair-able by redefining the data objects (as-of unadjusted price, type-conditional terminal returns, per-fold capacity, no cash sleeve), and the 504-day gate should be moved off the training path. Given the strength of the skeleton, the weaknesses are localized but they are precisely the ones that silently corrupt a backtest — hence the score.

Score: 7/10
