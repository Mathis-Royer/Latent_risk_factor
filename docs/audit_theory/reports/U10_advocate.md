## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** The unit converts the per-window encoder into a stock-level object. §4.6 defines a local vector $\mu_i^{(t)}\in\mathbb{R}^K$ and the composite $\bar\mu_i=\mathrm{Agg}(\mu_i^{(t_1)},\dots,\mu_i^{(t_M)})$ over the stock's full history (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:644-646`); the default is the simple mean, "all windows contribute equally, preserving memory of all historical regimes" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:656`). Stacking the composites gives B (n×K), later filtered to active dimensions, rescaled and rotated (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:660-672`). The intent is a shape-only, through-the-cycle loading matrix that preserves episodic exposures (`strategy_philosophy.md:44-56`), integrates new stocks without refitting (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:674`), and stays fixed between retrainings so factor dimensions have consistent meaning (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:676`). The ISD specifies a deterministic forward pass without sampling and a mean aggregation (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1594-1628`).

**2. State of the art.** A state-of-the-art version keeps the deterministic encoder but estimates a *conditional or distributional* loading, not only its unconditional mean. Three directions: (i) conditional loadings as neural functions of time-varying characteristics, as in Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2021), "Autoencoder asset pricing models", Journal of Econometrics, where the conditional autoencoder estimates latent factors and time-varying loadings jointly and nests PCA as a special case [unverified]; (ii) a learned aggregator with a declared estimand and rotation awareness, using the Deep Sets representation $\rho(\sum_t\phi(\mu_t))$ (Zaheer et al., 2017, "Deep Sets", NeurIPS; the mean is the identity case) or attention pooling (Ilse, Tomczak & Welling, 2018, "Attention-based Deep Multiple Instance Learning", ICML) [unverified]; (iii) an explicit regime variable — a mixture-of-experts or Gaussian-mixture VAE (Dilokthanakul et al., 2016, "Deep Unsupervised Clustering with Gaussian Mixture VAEs" [unverified]) — so that the crisis-state exposure is a parameter, not a frequency-weighted average. The defining property is that the estimated object is the one the risk model and the allocation actually need.

**3. What prevents the unit from reaching it.** Evidenced obstacles: (a) the mean is an unconditional, frequency-weighted average, while the anti-cyclical claim needs a conditional crisis exposure (U10-ADV-01); (b) the AU filter's average-KL criterion prunes rare dimensions (U10-ADV-06); (c) the posterior variance is discarded, so the second-stage OLS has no measurement-error correction (U10-ADV-04); (d) B rows are estimated on different stock histories, so the cross-section is not strictly comparable (U10-ADV-08); (e) the full-history Σ_z is fitted on the same data as B and the degrees-of-freedom count ignores the encoder (U10-ADV-07). Obstacles that are only apparent: the stride/calendar alignment is a quadrature choice, not a model choice (U10-ADV-02); rotation indeterminacy is harmless for linear aggregation because the mean commutes with the rotation (U10-ADV-03); the short-history reliability problem is handled conservatively by D_ε (U10-ADV-09); the "full history" wording is resolved by the fold protocol (inference windows are those available at the fold boundary, `ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1663`).

**4. Real versus self-imposed constraints.** Real: T = 504 is an architectural constraint (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:297`); per-window z-scoring is required for the shape-only property (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:724`); the active-dimension filter is required for conditioning (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:712-718`); a single latent basis is required to compute a full-history Σ_z (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:854-856`). Self-imposed: stride-1 as a "no degree of freedom" principle (the mean is stride-invariant up to quadrature, U10-ADV-02); the decision to keep only the mean and discard the within-stock distribution (U10-ADV-01); the decision to discard the posterior variance (U10-ADV-04); aggregating over each stock's own history rather than a common calendar window (U10-ADV-08); the EWMA half-life and attention hyperparameters, introduced as iterations without an identification argument (U10-ADV-10); the informal "confidence weight" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:674`), which duplicates D_ε and is never formalized (U10-ADV-09).

**5. What to remove, merge or move.** *Remove*: max-pool from the aggregation menu (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1037`) — not rotation-equivariant and not a stable statistic (U10-ADV-03); the "interpretable" claim for attention weights (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1171`) — unsupported (U10-ADV-10); the separate "diagnostic value" justification for stride-1 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:652`) — the trajectory's bandwidth is T, so it does not deliver daily resolution (U10-ADV-02). *Merge*: the aggregation function and the AU measurement into one window-weighting layer, because both are averages over the same windows (mean μ and mean KL) (U10-ADV-06); EWMA and attention into one "learned/weighted aggregation" family with a declared estimand (U10-ADV-10). *Move*: the memory guarantee from the encoder/retraining to an explicit stored trajectory plus a declared kernel (U10-ADV-01, U10-ADV-02); the dormant-risk representation from the mean to a distributional/regime summary (U10-ADV-01); the posterior variance from the encoder into the risk model as a measurement-error correction or diagnostic (U10-ADV-04); the definition of "full history" into the fold protocol, with a common calendar window or an explicit per-stock estimand (U10-ADV-08). *Can be removed without losing a strength*: stride-1 itself is not load-bearing for the mean (a stride of 5–21 preserves the linear result at a fraction of storage); the dense trajectory is load-bearing only if a nonlinear aggregator is actually evaluated; the "confidence weight" duplicates D_ε.

## Core strengths to preserve

- **Deterministic posterior-mean inference** (U10-ADV-04): B is a deterministic function of the data, with no Monte Carlo noise, so folds and aggregation variants are comparable. Any redesign should keep the deterministic encode and add the posterior variance as a diagnostic, not as a sampling step.
- **The stride-1 trajectory as a stored sufficient statistic** (U10-ADV-02, U10-ADV-12): it decouples the encoder from the aggregator and allows EWMA or other linear aggregators to be tested without retraining. This is the unit's main falsifiability asset.
- **The linear mean as the default** (U10-ADV-01, U10-ADV-03): it is the unique hyperparameter-free, rotation-equivariant, unbiased estimator of the unconditional loading. It should remain the baseline against which any learned aggregator is measured, even if the estimand is changed.
- **Exact shape/scale separation with dual rescaling** (U10-ADV-05): z-scoring makes the composite scale-free; date-specific rescaling for estimation and current rescaling for construction is the correct first-order correction, and P5/P95 winsorization protects the cross-sectional OLS.
- **Inductive, stock-agnostic encoding and conservative D_ε treatment of short histories** (U10-ADV-09): new stocks need no refitting, and missing history is converted into higher perceived risk with the correct sign.
- **Single-basis full-history recomputation and the rotation-invariant stability metric** (U10-ADV-07, U10-ADV-12): no splicing of incompatible latent bases; pairwise inter-stock distances are the right cross-retraining diagnostic.
- **The active-dimension filter propagated once per retraining** (U10-ADV-06): keep the mechanism for conditioning, but revisit the average-KL criterion, which is the weak link between B and the episodic-memory objective.

## Verdict

The inference layer is mechanically sound: deterministic encoding, a linear mean that commutes with filtering, per-row rescaling and rotation, an exact shape/scale separation, and an inductive treatment of new stocks. Those are real strengths and a redesign should keep them. The unit's weakness is not its arithmetic but its estimand: the mean is an unconditional, frequency-weighted average, while the philosophy's anti-cyclical guarantee requires a conditional crisis exposure that the mean systematically dilutes; the entropy rule then equalizes unconditional contributions, so a factor active in a fraction π of windows contributes c/π in the crisis state. The AU filter reinforces the dilution by pruning rare dimensions, the posterior variance is discarded, and B rows are estimated on different histories. The unit is therefore a good unconditional risk-model inference layer with an overstated claim. The redesign should keep the linear, equivariant core as the baseline, replace the memory claim with a distributional or regime-conditional object, and make the estimand explicit before any learned aggregator is compared.

Score: 6.5/10
