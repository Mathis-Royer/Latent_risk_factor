## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** The unit exists to deliver two things the methodology cannot deliver by itself: a compressed characterization the reader can hold (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:17`, "**Key properties:** no a priori bias in factor discovery, anti-cyclical by construction (no reactive selling after shocks), modular separation between risk model and return views, low turnover"), and a *pre-committed plan for being wrong* (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1086`, "alternatives should be applied iteratively in order of diagnostic priority"; `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1192`). The intent is epistemic, not promotional: the roadmap's stated function is to make the architecture's defeat observable rather than to defend it.

**2. What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** (a) *Specification tests, not single-model tests*: DeMiguel, Garlappi & Uppal (2009), *Optimal Versus Naive Diversification*, RFS 22(5), 1915–1953 — already the correct anchor, quoted at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1097`. (b) *Data-snooping-robust comparison across the whole model universe*: White (2000), "A Reality Check for Data Snooping", *Econometrica* 68(5) [unverified], and Hansen (2005), "A Test for Superior Predictive Ability", *JBES* 23(4) [unverified] — the right instrument when the roadmap itself contemplates 7 variants × 4 configs × 34 folds, because per-iteration paired p<0.05 accumulates. (c) *Selection-adjusted significance*: Bailey & López de Prado (2014), "The Deflated Sharpe Ratio", *JPM* 40(5) [unverified]. (d) *Perturbation theory for stability claims*: Davis & Kahan (1970), "The Rotation of Eigenvectors by a Perturbation. III", *SIAM J. Numer. Anal.* 7(1) [unverified] — the sin θ theorem gives the correct formal statement of "stable factors": eigenvector error ≤ ‖ΔΣ‖/gap. (e) *Conditional performance evaluation*: Ferson & Schadt (1996), "Measuring Fund Strategy and Performance in Changing Economic Conditions", *JF* 51(2) [unverified] — the standard way to test a claim that is *about regimes* using regime-conditional betas. (f) Honest inductive-bias framing: Locatello et al. (2019), already cited at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1656`.

**3. What prevents it — and which obstacles are only apparent.** Apparent: (i) "anti-cyclical cannot be formalized" — it can, and it nearly is (finding 02); (ii) "no a priori bias is naive" — the corpus already concedes its priors explicitly (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:404`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1153`) and the claim is scope-limited (finding 06); (iii) "the citations are unreliable" — the DVT bibliography `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1596–1665` is accurate in volume/issue/year; only the *reviews'* link apparatus is sloppy. Real: the go/no-go metrics are the optimizer's own objective (finding 08); the key-property labels cut across three subsystems that implement memory differently (finding 03); the roadmap has a failure exit but no success exit (finding 01, 09); the flagship mechanism has too few identifying events to be tested (finding 12).

**4. Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: retraining cadence of months (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:852`), the `AU_max_stat` estimation guard (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:718`), no-CRSP data, non-concavity of H (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:818`). Self-imposed: the decision to define the stock's risk profile as an *unweighted mean over the entire history* (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:184`) — which is what creates the need for the §6.2 patch later; the single-scalar K=200 with a 0.01-nat AU threshold (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:404`); the 30% turnover hard cap doubling as evidence for "low turnover" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:802`); and the choice to make "abandon the VAE" the *only* terminal state (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1192`).

**5. What to remove, merge or move.** *Remove*: `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1159–1167` as an admissible *outcome* (keep the paired test it defines as a diagnostic of whether nonlinearity pays); the "n ≥ 100" Tier-1 item (`literature_review_strategy.md:278`), vacuous at n=1000 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:978`) and contradicted by `literature_review_idiosyncratic_risk.md:411`; the numeric calibration ω≈0.20 (`literature_review_idiosyncratic_risk.md:283`) while keeping the qualitative goal. *Merge*: §6.2 and §6.3 into a single "memory kernel" decision with one shared half-life (finding 09); the two reviews' Tier lists into one decision register keyed to the AU filter and the OOS score; the six-metric "factor quality dashboard" (`literature_review_strategy.md:257–264`) into the existing three-layer metric set (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:906–923`) — it duplicates the AU filter and the stability metric. *Move*: the success criterion from `Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:293` into the roadmap, so both exits live in one place; and each "key property" from assertion into the metric that tests it — nothing is lost by this, because every one of the four already has a named metric or diagnostic.

## Verdict

This unit is the strongest part of the corpus as *epistemics* and the weakest as *framing*. As epistemics it does almost everything right: it pre-commits baselines, gives every alternative a named failure mode with a numeric trigger, states a defeat condition before seeing results, stratifies evaluation by regime, and — crucially — the two most quantitative claims in it survive checking. The anti-cyclical property is not rhetoric but a provable influence bound of 1/(M+1) on the composite, and the principal-basis rotation genuinely resolves the latent indeterminacy that would otherwise leave the diversification objective undefined. The sigma_z memo is faithful to the DVT it cites. The problems are of a different kind and they are structural rather than local: the four headline "key properties" are asserted as properties of *one* system while being implemented by *three* subsystems with incompatible notions of memory — expanding training, equal-weighted composites, full-history covariance, current-volatility rescaling, and γ=3 crisis upweighting all point in different temporal directions, so "anti-cyclical" is simultaneously true of B, true-but-diluted of Σ_z, and false of w. "Low turnover" is guaranteed only by a hard cap that would flatter any strategy, and "far more stable" is left as an adjective when the corpus elsewhere specifies exactly the metric that would settle it. The roadmap has a failure exit but no success exit, and its only terminal state is one the user decision excludes; its two top-ranked interventions would degrade the property it ranks first, and are really one decision about a memory kernel that should be taken once, for both layers, with the corpus's own argument at line 724 as the reason. The recommendation apparatus is not yet a decision register: the two reviews contradict each other on position count, one "calibration" conflates an objective weight with a variance share, and the go/no-go rule counts the optimizer's own objective twice among four primary metrics. None of these is fatal to a redesign — each is a labelling, merging or relocation problem, not a new mechanism. What must *not* be redesigned away is the discipline of the roadmap itself: the ordering, the triggers, the regime stratification and the defeat condition are the parts that make the deep learning bet falsifiable, and they are the reason this unit scores well despite its framing defects.
