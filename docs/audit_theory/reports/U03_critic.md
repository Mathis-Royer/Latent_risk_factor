## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** §3 poses the question literally — "Given these requirements … what data should the model consume?" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:93`) — and answers with a doctrine: prices for discovery, fundamentals only for post-hoc interpretation (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:136-137`; `strategy_philosophy.md:60-69`). §4.2 then specifies the measurement unit (per-window z-scored daily log-returns + 21-day realized vol), the universe (point-in-time, float-adjusted cap ≥ $500M, ADV ≥ $2M, 504-day listing, top-1000 by cap) and a vendor (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:261-291`). The intent is twofold and explicit: avoid injecting a priori structure, and avoid the survivorship bias that "silently invalidates the backtest" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:263`).

**2. A state-of-the-art version of this unit.** (i) **Separate estimation universe from investment universe** (MSCI Barra USE4 / Menchero, Orr & Wang 2011): exposures are estimated on a broad point-in-time listed set, and only the allocation step is restricted to liquid names; this is what prevents the selection rule from conditioning the estimated factor structure [unverified]. (ii) **Delisting returns conditional on delisting code**, not on exchange — Shumway (1997), *The Delisting Bias in CRSP Data*, JF 52(1), and Shumway & Warther (1999), JF 54(6), show the bias is concentrated in performance-related delistings, with merger delistings carrying positive returns [unverified]. (iii) **Systematic error screening of commercial return files** — Ince & Porter (2006), *Individual Equity Return Data from Thomson Datastream: Handle with Care!*, JFR 29(4) [unverified]; the corpus already owns this reference and does not apply it to its own vendor. (iv) **Explicit measurement-error / asynchronicity treatment** — Epps (1979) on correlation decay with sampling frequency [unverified]; the deep-learning analogue is a heteroskedastic state-space VAE (Krishnan, Shalit & Sontag 2017, *Structured Inference Networks for Nonlinear State Space Models* [unverified]; Fraccaro et al. 2017, KVAE [unverified]), in which observed returns are a noisy emission of a latent state and per-observation reliability is learned. (v) **A permanent entity key** (PERMNO/OpenFIGI) rather than a ticker, and a point-in-time share/float master [unverified]. (vi) **A pre-registered data-validity gate** with pass/fail statistics computed before any OOS number is admissible.

**3. What prevents the current unit from reaching it.** Not budget, not the vendor, but the absence of a measurement layer: CRI-01 (identification), CRI-02 (cap not constructible), CRI-03 (blanket delisting imputation), CRI-06 (no noise model), CRI-07 (N_obs misused). Add the selection rule CRI-04, the entity key CRI-05, the unstated history requirement CRI-08, the cross-document divergence CRI-09, and the absence of any acceptance criterion CRI-10.

**4. Real vs self-imposed constraints.** *Real*: no CRSP (user constraint), vendor history beginning ~2000, quarterly fundamentals, delisted series only obtainable from a vendor. *Self-imposed*: prices-only as a **principle** rather than a limit; top-n rank truncation and the $400M exit buffer; the unconditional −30%/−55%; discarding the delisting code; F = 2 as "the baseline" while `volume` is already loaded and unused (`implementation_decisions.md:106-108`); the 30-year premise retained rhetorically while 25 years is accepted operationally (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:577-581`); and the contradictory pair "504-day minimum listing" + "composite profile aggregates the stock's full history".

**5. What should be removed, merged or moved.** *Remove*: the price/fundamental dichotomy as a design principle (replace it with an explicit statement of the conditioning set and of what is identified); the top-n rank truncation and the 20% exit buffer; the unconditional −30/−55 imputation; the 30-year premise; the 20% zero-return tolerance; F = 2 as the baseline. *Merge*: universe construction, missing-data handling and delisting handling into one data object carrying a per-stock-day state (observed / stale / imputed / delisted), which enters both the loss as a weight and the risk model as a measurement variance. *Move*: market-cap construction from "derive from price" to a point-in-time fundamentals requirement; the data-validity gate from pipeline preconditions into the validation protocol as a reportable, pre-registered quantity; the delisting event out of the return matrix into a P&L event stream shared by model and portfolio.

## Root-cause hypotheses

**RC-1 — Data treated as plumbing, not measurement or identification.** One cause behind CRI-01, -03, -06, -10: the corpus reasons about *which columns* to load and never about *what the data can identify* or *how noisy the observation is*. Every downstream layer (co-movement supervision, Σ_z, D_ε, the entropy objective) then assumes a noise-free, identified B.
- *Alternative 1a — heteroskedastic measurement VAE*: observed return as a noisy emission of a latent factor state, with idiosyncratic variance D_{i,t} = g_θ(window shape, liquidity state) learned and amortized. What a linear model cannot do: make reliability a nonlinear functional of the window's shape, and propagate exposure uncertainty into Σ. Discriminating test: does D̂ beat GARCH(1,1) and a per-stock constant at predicting realized |residual| OOS, and does the reliability-weighted Σ reduce covariance forecast error on the same folds? Trade-off: adds a latent layer and an identification burden of its own.
- *Alternative 1b — multimodal encoder* (price window + ex-ante characteristics) to give dormant factors an identified channel; test on a held-out crisis: does the ex-ante branch predict realized co-movement better than a linear characteristics-based loading? If not, the honest conclusion is that the deep contribution lives only in the price branch.
- *Alternative 1c — narrow the claim*: state that only factors observed at least once can be hedged, and delete the "before it activates" language. Cheapest, and it is the claim `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:78` already makes.

**RC-2 — A selection rule disguised as an accounting convention.** CRI-02, -04, -08 (and part of -09): the universe is treated as a neutral bookkeeping boundary while it is in fact an economic filter that conditions the estimand.
- *Alternative 2a — two-universe architecture*: estimate exposures on a broad point-in-time listed set, restrict only the allocation step; removes rank conditioning at the cost of a much larger data requirement and a projection step.
- *Alternative 2b — smooth reliability weights* replacing hard cap/ADV gates, so that no stock is deleted but noisy ones are down-weighted; requires all benchmarks to adopt the same weights or the comparison breaks.
- *Alternative 2c — frozen universe per fold* (no rank churn between reconstitutions) as a diagnostic configuration, to measure how much of the OOS result is universe churn rather than risk modelling.

**RC-3 — No falsifiability contract at the data boundary.** CRI-05, -07, -10 (and -09): data requirements are written as a loading spec, never as pre-registered pass/fail properties, so every data defect surfaces as noise in a downstream metric rather than as a verdict.
- *Alternative 3a — a data-validity gate as a first-class layer of the protocol*, with a legitimate "not evaluable" outcome; cheap, but it can veto the whole campaign.
- *Alternative 3b — dual-vendor replication*: conclusions must survive two independent suppliers; the only test that separates a factor from a vendor artefact, at the price of a second subscription.
- *Alternative 3c — synthetic corpora with known factor structure* replacing the i.i.d. GBM fixture: known K, known delisting–performance dependence, known stale-price probability, with recovery tolerances. Trade-off: the generator becomes part of the theory and must itself be defended.

## Verdict

The unit is stronger on paper than most survivorship discussions — it names the bias, adopts a point-in-time universe, unions the training sample over delisted names, and names a vendor. But every load-bearing object it then constructs fails: the market cap cannot be built from the specified fields without contaminating it with future dividends (CRI-02), the delisting return is assigned unconditionally and contradicts the liquidation rule it is meant to feed (CRI-03), the entity key is a recycled ticker (CRI-05), the statistical guard does not reproduce its own table (CRI-07), and the central promise of diversification against dormant factors requires exactly the ex-ante data the document relegates to post-hoc interpretation (CRI-01). Throughout, data quality is treated as a geography problem (CRI-10) and the universe filter as a neutral convention (CRI-04), so the two channels that most determine what the VAE can discover are the two that are never analysed. This is a systemic defect of the data layer, not a list of local errors.
