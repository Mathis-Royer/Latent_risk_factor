## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent (cite the spec).** §4.8 states the intent in one sentence: "The validation protocol answers whether the strategy *actually* works without hindsight. Without it, all metrics are suspect" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:862`). The unit carries two distinct jobs: produce a *distribution* rather than a point estimate — "A single split yields one data point; walk-forward produces a **distribution** of OOS results across ~34 folds" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:866`) — and adjudicate whether the architecture earns its complexity against six benchmarks, each neutralising one stage of the chain (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:324`). The second job is the falsifiability backbone of the whole theory: "if … the VAE strategy does not statistically outperform the PCA factor risk parity baseline … the VAE is abandoned" (`strategy_philosophy.md:183`). So the unit is not bookkeeping; it is where the theory is allowed to die.

**2. What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** Four elements, none exotic:
- *Selection nested inside every fold*, so the reporting set is never the selection set. Cawley & Talbot (2010), "On over-fitting in model selection and subsequent selection bias in performance evaluation", JMLR 11:2079–2107: selection on the same data used for estimation produces an optimistic bias that is **not** removed by re-training the selected model — nested validation is the fix [unverified].
- *Purging by the feature/label horizon, not a fixed gap.* López de Prado (2018), *Advances in Financial Machine Learning*, ch. 7: purge training observations whose span overlaps the test span, then embargo after it; a fixed 21-day gap is not a substitute when the observation window is 504 days [unverified].
- *A max-statistic correction when many configurations are tried.* White (2000), "A Reality Check for Data Snooping", Econometrica 68(5):1097–1126, and Hansen, Lunde & Nason (2011), "The Model Confidence Set", Econometrica 79(2):453–497: the selected best must be compared with the null distribution of the *maximum*, not with a nominal paired test [unverified]; Bailey & López de Prado (2014) for the trial-count deflation [unverified].
- *Fold-level dependence acknowledged in the test.* Nadeau & Bengio (2003), "Inference for the Generalization Error", Machine Learning 52:239–281: CV variance is biased low because training sets overlap; a corrected variance is needed [unverified].

A SOTA version here is therefore the same 34-fold expanding schedule, plus (i) an inner purged block used only for HP selection, (ii) a purge of T−1 days at every split boundary, (iii) a written pre-registration of the configuration grid and of the holdout decision rule, (iv) seed/restart aggregation, and (v) a max-statistic test.

**3. What prevents reaching it — and which obstacles are only apparent.** The two real obstacles are structural and internal: the inner split is not purged for a 504-day window (U14-ADV-03), and the OOS plays both the selection and the reporting role (U14-ADV-04). Apparent obstacles: the absence of CRSP is *not* an obstacle to validation design — the DVT itself names delisted-inclusive vendors as alternatives (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:291`); compute is not an obstacle — the budget is already ~1,100 runs (`implementation_decisions.md:186`); and "only 34 folds" is not the problem — the folds are fine, it is what they are used *for*. A fourth obstacle is genuine but secondary: with 6-month non-overlapping OOS periods over 17 years, the number of independent macro episodes is in the single digits, so the "distribution across ~34 folds" is a distribution over paths, not over independent draws.

**4. Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: 30 years of daily data (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:882`), T = 504 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:998`), non-stationarity, a small number of crises. Self-imposed: the 2-year validation block being *simultaneously* the early-stopping set and adjacent to training with no purge (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:874`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:616`); the choice to compare configurations on OOS (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:929`); the explicit defence of the shared validation set (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:620`); the 21-day embargo used as a universal constant (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:876`); per-fold selection of 32 configurations including structural HPs (`implementation_decisions.md:186`); single-seed evaluation. None of these is forced by the data.

**5. What should be removed, merged or moved.**
- *Remove* the `−λ_est·max(0, 1−R_Σ)` term from the score: with `AU_max_stat = floor(sqrt(N_obs))` it can never fire (U14-ADV-07). Keep the guard; it is the real safeguard.
- *Remove* OOS from the configuration comparison: move the comparison to an inner purged block, and leave OOS for reporting only. Phase B survives (it is deployment-parity retraining); what disappears is "select best config by OOS score" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:894`).
- *Merge* the three EP thresholds (0.60 / 0.50 / 0.40) into one gate, and *move* EP into the selection score, where it is currently absent (U14-ADV-08).
- *Merge* the two uses of the 2-year block into one purged inner block; if 2 years cannot supply T-pure validation windows, lengthen it or drop early stopping in favour of a pre-registered fixed epoch — but do not keep a validation set that is ~99% in-sample.
- *Remove* the 20% training-loss sanity check as a validation criterion; it measures fit, not generalisation (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:900`).
- *Pre-register* the per-fold configuration grid and the crisis/calm labelling rule so that "34 folds reported individually" cannot be read post hoc.

1. **A single, frozen, single-use holdout, explicitly labelled "the only uncontaminated evaluation"** (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:880`), plus the explicit holdout-vs-walk-forward comparison to detect structural overfitting (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:283`). Any redesign must keep an untouched final block and a written rule that it is opened once.
2. **Point-in-time discipline applied to second-order objects.** The E* expanding median (`implementation_decisions.md:313-320`, `ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:2291-2292`) is the model of how the rest of the protocol should reason: it caught look-ahead in something no one would have checked. Generalise the same test to every derived quantity.
3. **Ex-ante metric hierarchy and per-fold reporting** (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:914-923`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:959`). This is what makes the result falsifiable rather than narratable; it must survive the redesign intact.
4. **"Complexity must earn its place" with PCA factor risk parity as the gate** (`strategy_philosophy.md:183-185`, `Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:321`). Under the deep-learning mandate this becomes the correct stopping criterion for the architecture search: if a redesigned deep model cannot beat linear factor extraction, the *learned object*, not the validation protocol, is what should change.
5. **Shared folds, universe, constraints and penalty parameters across all models** (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:48`, `Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:52-61`, `Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:267`) — this eliminates "the benchmark got its own tuning" as an explanation of any result.
6. **Causal feature construction** (per-window z-score, 21-day warm-up, expanding VIX percentile: `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:226-252`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:554`). Any redesign that changes the input representation must re-derive this argument, not inherit it.

## Verdict
From the advocate's side, this is a genuinely well-conceived validation design: the fold geometry is arithmetically exact and leak-free at the fold boundary, the train→OOS separation claim is correct, the causal feature construction is right, the entropy normalisation is correct mathematics, the holdout is properly isolated, and the protocol caught a look-ahead in E* that most designs would have shipped. It is also unusually candid about its own trade-offs (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:620`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:876`). Two conditions, however, are load-bearing and currently unmet: the inner 2-year split is not purged for a 504-day window, so early stopping and E* rest on a nearly in-sample signal; and the OOS period is simultaneously the HP-selection set and the reporting set, so the paired significance test its own decision rule depends on is not valid as specified. Both are repairable within the existing architecture — an inner purged block plus a max-statistic correction — and neither requires abandoning anything the unit is trying to do. The remaining issues (an inert R_Σ penalty, a dimension-monotone EP gate with three thresholds, a mis-calibrated crisis label, contradictory OOS rebalancing) are consistency defects rather than structural ones. This unit is the theory's strongest epistemic component and also the place where a single unexamined convention can invalidate the headline claim.
