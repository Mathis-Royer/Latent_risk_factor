## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** §4.7 must turn a scale-free, rotationally arbitrary latent geometry into a usable and estimable n×n covariance. The spec states the object (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:710`), the identification problem and its AU filter (`:712–718`), the missing-scale problem and the dual rescaling (`:724–744`), the factor-return estimator (`:746–750`), the factor and idiosyncratic covariances (`:752–753`), and the closure claim "All inter-stock correlation passes through the factors" (`:761`). The ISD states the same as one module objective (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1688`). Sub-intents: conditioning (`:718`), anti-cyclical covariance (`:752`), and a basis in which risk contributions are non-negative so entropy is defined (`:684–688`).

**2. State of the art for this objective.** Three things a contemporary version would do that this one only partly does. (i) *Target the right conditional object*: a factor risk model with time-varying loadings and a covariance that adapts to state — the DCC / conditional-correlation tradition (Engle 2002, "Dynamic Conditional Correlation", *JBES*; result: two-step GARCH-type estimation of a time-varying correlation matrix) [unverified] — or, in a deep formulation, have the encoder emit a state-dependent factor covariance (or a low-rank + diagonal scale) with the full-history matrix as long-run anchor. (ii) *Shrink toward structure, not sphericity*: analytical nonlinear shrinkage is rotation-equivariant and does not flatten the spectrum (Ledoit & Wolf 2020, "Analytical nonlinear shrinkage of large-dimensional covariance matrices", *Annals of Statistics*; result: the optimal rotation-equivariant nonlinear transformation of the sample eigenvalues, no identity target) [unverified; the corpus cites the paper at `sigma_z_ewma_justification.md:123` but not this property]. (iii) *Score the covariance with a robust loss*: only QLIKE and MSE are robust to noisy volatility proxies (Patton 2011, "Volatility forecast comparison using imperfect volatility proxies", *J. Econometrics*) [unverified] — relevant because the corpus's Layer-2 instrument is a level ratio (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:197`). For the cross-sectional estimator, Fama & MacBeth (1973, *JPE*) [unverified] and GLS/robust treatments of heteroskedastic residuals are the standard reference class.

**3. What prevents reaching it.** From my role, three obstacles are *only apparent*: latent non-identifiability (killed by GL-invariance, U11-ADV-05), ill-conditioning (converted into a checkable invariant by the κ guard, U11-ADV-04), and the unstated absolute scale of Σ (weight-neutral, U11-ADV-08). The real obstacles are conceptual, not numerical: the estimator's *target quantity* is asserted by a slogan rather than chosen (U11-ADV-07), the shrinkage prior silently reshapes the very eigenvalue spectrum the objective reads (U11-ADV-10), the systematic and idiosyncratic components are dated to different times (U11-ADV-09), and the risk model discards all state-dependence the encoder could supply — Σ_z is one static matrix per retraining, so nothing the network learns about *when* risk co-moves survives into the covariance.

**4. Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: n(n−1)/2 target parameters vs T observations forces a factor structure; serial correlation reduces the effective N; the process is non-stationary; the panel is unbalanced. Self-imposed: equal (exponential-free) weighting, the scaled-identity shrinkage target, one scalar volatility per stock per date, the 252-day window, diagonal D_ε, and a static Σ_z.

**5. What to remove, merge or move.** *Remove*: the second, wrong closed form for AU_max_stat (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:424`) — the table and the ISD already carry the correct one; the decorative DeMiguel "3000 months" figure (`sigma_z_ewma_justification.md:50`), which is a *mean*-estimation result and carries no weight here; and the "n/AU ≈ 12.5 ⇒ well-conditioned" sentence (`:718`), which is not a conditioning argument. *Merge*: the two rescaling equations into one operator with a date argument — this removes INV-004 as an entire class of possible error rather than policing it (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1792`); and §6.3's regime blending (`:1119–1121`) with the EWMA prohibition (`sigma_z_ewma_justification.md:109`) into a single statement of *which covariance is targeted*, since both documents are arguing about half-lives when the real decision is unconditional vs conditional. *Move*: the shrinkage out of the estimator and into the objective (or replace the identity target by a factor-structured one), so the variance and entropy terms see the same spectrum (U11-ADV-10); and the absolute risk level out of the exposure layer into an explicit calibration step, because two diagnostics depend on a constant the theory never pins (U11-ADV-08).

## Core strengths to preserve

1. **Shape × scale factorization with date-matched rescaling** (U11-ADV-01). Any redesign must keep the separation of "what the network can see" (scale-free shape) from "what must be supplied exogenously" (scale). It is the only honest way to turn a z-scored representation into a covariance.
2. **The parameter-count discipline** (U11-ADV-03): one quantity — AU(AU+1)/2 vs N — governs the truncation rule, the selection penalty and the shrinkage regime. Whatever replaces AU, a redesign must keep a single, stated observability budget rather than three detached thresholds.
3. **The objective-consistent evaluation harness** (U11-ADV-06): direct-vs-factorized estimation on identical information, scored on level, factor share, *and* structure. This is what makes the whole layer falsifiable, and it is the reason a linear baseline is a legitimate stopping criterion rather than a rhetorical one.
4. **GL-invariance plus principal-basis rotation** (U11-ADV-05). Preserve both halves: the risk model must never depend on latent coordinates, and entropy must only be computed on non-negative principal-factor contributions.
5. **The self-correcting treatment of misspecified D_ε** (`:753`) — reasoning from the objective's effect rather than from the estimator's bias. This style is the most transferable thing in the unit.

## Verdict

As an advocate I can defend the core of this unit. The rescaling layer is not an ad-hoc patch but a correct scale-restoration operator with a real, checkable weighting consequence; the parameter-count guard is internally coherent across three documents; the AU filter uses the VAE's own signal rather than working around it; and the risk model is provably invariant to the latent indeterminacy that would otherwise sink it. The statistical framing is, however, less secure than it presents itself: the choice of a full-history equal-weighted Σ_z is defended by a slogan about crisis memory when its real defence is that equal weighting is the premise of the Ledoit-Wolf formula the strategy relies on — and the same choice silently mixes in-sample projection error with sampling precision. Two things are genuinely fragile and both are repairable bookkeeping rather than architectural flaws: the systematic/idiosyncratic components are dated to different times, and the scaled-identity shrinkage prior compresses exactly the spectrum that the entropy objective reads, weakening both terms of the objective against factor concentration. None of this justifies leaving deep learning; it argues that the *conditional* structure the encoder is capable of producing is currently thrown away at the risk-model boundary. Fix the dating, retarget the shrinkage, and make the level explicit — do not replace the factorization.
