## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** The loss is explicitly what "determines what the latent space captures" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:440`): reconstruction of the window as the foundation (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:444`), KL regularization to prevent degenerate encodings and auto-prune unused dimensions (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:452`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:404`), learned scalar σ² to balance reconstruction against KL without a schedule (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:458-466`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:484`), co-movement scaffolding to align latent geometry with empirical correlation (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:496-504`), and crisis weighting to compensate for the underrepresentation of stress (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:546`). The intended output is a "pure risk model" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:680`), with the encoder as the exposure estimator (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:174`) and the decoder discarded after training (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:170`). The ISD calls this "the mathematical core of the VAE" (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1293`).

**2. What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** A task-aligned deep risk model, not a per-stock autoencoder:
- **Cross-sectional likelihood or pricing objective** instead of per-stock reconstruction. The corpus's closest reference is Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2021), "Autoencoder Asset Pricing Models," *Journal of Econometrics* [11], a conditional autoencoder with a no-arbitrage/pricing objective [unverified for details].
- **A permanent, calibrated relational objective** — the corpus's own InfoNCE proposal (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1149-1157`), or a contrastive objective (van den Oord et al., 2018, "Representation Learning with Contrastive Predictive Coding" [unverified]) — rather than a temporary linear scaffold.
- **A heavy-tailed observation model** (Student-t or scale mixture) so tails live in the likelihood, not in a post-hoc crisis weight.
- **A risk-relevant validation criterion** (OOS covariance forecast error or cross-sectional predictive likelihood; the corpus's own Layer 2 metrics at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:910`; Ledoit & Wolf (2004) [18] as the covariance benchmark), not the ELBO.
- **A rotation-invariant or identifiable latent basis.** The principal-factor rotation already resolves rotational indeterminacy (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:692`); true identifiability would require auxiliary variables (Khemakhem et al., 2020, "Variational Autoencoders and Nonlinear ICA: A Unifying Framework," AISTATS [unverified]).

**3. What prevents the current unit from reaching it.** The per-stock factorization (U07-CRI-01), the misspecified i.i.d. Gaussian/D-scaling (U07-CRI-02), the validation mismatch (U07-CRI-03), the unfalsifiable Phase 3 (U07-CRI-04), the hidden effective β (U07-CRI-05), the AU criterion (U07-CRI-06), the crisis-weight inconsistency (U07-CRI-07), the scale-blind loss (U07-CRI-08), the absence of model-level risk validation (U07-CRI-09), and the Gaussian likelihood (U07-CRI-10).

*Steelman and rebuttal.* Steelman: reconstruction is a rich self-supervised task; a stock's returns are largely common variation, so reconstructing them should force the encoder to encode common factors; the shared encoder is an inductive bias; the co-movement term is a warm start; the ELBO is a principled, assumption-light criterion. Rebuttal: per-window z-scoring removes the common level and leaves a shape that can be encoded in stock-specific directions; the document itself says the mechanism is "not guaranteed" and "theoretically impossible" without an inductive bias (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:496`); the co-movement term is inert at 0.5 and removed; the ELBO assumes conditional independence across 1008 elements, which the document's own effective-sample-size remark contradicts (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1076`); and the one test that would settle it — lower ELBO implies better Σ_z/portfolio — is not in the protocol (U07-CRI-09).

**4. Real versus self-imposed constraints.** *Real:* stay deep learning (user decision); no return forecast by default (`strategy_philosophy.md:9`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:680`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:776`); variable universe (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:542`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:307`); non-CRSP data with delisted tickers (briefing 9); compute budget (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:598`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:614`). *Self-imposed:* per-stock encoder and per-stock reconstruction (the cross-attention alternative is rejected at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:542`, but a masked set encoder can handle a variable universe); Gaussian scalar-σ² likelihood (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:458`); D=T×F scaling (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:462`); 0.01-nat AU threshold (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:404`); λ_co=0.5 and the 30/30/40 curriculum (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:514`); β=1 fixed and the three modes (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:478-492`); mean aggregation (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:656`, alternatives at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1037`); K=200 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:420`); validation ELBO (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:616-622`); 252-day vol rescaling (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:724-734`); T=504 (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:301`, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:998`).

**5. What should be removed, merged or moved.**
- **Remove** Mode A and its disentanglement rationale (U07-CRI-11); the co-movement curriculum as specified (U07-CRI-01) — either rescale λ_co to the KL scale and make it permanent, or replace it with a cross-sectional objective; the "automatic factor count" claim and the 0.01-nat AU threshold as the bridge to the risk model (U07-CRI-06); the validation ELBO as the stopping criterion (U07-CRI-03); the crisis weighting as a reconstruction weight (U07-CRI-07, U07-CRI-10).
- **Merge** σ² and γ into one observation model (per-window/per-feature variance or a heavy-tailed likelihood); the three modes into one parametrization with an explicit effective β; the AU filter into a risk-contribution filter; the Layer 1/2 diagnostics into one risk-relevant validation.
- **Move** the cross-sectional alignment from an auxiliary loss to the primary objective (a deep factor model with a cross-sectional likelihood); the factor-count decision from the KL to the risk model (a penalized likelihood on Σ_z); the scale from the downstream 252-day vol estimate into the learned object (a scale-aware input or a two-head model).

## Root-cause hypotheses

**RC1 — Per-stock generative architecture versus cross-sectional target.** The unit inherits a per-stock VAE, so the loss must factorize, while the target is a cross-sectional dependence object. This generates U07-CRI-01, U07-CRI-06, U07-CRI-08, U07-CRI-09. Structural alternatives:
- **(a) Cross-sectional/set encoder with masking and a cross-sectional likelihood.** The loss becomes the risk object and the variable universe is handled by masking. Trade-off: each date is one high-dimensional observation, so the effective sample size is the number of dates, and masking must be strictly point-in-time.
- **(b) Two-stage deep factor model.** Stage 1 learns exposures (unsupervised or contrastive); stage 2 jointly estimates exposures and Σ_z by maximizing the cross-sectional Gaussian likelihood (or a penalized version). Trade-off: the second stage is the risk objective, but stage 1 remains a proxy unless trained end-to-end, and the joint problem is non-convex.
- **(c) Keep the per-stock encoder but add a permanent, calibrated covariance-matching term on the implied asset covariance.** Trade-off: minimal architectural change; but the per-stock bottleneck and factorized reconstruction remain, and the term's scale must be calibrated (the current L_co failure shows what happens when it is not).

**RC2 — The ELBO treated as a universal quality criterion.** The unit conflates density fit of z-scored windows with risk-model accuracy. This generates U07-CRI-02, U07-CRI-03, U07-CRI-05, U07-CRI-09. Structural alternatives:
- **(a) Risk-relevant validation.** Hold out a block, estimate Σ_z, and score the covariance forecast error or the cross-sectional predictive likelihood. Trade-off: directly aligned; but noisy, expensive, and needs a second estimation step.
- **(b) Multi-objective stopping rule** with explicit weights (profile ELBO + covariance error + latent stability). Trade-off: transparent; but more hyperparameters and a risk of gaming the weights.
- **(c) Profile the likelihood on validation** (σ²_val=m_val) and use it only as a diagnostic, with the risk criterion as the stopping rule. Trade-off: statistically coherent; but still a density criterion, not a risk criterion.

**RC3 — Convenience normalizations strip risk-relevant information.** Per-window z-scoring, scalar σ², and the Gaussian likelihood are chosen for stability, but they remove the scale and tail information the risk model needs. This generates U07-CRI-02, U07-CRI-07, U07-CRI-08, U07-CRI-10. Structural alternatives:
