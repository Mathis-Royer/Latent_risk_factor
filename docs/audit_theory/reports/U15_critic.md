## Five questions

1. **Purpose and original intent.** `Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:5` states the guiding principle: "la complexité du VAE n'est justifiée que si elle produit un bénéfice OOS mesurable et statistiquement significatif par rapport à des alternatives plus simples, sur les mêmes données, avec les mêmes contraintes, évaluées par les mêmes métriques." `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1097` draws the operational consequence: "If the VAE strategy does not surpass these benchmarks, its additional complexity is not justified." The unit is the theory's only falsification device for the complexity claim: a controlled horse race followed by a pre-committed decision grid (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:287-311`).

2. **State of the art.** A state-of-the-art version would have: a pre-registered primary endpoint and a single primary comparator, with everything else secondary (Arnott, Harvey & Markowitz 2019, "A Backtesting Protocol in the Era of Machine Learning" [unverified]); a factorial design over the two things the claim actually varies — factor extractor and portfolio objective — plus naive and market reference points; equal *selection* budgets under nested tuning, not identical constants (Gu, Kelly & Xiu 2020, "Empirical Asset Pricing via Machine Learning", RFS [unverified], is the convention for ML-vs-linear comparisons); model-free asset-space metrics — effective number of bets (Meucci 2009, "Managing Diversification" [unverified]) and diversification ratio (Choueifaty & Coignard 2008, "Toward Maximum Diversification", JPM [unverified]) — rather than a model-internal entropy; factor-spanning regressions against market/size/value/momentum to rule out a beta repackaging (Fama & French 2015 [unverified]); net-of-cost returns under a common cost model; an equivalence margin and a power/MDE calculation (TOST, Schuirmann 1987 [unverified]); dependence-robust inference and a deflated threshold for the number of configurations tried (Bailey & López de Prado 2014, "The Deflated Sharpe Ratio" [unverified]); a research-process alpha budget across the Iteration 1–5 roadmap; and a competitor set containing the strongest linear methods: nonlinear shrinkage (Ledoit & Wolf 2020, "Analytical nonlinear shrinkage", Annals of Statistics [unverified]), POET (Fan, Liao & Mincheva 2013 [unverified]), HRP (López de Prado 2016, "Building Diversified Portfolios that Outperform Out of Sample" [unverified]), MDP (Choueifaty & Coignard 2008 [unverified]) and Roncalli-Weisang factor risk budgeting (2016, "Risk Parity Portfolios with Risk Factors", Quantitative Finance [unverified]). None of this requires abandoning the deep model; it requires a comparison capable of saying no.

3. **What prevents it.** CRI-01 (the VAE is selected on the test folds), CRI-02 (the primary metric is model-internal and undefined for 2/6 benchmarks), CRI-03 (non-rejection is converted into an architectural verdict), CRI-04 (the ablation is not factorial), CRI-05 (no market/beta control), CRI-06 (identical constants mistaken for equal tuning), CRI-08 (the strongest linear alternatives are missing), CRI-09 (the one feature-isolation benchmark is uncomputable), CRI-10 (CRSP-grade data under a no-CRSP constraint). *Steelman:* the unit is unusually disciplined for a theory document — it fixes the universe (`Benchmark_latent_risk_factor.md:23-34`), fold dates (52-61), constraints (38-46), solver (142), metrics (164-199), and pre-commits to adopting PCA if the VAE does not win (299-301); refusing to re-tune benchmarks (48) is a genuine anti-overfitting instinct. *Rebuttal:* holding constants equal is not the same as holding conditions equal. What is held equal (penalties, cadence, metric) is the strategy's own calibration; what is left unequal (selection budget, factor count, regularization, factor-selection rule) is what determines the outcome. A controlled experiment equalizes information and selection conditions, not constants.

4. **Real vs self-imposed.** Real: ~30 years and one equity universe; long-only, fully invested, 5% cap; no CRSP; VAE retraining cost per fold. Self-imposed: (i) using the strategy's entropy objective for the benchmark instead of also testing the VAE covariance under min-variance; (ii) fixing φ, κ₁, κ₂ at the VAE's values; (iii) making latent entropy the primary metric; (iv) deciding on walk-forward folds and leaving the holdout as a sanity check; (v) exempting 1/N from turnover; (vi) treating six benchmarks as the relevant alternative space; (vii) defining "same infrastructure" as "same code path" rather than "same information set and selection budget."

5. **What should be removed, merged or moved.** Remove: Phase A's OOS-based selection (CRI-01); the composite score as the benchmark decision statistic (CRI-02); the five-scenario grid (CRI-03); the 1/N turnover exemption (CRI-07); the "identical constants" rule (CRI-06). Merge: the six benchmarks into a 2×2 factorial {extractor} × {objective} plus reference points (CRI-04); the DVT's four-benchmark list into the six-benchmark list (CRI-12); a crisis-weighted linear analogue into the extractor comparison (CRI-04). Move: PCA+vol out of the benchmark set into the feature-ablation set, or specify it as a multi-view model (CRI-09); the holdout from sanity check to decision sample (CRI-01/03); the cost model from an optimizer penalty to a realized net-return calculation (CRI-07). Do not add benchmarks: the set already exceeds the power available.

## Root-cause hypotheses

**RC1 — Certification by accumulation, with no theory of what would count as evidence.** The unit adds benchmarks, metrics and tests, but never a single decisive endpoint; the five-scenario grid is a set of narratives rather than a pre-specified test. Symptoms: CRI-02, CRI-03, CRI-04, CRI-05, CRI-08, CRI-12. Structural alternatives: (a) a pre-registered single-endpoint design — one primary metric, one primary comparator, one threshold; trade-off: loses diagnostic richness, gains a valid test; (b) two-arm factorial — extractor arm (same objective, PCA vs VAE) and objective arm (same extractor, min-var vs entropy); trade-off: more cells, but each is interpretable; (c) adversarial benchmark — commission the strongest linear competitor (POET/HRP/MDP) as the sole hurdle; trade-off: less control over specification, but removes the weak-baseline objection.

**RC2 — The theory uses its own coordinate system as the referee.** Latent entropy is both the objective and the primary metric; the benchmark inherits it. Symptoms: CRI-02, CRI-05, CRI-11. Alternatives: (a) model-free primary (asset-space ENB or DR), factor entropy as diagnostic; trade-off: loses the factor-level story the theory cares about; (b) a common external factor basis for every model's entropy; trade-off: favours linear models by construction, but is a neutral referee; (c) require agreement between a model-free and a model-internal metric before any architectural conclusion; trade-off: harder to pass, more "inconclusive" verdicts.

**RC3 — Leakage is defined as date overlap, not as information flow.** INV-005 and `test_fold_no_overlap` check dates; Phase A selects on the outer OOS; the iteration roadmap conditions on OOS results. Symptoms: CRI-01, CRI-06. Alternatives: (a) nested selection inside the training window, outer OOS untouched; trade-off: much more compute, and the VAE's advantage may shrink; (b) three-way split — design / validation / test — with the test touched once; trade-off: fewer test folds, less power; (c) freeze the configuration a priori and delete Phase A; trade-off: may under-sell the VAE, but the comparison becomes valid.

**RC4 — The strategy's cost/constraint model is imported into the comparison.** Penalties calibrated for the VAE become the benchmarks' objective, gross returns are the currency, and the data fields are the strategy's. Symptoms: CRI-07, CRI-10. Alternatives: (a) evaluate net of a common realized cost model and drop the penalties from the comparison; trade-off: noisier, but it is the quantity investors receive; (b) give each model the same calibration protocol and budget; trade-off: more compute, but removes the fit-to-VAE objection; (c) separate risk-model quality from portfolio-construction quality by fixing the construction across extractors; trade-off: less realistic, but isolates the covariance forecast.

## Verdict

The unit has the right instinct — a common universe, common fold dates, common solver, a pre-committed decision grid — and is more explicit than most theory documents.
