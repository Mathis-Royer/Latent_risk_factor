## Five questions

1. **Purpose and original intent.** The unit exists to supply the one thing the per-sample objective cannot: cross-sectional dependence. The corpus states that reconstruction is "a per-sample MSE: each stock is reconstructed independently" and that the shared encoder's tendency toward similar latents for similar stocks is "**not guaranteed** to preserve empirical correlation structure" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:496`), so a co-movement loss "explicitly guides the latent space to reflect pairwise dependence structure" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:496`). Crisis weighting exists for a different stated reason: stress is "~2–5% of historical data, so the gradient is dominated by normal-regime patterns," and γ "incentiviz[es] the encoder to allocate capacity to tail co-movements" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:546`). The intent is therefore twofold: (i) make latent geometry carry covariance information, (ii) make that geometry asymmetric in favor of crises.

2. **What a SOTA version would look like.** The correct object for a *risk* model is the conditional covariance operator, not the geometry of exposure vectors. A SOTA version would (a) parameterize the cross-section jointly and fit it with a proper scoring rule; (b) use a dependence measure without a prescribed functional form; (c) treat crisis as a latent regime variable, not a sample weight; (d) use a latent geometry consistent with the distance used. Relevant anchors: Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2021), *Autoencoder Asset Pricing Models*, J. Econometrics — joint estimation of loadings and factor returns under one objective rather than two stages [unverified]; Engle (2002), *Dynamic Conditional Correlation* — the dependence object is Σ_t, estimated conditionally [unverified]; Hamilton (1989) — regime as a latent state [unverified]; Gretton, Bousquet, Smola & Schölkopf (2005), *Measuring Statistical Dependence with Hilbert–Schmidt Norms* — HSIC is zero iff independence, no functional form imposed [unverified]; Davidson, Falorsi, De Cao & Kipf (2018), *Hyperspherical Variational Auto-Encoders* — if the metric is cosine, the latent must be spherical (von Mises–Fisher), not Gaussian [unverified]; Hyvärinen & Morioka (2016), *Unsupervised Feature Extraction by Time-Contrastive Learning and Nonlinear ICA* — identifiability from time structure rather than from correlation labels [unverified]; Oord, Li & Vinyals (2018), *Contrastive Predictive Coding*, and Poole et al. (2019), *On Variational Bounds of Mutual Information* — the InfoNCE bound loosens as the negative pool shrinks [unverified]. A defensible test set: OOS negative log-likelihood of cross-sectional returns plus minimum-variance variance ratio against PCA/Ledoit–Wolf, and a *tail-specific* test (joint lower-tail exceedance rates) that a Gaussian copula cannot pass.

3. **What prevents it.** The encoder never sees the object the portfolio uses: B is trained on reconstruction, and Σ_z, f_t and D_ε are all computed afterwards by OLS and Ledoit–Wolf (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:710`). The only term that could connect the two is a pairwise distance target whose fitted fixed point (cos_sim = ρ) is inconsistent with that same decomposition (CRI-01). Secondary blockers: the crisis lever is attached to a term with no cross-stock coupling (CRI-02); the dependence family is elliptical (CRI-03); the comparison against PCA is confounded by supervision (CRI-05).

4. **Real vs self-imposed constraints.** *Real*: rotational indeterminacy of the latent (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:692`); per-window z-scoring erases volatility level (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:240`); a per-stock encoder cannot see other stocks *if* it is truly per-stock. *Self-imposed*: (a) that auxiliary dependence must be a **penalty added to a VAE ELBO** rather than a likelihood on a dependence object; (b) that the dependence target must be **pairwise correlation** rather than a copula/KDE/HSIC functional; (c) that crisis must be a **threshold on one market-wide index** rather than a latent regime; (d) that the term must be **annealed to zero** (a curriculum choice with no stated mechanism); (e) that D_ε must be diagonal and Σ_z unconditional while the scaffold targets their product (CRI-01). The rejection of a cross-sectional encoder (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:542`) conflates "fixed universe" with "cross-sectional model": permutation-invariant set encoders (Deep Sets / attention over a variable-size set) do not require a fixed universe — this is a design choice presented as a constraint.

5. **What should be removed, merged or moved.** *Remove*: γ on the reconstruction term (it cannot reach co-movement — CRI-02); the Phase-3 "release" as currently specified (no mechanism, no measurement — CRI-04); the "Spearman vs raw returns" precaution (vacuous — CRI-10); the InfoNCE roadmap item as specified (CRI-07). *Merge*: crisis weighting and §6.3 regime-aware Σ_z blending (both are regime conditioning, split across two layers). *Move*: dependence from the encoder's latent geometry into an explicit conditional dependence object — a network that outputs Σ_z,t or a copula/regime parameter, fitted by likelihood on factor returns — leaving B's rows to satisfy the OLS factor structure rather than a geometric target that contradicts it. *Move*: the k-means stratification from batching into the feature/regime layer — the philosophy forbids a priori categories (`implementation_decisions.md:293`) yet the sampler reintroduces one, silently reweighting the objective (CRI-09).

## Root-cause hypotheses

**RC-1 — Object confusion: the loss supervises an exposure geometry while the criterion scores a covariance operator.** Explains CRI-01, -03, -05, and the absence of any Σ_z- or f_t-dependent term in §4.4.
- *Alternative A (likelihood on the dependence object):* keep the per-stock encoder for B, add a second head/network emitting Σ_z,t (or a low-rank+diag + regime parameter) fitted by Gaussian/Student-t likelihood on realized factor returns. Trade-off: well-posed and scoreable, but re-opens the AU statistical guard and reintroduces a parametric dependence family (mitigable with a copula or score-based estimator).
- *Alternative B (joint cross-sectional model):* a permutation-invariant set encoder over the cross-section producing per-date loadings and a factor-return map under one likelihood. Trade-off: the only formulation where the deep model can express something PCA cannot (state-dependent, non-Gaussian dependence), but it costs the "universe flexibility" argument, which the corpus overstates (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:542`).
- *Alternative C (separation):* declare B purely a shape embedding, delete all geometric dependence terms, and place *all* dependence in an explicit conditional model. Trade-off: honest and minimal, but then the VAE's only claim on the risk model is the quality of B as a feature map — a much weaker claim that must be defended on its own.

**RC-2 — Auxiliary-term inflation: every failure is answered by a weighted penalty with a heuristic constant rather than by re-specifying the estimand.** Explains CRI-02, -04, -08, -09, -11; recurring across the loss (β, σ², γ, λ_co) and cross-unit (U07, U18).
- *Alternative A:* one likelihood per object (per-window shape; cross-sectional dependence), no cross-term weights — every constant becomes a parameter with a meaning.
- *Alternative B:* treat γ as **importance weighting with a declared target distribution**, so the strategy must state which distribution it wants the risk model for (unconditional vs. crisis-tilted). Trade-off: converts an arbitrary knob into a testable choice, at the cost of admitting the model is regime-specific.
- *Alternative C:* make every auxiliary term a **validated predictor** with its own OOS target and ablation, so a term that cannot move its target is deleted rather than tuned.

**RC-3 — Training-path artifacts mistaken for structure.** Explains CRI-04, -06, and the curriculum's unfalsifiability.
- *Alternative A:* define the end-of-training property as a measurable statistic and make it the stopping rule (§4.4's stopping rule currently optimizes a different objective).
- *Alternative B:* drop the curriculum; average over basins with restarts/ensembling — the effect the curriculum claims (escaping a poor initial geometry) without the unverifiable "release" narrative.
- *Alternative C:* initialize from a linear solution (legitimate: PCA as initialization, not as model) and fine-tune. Trade-off: fast and stable, but risks anchoring the deep model on the linear structure it is supposed to transcend — the very failure mode of CRI-03.

## Verdict

The unit is meticulously specified and internally documented, but its central mechanism does not survive inspection. The co-movement scaffold's fixed point (cos = ρ) contradicts the strategy's own covariance decomposition unless factor variances are equal and residual variances are homoskedastic — so the term imposes *more* linear structure than the PCA benchmark it is meant to beat, and it does so on the wrong object (exposure geometry instead of the covariance operator). Crisis weighting cannot reach co-movement at all: γ multiplies a per-stock, per-window reconstruction term with no cross-stock coupling, and because VIX is market-wide it is a pure temporal tilt — which invalidates the asymmetry claim used elsewhere to justify skipping downside risk. The "release to non-linear dependence" is unsupported twice over: Phase 3 contains no cross-stock term, and early stopping selects checkpoints against an ELBO that excludes the scaffold. The comparison with PCA is confounded by supervision on the benchmark's own target, with no λ_co=0 ablation. The InfoNCE alternative re-encodes the same statistic and adds a block-identification degeneracy. What remains salvageable is the *diagnosis* — the corpus correctly identifies that a per-sample objective cannot carry cross-sectional dependence — and the discipline of documenting invariants. The remedy is architectural, not parametric: move dependence to an explicitly conditional object fitted by likelihood, and delete γ and the geometric scaffold. Score reflects that the load-bearing mechanism fails and the evaluation cannot detect the failure.

Score: 3/10
