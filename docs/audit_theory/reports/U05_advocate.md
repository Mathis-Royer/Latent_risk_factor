## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** The unit must turn a stock's own price history into an exposure vector $\beta_i\in\mathbb{R}^K$ to latent risk factors, with the *number* of factors discovered rather than chosen: the model is stated as $r_{i,t}=f(z_{\cdot,t},\beta_{i,\cdot})+\varepsilon_{i,t}$ with $f$ "potentially non-linear", and the corpus claims the estimation "simultaneously estimates the K latent factors, each stock's exposures, and the dependence structure" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:154-158`). The VAE is chosen because it maps an input to a *distribution*, which supposedly forces a continuous, gap-free latent space so that "distances between stocks are therefore meaningful and comparable" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:162-164`); the decoder is training scaffolding only, and the encoder's mean "are the estimated exposures" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:170-174`). The explicit secondary intent is amortization: one function processes any stock, so "a new stock can be processed without retraining" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:307`), and the factor count is read off the KL rather than set (`strategy_philosophy.md:73-82`).

**2. What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** For this objective — a *risk* object, not a return forecast — the SoTA formulation keeps the loading map nonlinear but makes the factor a **shared cross-sectional latent**: $r_t = B_\theta(\text{window}_t)\,z_t+\varepsilon_t$, with $z_t \sim \mathcal N(0,\Sigma_z)$, trained by maximizing a panel likelihood/ELBO over $n\times T$, so that training and the risk model estimate the same object. This is the class of Kelly, Pruitt & Su (2019), "Characteristics are Covariances", *JFE* (instrumented PCA: loadings are functions of observables, factors common) and Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2021), "Autoencoder Asset Pricing Models", *J. Econometrics* (loadings are neural functions of characteristics, factors estimated jointly, no-arbitrage restriction) `[unverified]`. The corpus's own references point the same way: [30] is described as "a probabilistic dynamic factor model" and [31] as "a dynamic factor model ... for dynamic factor extraction" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1636-1638`), and Sicks, Korn & Schwaar (2023), "Estimating the Value-at-Risk by Temporal VAE", *Risks* 11(5):79, is used only for auto-pruning (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:1628`) `[unverified]`. The genuinely deep-only increment for *this* strategy is not a bigger encoder but a **state-conditional second moment** — $\Sigma_z$ or $D_\varepsilon$ as a learned function of the factor state — because that is what linear factor models cannot represent and what episodic risk requires (`strategy_philosophy.md:50-54`). The falsifiable test is clean: hold $B$ fixed, compare constant-$\Sigma_z$ against a conditional $\Sigma_z$ on out-of-sample covariance error (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:910`).

**3. What prevents it.** Three evidenced obstacles, and two that are only apparent. Real: (i) the loss never sees the cross-section — reconstruction is per-window MSE (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:444-450`), and the document itself concedes this is "not guaranteed to preserve empirical correlation structure" (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:496`), with the co-movement term introduced as the patch and then scheduled to zero (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:508-512`); (ii) $K$ is read out post hoc by a KL threshold (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:404`), so the factor count is a training artifact rather than a model parameter; (iii) the non-linearity is confined to the map window→price-of-risk, while the factor and its distribution stay linear-Gaussian (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:710-753`). Only apparent: the "capacity-data constraint" is presented as the binding limit (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:364-372`) but the real limit is the KL rate (ADV-04, ADV-09); and universe flexibility is invoked to reject cross-attention (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:542`) — a valid rejection of that *encoder*, but not of a cross-sectional *loss*, since a per-stock loading function can be trained on each date's panel and still applied row-by-row at inference (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:674`).

**4. Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: ~1,000 stocks, 30 years of daily data, no CRSP (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:291`), so inference must be amortized and per-window volumes are needed to train anything deep; per-date cross-sections of ~1,000 rows bound $\mathrm{AU}$ through the statistical guard (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:422-436`). Self-imposed: reconstruction as the *only* generative objective; discarding the decoder so the generative model is never used as a model (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:170`); two input features, which keeps the input tensor small but forces scale entirely outside the network; $K=200$ as a hard ceiling resolved by a 0.01-nat threshold instead of a low-rank prior on the loading columns; and GAP, which deliberately drops within-window timing (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:971`).

**5. Remove, merge, move.** Remove: the claim that the model estimates $f$ jointly (ADV-10) — the honest object is a neural loading map in front of a linear factor model; the co-movement scaffold (ADV-07), which is a proxy objective that becomes unnecessary once the panel likelihood exists; the 0.01-nat AU definition as a *definition* (keep it as a diagnostic alongside the Bai–Ng floor, `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:416`); and the $r_{\max}=5$ wording as a statistical guarantee (ADV-09) — keep the ratio as a compute gate. Merge: the two scale mechanisms (per-window z-scoring and $\sigma_{i,t}/\bar\sigma_t$) into one stated contract — "the network learns shape, the risk model owns scale" (ADV-06) — so the boundary is not re-litigated per section. Move: the cross-sectional identification out of the post-hoc rotation (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:692`) and into the training objective, keeping the per-stock encoder unchanged (ADV-05). What can be removed *without* losing a strength: the entire U-Net/`output_padding`/trim-pad discussion (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:394-398`) — it is engineering detail about a discarded component and carries no theoretical content beyond the length-matching fact already fixed by the sizing rule.

## Core strengths to preserve
1. **The two-stage contract** (ADV-02): nonlinear, amortized loading map in front of an explicit, exactly-solved linear factor model. It guarantees PSD covariances, diagonal consistency with sample variances, and an honest evaluation target (subspace quality vs PCA). Any redesign must keep this boundary.
2. **Scale outside the network** (ADV-06): shape from the encoder, level from $\sigma_{i,t}/\bar\sigma_t$. This is what makes loadings regime-stable and the risk model auditable; a redesign that lets the network learn levels loses the only mechanism that separates "same shape, different risk".
3. **Shared row-wise encoder** (ADV-05): a single coordinate system plus universe flexibility without retraining — the precondition for any cross-stock comparison at all.
4. **KL as a rate constraint with AU as its readout** (ADV-04), triangulated against the Bai–Ng floor and explanatory power (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:416,475`). The triangulation, not the threshold, is the strength.
5. **Closed-form sizing** (ADV-01/ADV-09): depth/width/channel rules derived from T and K, so capacity is reproducible and auditable — keep the rules, demote the ratio's claim.
6. **Invariant stability metric** (ADV-07): pairwise-distance rank correlation (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:908`) is the correct way to certify a latent space that is only identified up to $O(K)$.

## Verdict
This unit is technically the strongest part of the corpus: the receptive-field sizing is exact, the decoder geometry is exact, the OLS-plus-rescaling stage is an exactly solved factor decomposition, and the discriminations it makes (shared encoder vs cross-attention, shape vs scale, rotation vs raw latent basis, skip-connections-not-needed) are each correct for the stated reason. Its weaknesses are not errors of mathematics but of *placement*: the network builds loadings while the document narrates a generative factor model it never estimates (ADV-10); two "guarantees" — gap-free latent space (ADV-04) and $r\le5$ as overfitting control (ADV-09) — are attached to the wrong mechanisms; and the one term that does encode the cross-section is a scaffold scheduled to vanish (ADV-07). The right redesign therefore keeps almost every component and changes what is optimized: same encoder, same sizing, same downstream algebra, but a loss defined over the cross-section, with the deep-only increment placed in the conditional second moment rather than in reconstruction.
