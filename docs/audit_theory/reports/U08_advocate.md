## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** The encoder minimises a per-sample MSE, so cross-sectional dependence is only an "implicit correlation capture" of the shared weights (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:450`), explicitly declared "not guaranteed" at `DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:496`. The co-movement term makes that structure explicit and is then removed (`:508-512`). Crisis weighting exists because "stress periods represent ~2–5% of historical data, so the gradient is dominated by normal-regime patterns" (`:546`). Both are training-time-only and neither belongs to the generative model (`:540`, `:622`) — the intent is a relational prior on the encoder, not a change to the risk model.

**2. What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** (i) A pair objective whose *target* carries an explicit noise model, weighting each pair by its estimation precision (a 483-day Spearman has SE ≈ 1/√T ≈ 0.045; precision-weighted pair losses are the standard fix). (ii) A target statistic chosen for the estimand actually needed — tail/copula dependence rather than a scalar rank correlation — since the corpus itself wants tail co-movement (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:546`, `strategy_philosophy.md:50`). (iii) Auxiliary weights normalised to the units of the term they balance. (iv) The auxiliary's *incremental* value established by a pre-registered ablation that includes the null. Lineage: pairwise embedding losses (Hadsell, Chopra & LeCun 2006, "Dimensionality Reduction by Learning an Invariant Mapping" — contrastive pair loss on a shared embedding) [unverified]; distance-from-dissimilarity reconstruction (Torgerson 1952, classical MDS — a Euclidean embedding from a dissimilarity matrix, which is literally what ρ→1−ρ plus cosine asks for) [unverified]; curriculum relaxation (Bengio, Louradour, Collobert & Weston 2009, "Curriculum Learning") [unverified]; and, for the rejected alternative, InfoNCE as an MI lower bound whose tightness depends on negatives and batch size (Oord, Li & Vinyals 2018) [unverified].

**3. What prevents it (and what is only apparent).** Only apparent: the charge that the scaffold injects linear structure. The target is a *rank* statistic, the metric is matched to a scale-free representation, and the term is removed and excluded from model selection — the design is structurally clean on all three counts (findings 01, 02, 04). Real: the auxiliary is specified in a different unit system from the terms it must compete with, so its claimed effect is unsupported by the spec's own constants (03); the curriculum is keyed to an epoch count that early stopping sets using a criterion blind to the curriculum (08); and neither auxiliary can be switched off inside the specified search grid, so the corpus cannot test whether either earns its place (09).

**4. Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: within-window dependence statistics require temporally synchronised windows, so synchronous batching is forced while λ_co > 0 (`:518`) — correctly stated, and its variance cost is correctly quantified. Real: the auxiliary must be computed on μ, the object deployed at inference, not on reparameterised samples (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1367`, `:1379`). Real: inference must stay per-stock to preserve universe flexibility (`:542`, `:674`). Self-imposed: a single ρ over the whole 504-day window (the corpus lists tail dependence and copulas as alternatives, `:1012`), cosine rather than Euclidean (`:1013`), the linear mapping g (`:1014`), VIX as the only crisis label (`:1018`), a three-phase epoch-fraction curriculum rather than a fixed budget (`:510-512`), and λ_co^max = 0.5 with an "43% effective crisis contribution" target (`:571`) that no argument derives.

**5. What to remove, merge or move.** *Remove* Iteration 5 (InfoNCE): it targets a mechanism the design does not have, and its positive set is a threshold on the same statistic (finding 11). *Remove* the self-description of the scaffold as imposing "linear correlation structure" (`:1153`) — it is rank-based and the corpus is being harsher on itself than the mathematics warrants (02). *Remove* the claim that Phase 3 lets the encoder "refine beyond linear co-movement" (`:511`): the Phase-3 objective contains no cross-sectional term at all (`:512`), so the honest claim is only "no permanent commitment" (04). *Merge* crisis weighting and co-movement into one crisis-weighted relational term — the emphasis belongs where the estimand is (the covariance), not only in reconstruction (07) — and merge the three-phase schedule into a single epoch budget fixed independently of E* (08). *Move* the crisis weight from the reconstruction term onto the pair term, where γ already has the correct dimensionless form (05). Nothing in this list adds a mechanism; every item either deletes a claim the mathematics does not support or relocates an existing weight.

## Core strengths to preserve

- **Relational prior without universe coupling.** Cross-sectional information enters only through the loss; the deployed map stays per-stock (`DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:540`, `:542`, `:674`). Any redesign that makes inference depend on the contemporaneous cross-section buys joint estimation at the price of retraining on every universe change — a bad trade for this strategy.
- **Supervision on the deployed object.** The auxiliary constrains μ, not samples from q(z|x) (`ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1367`, `:1379`). This removes an entire class of train/inference mismatch and keeps the auxiliary out of the variational bound.
- **Auxiliary excluded from model selection.** Excluding γ and λ_co while retaining σ² (`:622`; `ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:163-166`) is methodologically right: a validation criterion contaminated by a training-time reweighting would select folds, not models. Keep the principle even if finding 06 says its γ side effect must be handled.
- **Scale-free target matched to a scale-free representation.** Per-window z-scoring makes the exposure shape-only (`:672`, `:724`), and both cosine distance and Spearman are invariant to exactly that transformation; absolute scale is restored later by volatility rescaling (`:728`). The invariance chain is coherent end to end.
- **Dependence measure, metric and mapping are explicit hyperparameters** with tail/copula and Euclidean/non-linear alternatives named (`:1012-1014`). This is what makes the "linear" charge answerable: the baseline is a declared choice, and the theory has already written down its own escape routes.
- **Crisis weighting is dimensionally correct, point-in-time, and honestly calibrated.** γ is dimensionless and multiplies a properly scaled term (`:586-588`), the threshold is an expanding percentile recalculated per fold with documented pre-1990 proxies (`:554-558`), and the η machinery is arithmetically exact (`:562`, `:576`). This is the template λ_co should have followed.
- **Honest self-diagnosis.** §6.6 names the risk that the scaffold biases the geometry and gives a diagnostic trigger (`:1155`); the mechanism is at least falsifiable in principle, which is more than most auxiliary losses offer.

## Verdict

The unit's conceptual core is sound and, on the three charges that matter, defensible: the scaffold is rank-based rather than linear (02), its target is exactly the shape geometry the risk model consumes and is realisable in the latent space (01), and it is genuinely released and kept out of model selection (04, 12). What the corpus gets wrong is not the *direction* of the design but its *accounting*: λ_co is added without the dimensional scaling that the reconstruction term is carefully given, so the specification simultaneously asserts that the scaffold strongly guides the geometry and that it risks permanently biasing it — the spec's own constants make the first claim unsupported and the second implausible (03). Crisis weighting is the better-engineered of the two mechanisms, but its coupling to σ² is misdescribed as orthogonality and mechanically penalises γ>1 in the validation criterion that selects it (06). Two structural holes remain: the curriculum is keyed to an epoch count that early stopping sets blind to it (08), and neither auxiliary has an admissible "off" in the specified grid, which makes the corpus's central claim about temporariness untestable by its own protocol (09). None of these requires abandoning the approach or falling back on a linear risk model; they require the auxiliary terms to be specified in the same unit system as the objective they modify, their null to be admissible, and the curriculum to be decoupled from E*. The InfoNCE iteration should be deleted rather than pursued (11).
