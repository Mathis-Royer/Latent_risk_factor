## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** Turn a *shape-only* latent exposure matrix into a complete PSD asset covariance, without letting scale leak into factor discovery. The spec is explicit: `Σ_assets = B̃_port Σ_z (B̃_port)^T + D_ε` with all quantities in the active subspace (DVT:710), a dual rescaling because "using today's σ_i to rescale loadings applied to returns from 2008 … misattributes systematic risk to idiosyncratic residuals" (DVT:724), a cross-sectional OLS recovering z_t in a stable unit (DVT:746–750), Ledoit–Wolf shrinkage over full history (DVT:752), and a diagonal idiosyncratic block so that "all inter-stock correlation passes through the factors" (DVT:761). MOD-007 restates the same four sub-tasks and four invariants (ISD:1686–1794). The intent is a **pure risk model**: no expected returns anywhere in the block (DVT:680).

**2. What a state-of-the-art version looks like.** A Barra/Axioma-grade cross-sectional risk model: WLS factor regression with cap-based weights and HAC/Newey–West inference; robust selection of the number of statistical factors (Bai–Ng 2002, Onatski 2010); a factor-covariance estimator tuned for the *portfolio* loss rather than Frobenius loss (Ledoit–Wolf 2017, "Markowitz meets Goldilocks"); dynamic covariance combining EWMA with shrinkage (Engle–Ledoit–Wolf 2019); eigenfactor risk adjustment and a volatility-regime adjustment instead of a plain holdout ratio (Menchero, Orr & Wang 2011, USE4 §4); and a residual covariance richer than a diagonal when explanatory power is low. For a VAE factor model specifically, the modern analogue is a latent-factor risk model whose factor count is identified statistically, not architecturally (Gu–Kelly–Xiu 2021 use ML factors for *returns*; the risk-model side is still Barra-shaped).

**3. What prevents it — and which obstacles are only apparent.** Real blockers: (a) factor explanatory power is low — the diagnostic reports EP 0.356 OOS and 0.219 IS (`diagnostic_report.md:59-60`), so 64–78% of variance sits in a **diagonal** residual block (DVT:761), which structurally cannot price residual co-movement; the OOS variance ratio of 2.74 (`diagnostic_report.md:57`) is consistent with that, not with a shrinkage bug. (b) B is frozen between retrainings (DVT:676), so in the late folds the factor *definitions* are years old even while the covariances refresh. Apparent obstacles: *ridge* (SVD-`lstsq` is better than the specified ridge; the reported κ(B)=2.4e4, `diagnostic_report.md:70`, means OLS error scales with 1e4, not with κ(B^T B)≈6e8); *EWMA incompatibility with LW* (a weighted covariance is still shrinkable — only the analytic intensity is mis-specified, and the code's raw/EWMA decoupling is a reasonable response, `covariance.py:313-349`); *"all AU dimensions must enter Σ_z"* (BBP identifies only 12 of 27, `diagnostic_data.json:116`, so most of the covariance is bulk); *"full history is required"* (the 20% VT carve-out already gives that up, see ADV-03).

**4. Real vs self-imposed constraints.** *Real*: the VAE is trained on z-scored windows so B carries no scale (DVT:724) — the rescaling is forced and correctly designed; no CRSP, no delisting returns (shared briefing), so residuals at delistings are contaminated (U02/U03); 31 years of daily data, one market family; only ~12 statistically identifiable factors. *Self-imposed*: the proportional 20% VT carve-out (should be a fixed horizon); three shrinkage estimators of which one is used; two dead conditioning knobs; **four** copies of the same build path; winsorization carried into the portfolio matrix; a full-AU Σ_z that is then split into signal/noise downstream.

**5. Remove, merge, move.** Remove: `conditioning_threshold`/`ridge_scale` (dead, `conditioning.py:40-41`), the `truncation` path (scale-inconsistent under EWMA, ADV-05), `_estimate_n_signal_from_gap` (admitted to fail on DGJ output, `covariance.py:290-293`), the portfolio-side winsorization (ADV-04), the scalar "block VT" fallback, and three of the four build copies. Merge: VT into the covariance module and signal/noise splitting into `estimate_sigma_z`, so the off-by-one cannot recur in three files. Move: PC1/market exclusion into the eigen-decomposition step; the 80/20 split into an explicit (estimation window, calibration horizon) interface. None of these removals touches a strength listed below.

## Core strengths to preserve

1. **Dual rescaling (estimation vs portfolio).** The single most important idea in the unit. Rescaling historical loadings by the σ_i,t/σ̄_t that prevailed when the returns were realized is the mathematically correct way to stop regime volatility from being misattributed to idiosyncratic residuals (DVT:724). It also makes D_ε a scale-normalized quantity, which is why a single long-run idiosyncratic variance is defensible (A6). Any redesign must keep the *two* matrices and never collapse them into one.
2. **Cross-sectional OLS with date-specific B_t, active-universe rows, and correct row alignment.** The row-slicing bug fix (factor_regression.py:74-78, 232-238) is exactly the kind of silent misalignment that would invalidate the whole model; it is handled and documented.
3. **SVD-`lstsq` instead of (B^T B)^{-1} + ridge.** Numerically superior, rank-revealing, and it removes an uncalibratable hyperparameter — the reasoning at DVT:720 against ridge and SVD *dimension selection* is sound, and using SVD only as a *solver* is consistent with it. Preserve the solver; replace the dead guard with a diagnostic (ADV-08).
4. **Signal/noise separation with a defensible bulk model.** Only statistically identified eigenvalues enter the entropy objective; noise eigenvalues are kept at σ² in Σ_assets rather than zeroed (pipeline.py:2286-2289), which is the correct DGJ prescription and prevents systematic-risk understatement. The *concept* is right; only the index arithmetic is wrong (ADV-02).
5. **Per-factor variance targeting in the eigenbasis.** Scaling eigenvalues by positive factors preserves PSD by construction, unlike elementwise covariance scaling; the Bayesian shrink toward 1 with prior strength 20 (pipeline.py:119, 2335-2337) is a principled way to avoid overfitting a short holdout; and the holdout is genuinely disjoint from the Σ_z/D_ε estimation window (pipeline.py:2243-2251). Keep the mechanism, change the horizon (ADV-03).
6. **Kurtosis-corrected James–Stein shrinkage of D_ε.** Var(s²)=d²(κ₄+2)/(T−1) is the correct non-normal formula, κ₄≥0 is conservative, the floor is reapplied, and floored stocks are excluded from the mean (covariance.py:534-569). This is above the standard of most production code and better than its own documentation (ADV-10).
7. **EWMA bookkeeping when it is used.** Weighted mean, sqrt-weight transform, Kish n_eff, and the explicit choice of z_input.T@z_input over `np.cov` in the EWMA branches (covariance.py:133-161, 279-286, 342-349) are exactly right; the raw-vs-EWMA decoupling for n_signal is a thoughtful, documented mitigation of a real statistical conflict.
8. **Assembly invariants as executable guarantees.** Spectral reconstruction of Σ_assets (covariance.py:607), eigenvalue ≥ 0 (618), rotation equivalence (test_risk_model.py:423-457), D_ε floor (669), and the explicit symmetrization after VT (pipeline.py:2401). In exact arithmetic Σ_assets is PSD by construction.
9. **Point-in-time discipline in rescaling.** Median and winsorization are computed per date over the active universe only, missing vols are imputed at that date's median, and the portfolio lookup snaps to the last trading day ≤ the rebalance date (rescaling.py:106-135, 190-225). No full-sample statistic leaks in.
10. **OOS refresh design.** The encoder is frozen, only rescaling/regression/covariance are refreshed on the expanding window, and the refreshed model is aligned to the point-in-time universe before optimization (oos_rebalancing.py:306-310, 768-780). This is the correct separation of "factor discovery" and "risk calibration".

## Verdict

The architecture of this unit is the strongest part of the strategy's risk side, and several implementations are genuinely above standard: dual rescaling, per-factor VT in the eigenbasis with a disjoint holdout, kurtosis-corrected James–Stein on D_ε, and PSD-by-construction assembly are all correct ideas, correctly motivated and mostly correctly executed. Against that, four things are real: the DGJ inversion has a sign error that invalidates the "Frobenius-optimal" label for weak factors (bounded in impact because γ is small); the 20% proportional VT carve-out silently deletes the most recent ~6 years of covariance history from a model whose entire justification is permanent crisis memory; the signal/noise slice keeps one noise factor in the entropy objective in three separate files; and the unit's central estimator choices are not reproducible from the committed notebook and config. None of these is fatal, but the third and fourth are architectural symptoms — four divergent copies of one build path — and the fifth, the diagonal residual block at 0.36 explanatory power with an OOS variance ratio of 2.74, is the limitation a redesign should address rather than tune. What must *not* be lost is the estimation/portfolio split, the PSD-preserving per-factor VT with a real calibration horizon, and the honest signal/noise separation.

Score: 7/10
