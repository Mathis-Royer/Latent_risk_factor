## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent of this unit.**
The unit answers one question: *which data may the discovery mechanism consume?* The requirements it must satisfy are stated up front: "continuous multi-dimensional exposures, memory of dormant factors, sensitivity to tail co-movements" (`docs/DVT_...v4_1.md:95`). The resolution is explicit and two-part: "Price data is used for latent factor discovery. Fundamental data is used only for *a posteriori* interpretation" (`docs/DVT_...v4_1.md:136-137`). The deeper criterion is in §2.4: "what matters is the **effective correlation of assets**, regardless of its origin" (`docs/DVT_...v4_1.md:89`). So the intent is not "prices are a better dataset" but "the optimizer consumes a covariance structure, therefore measure dependence where it is observable at high frequency and defer economic naming to a separate, non-load-bearing step". `docs/strategy_philosophy.md:69` restates the same intent.

**2. What a state-of-the-art version would look like.**
(i) A point-in-time, survivorship-bias-free panel with delisting returns (CRSP/Shumway 1997, already reference [22] at `docs/DVT_...v4_1.md:1620`). (ii) A statistical factor model as the null — Connor & Korajczyk (1988), Bai & Ng (2002, already [27]), Lettau & Pelger (2020), and commercial statistical/structural risk models (Barra USE4, already invoked at `src/config.py:437-442`). (iii) A characteristics-conditioned challenger, because the dominant modern design maps firm characteristics to exposures rather than a firm's own return history: IPCA (Kelly, Pruitt & Su 2019) and the conditional autoencoder of Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2021, already [11] at `docs/DVT_...v4_1.md:1598`). (iv) Intraday/realized covariance estimators to handle the Epps effect (Epps 1979; Hayashi–Yoshida 2005) rather than daily close-to-close only. (v) Interpretation by Fama–MacBeth regressions of factor returns/exposures on characteristics and sector dummies; tail dependence via realized semivariance or downside beta (Ang, Chen & Xing 2006, already in the repo's own list at `docs/literature_review_strategy.md:307`). The repo already contains the right parsimonious control: PCA factor RP / PCA+vol benchmarks (`docs/DVT_...v4_1.md:1159-1167`).

**3. What prevents it — and which obstacles are only apparent.**
*Apparent:* "fundamentals are too low-frequency" (see U02-ADV-10) — false for cross-sectional exposures; "fundamentals would bias discovery" — true only if fed as *inputs*, irrelevant for a benchmark or an ex-post regression; "prices cannot be interpreted" — sector/characteristic correlations cost one regression. *Real:* the price panel does not satisfy the premise. Delisting returns are structurally absent (`scripts/download_tiingo.py:911`; `src/data_pipeline/returns.py:182-195`), universe selection is full-sample (`src/data_pipeline/data_loader.py:339-348`), the point-in-time universe module is never called by the pipeline (`src/data_pipeline/universe.py:13`, only used at `:166` and in tests), and the documented 20%-missing guard is dead (`src/config.py:137`; no use in `src/data_pipeline`), leaving 410/788 stocks above that threshold (`results/diagnostic/diagnostic_report.md:30-31`). Consequence: the choice "prices vs fundamentals" cannot currently be *tested*, because neither side is measured properly and there is no characteristics challenger.

**4. Real constraints vs self-imposed choices.**
*Real:* Tiingo free tier has no shares outstanding (hence the price×volume×20 cap proxy, `scripts/download_tiingo.py:890-901`) and no delisting returns; CRSP/Compustat are costly; daily Epps noise; 788 usable names over 31.2 years (`results/diagnostic/diagnostic_report.md:26-29`); 16 GB compute. *Self-imposed:* F=2 exactly (`src/config.py:133-135`), per-window z-scoring that erases absolute volatility (`docs/DVT_...v4_1.md:240`), static per-fold composite exposures (`src/inference/composite.py:155-165`), an imposed unit-exposure market factor (`src/config.py:464`; `src/integration/pipeline.py:2205-2224`), exclusion of fundamentals *even from interpretation*, and the absence of any characteristics-based benchmark. Only the first group is a constraint; the second is design.

**5. What should be removed, merged or moved.**
- **Remove** the market-efficiency justification (`docs/DVT_...v4_1.md:99`). It is unnecessary (the risk-measurement argument at `docs/strategy_philosophy.md:11` is stronger and does not require efficiency) and it is in tension with the premise that structure can be learned. No strength is lost.
- **Remove** the "frequency problem" argument (`docs/DVT_...v4_1.md:126`) or restate it narrowly as "cannot estimate high-frequency dependence dynamics from quarterly data". The other two arguments (bias at :130, coverage at :132) already carry the decision.
- **Remove** "fundamentals for interpretation" from §3.4's architecture claim (`docs/DVT_...v4_1.md:136-137`) until implemented; **move** it to an explicitly optional post-hoc step outside the critical path. Nothing in the pipeline consumes it.
- **Remove (or wire)** the dead point-in-time universe implementation (`src/data_pipeline/universe.py:13-99`): leaving it un-wired creates the false assurance that point-in-time construction exists.
- **Merge** the discovery narrative: cross-sectional discovery is performed by the Spearman co-movement target (`src/vae/loss.py:451-506`) and the cross-sectional OLS (`src/integration/pipeline.py:2230`), not by the input channels. Saying so loses nothing and corrects §4.2:222.
- **Move** the adjudication of the price-only thesis into the benchmark layer (PCA factor RP already there) and add one characteristics challenger. Keep: price-only discovery, per-window normalization, the realized-vol channel, and downstream vol rescaling — these are the unit's real assets.

## Core strengths to preserve

1. **Price-only discovery with a hard, structural guarantee.** The absence of any fundamental column in the real schema (`scripts/download_tiingo.py:903-912`) makes "no a priori economic bias" enforceable rather than aspirational. Any redesign that adds characteristics must keep this as a *measured* ablation, not a default.
2. **Shape/scale separation.** Per-window z-scoring plus realized-vol channel plus downstream vol-ratio rescaling (`src/data_pipeline/windowing.py:251-260`; `src/risk_model/rescaling.py:38-49`) is a correct decomposition: discovery learns dependence shapes, covariance learns scale. Replacing it with a raw-return encoder would confound the two.
3. **Raw series rather than pre-computed correlations.** Even though §4.2:222 overstates what the encoder can see, feeding windows rather than a correlation matrix preserves temporal information and allows the same input to serve the co-movement target. Keep the input representation.
4. **Causal feature construction.** Rolling vol uses only prior returns; warm-up is respected; edge cases are clamped and filtered exactly as specified (`src/data_pipeline/features.py:76-78`; `src/data_pipeline/windowing.py:108-109`). This is verified no-look-ahead plumbing.
5. **A parsimonious statistical null already exists.** PCA factor RP and PCA+vol instantiate "prices for discovery" without a VAE (`docs/DVT_...v4_1.md:1159-1167`). This is the correct arbiter of whether the neural path adds anything and should survive any redesign unchanged.
6. **The decision criterion is right.** "Effective correlation of assets, regardless of its origin" (`docs/DVT_...v4_1.md:89`) is the correct objective for a risk model and justifies ignoring data-type ideology in favour of measured co-movement.

## Verdict

The unit's thesis is correct and, at the mechanism level, faithfully implemented: discovery consumes only price-derived series, the two-channel input is causally constructed, scale is separated from shape and re-attached downstream, and the absence of fundamental columns in the real dataset gives the "no economic prior" guarantee real teeth. What is weaker is the supporting argument and the data instantiation. Two of the four justifications should be deleted (the EMH paragraph and the "80 quarterly observations" argument), the claimed "no revisions" advantage is false for adjusted prices, and §4.2 credits the input channels with a cross-sectional discovery that actually happens in the co-movement loss and the factor regression. The decisive problem is that the premise's load-bearing assumption — a complete, point-in-time price record — is not met: delisting returns are structurally absent, the universe is selected with full-sample information, and the documented missing-data guard is dead. Finally, the "fundamentals for interpretation" half of the resolution has no implementation and no characteristics challenger exists, so the choice of data type is an untested commitment rather than a demonstrated result. Keep the price-only discovery architecture; fix the panel and add one characteristics benchmark before anyone claims the choice is validated.

Score: 7/10
