## Five questions

1. **Purpose and original intent.** The unit is the post-mortem and decision layer: after V1/V2 it was supposed to diagnose the failure, attribute causes, and choose Path A/B/C while honoring the terminal principle “if the VAE does not statistically outperform PCA … the VAE is abandoned” (`docs/strategy_philosophy.md:174-183`). `strategic_paths_post_v2.md:1-5` frames it as a “Decision Framework” whose trigger is the clean Phase 1 run. The intent is diagnostic and falsifiable; the execution is advocacy with a pre-committed conclusion.

2. **State-of-the-art version of this unit.** A credible post-mortem would have: (i) a pre-registered primary endpoint and minimum power, with an explicit “inconclusive” outcome; (ii) controlled ablations of the V2 bundle, not one aggregate Sharpe; (iii) multiplicity control and effect sizes with confidence intervals; (iv) 1/N and passive benchmarks as first-class hurdles (DeMiguel et al., 2009, already cited at `docs/literature_review_strategy.md:105-109`); (v) reproducibility artifacts (config/data/checkpoint hashes); and (vi) benchmark parity. The relevant external standards are Bailey & López de Prado (2014) on deflated Sharpe ratios, Harvey, Liu & Zhu (2016) on multiple-testing hurdles, and Nosek et al. (2015) on reproducibility. The current unit has none of (i)–(v) in operational form.

3. **What prevents the current unit from reaching it.**
   - The decision code cannot say “not enough data”. With `n < 5` folds every metric is skipped (`selection.py:112-113`), leaving `wins=0` for every benchmark; `pca_tie = wins <= 1` is then vacuously true (`:130`) and Scenario C → “Adopt PCA” is emitted (`:135-144`). The real diagnostic has one fold (`results/diagnostic/report.json:153`), all tests skipped, `n_tests=0` (`:137`), and yet `report.txt:5-6` prints “Scenario: C / Recommendation: Adopt PCA (cost /100)”.
   - The only real evaluation is a single 20% holdout via `pipeline.run_direct` (`scripts/run_diagnostic.py:438,444`; `results/diagnostic/config.json:134`), not the claimed 34-fold walk-forward (`docs/strategy_philosophy.md:174`). The only multi-fold artifact is a 9-fold synthetic E*=1 smoke test (`results/report.json:287`), and it does not say “adopt PCA” (`:3,30`).
   - The V1/V2 numbers used as the decisive evidence are unreproducible: “Previous diagnostic runs were lost (Colab storage)” (`.claude/rules/project/changelog.md:31`), yet the document quotes Sharpe, MDD and CS R² to two decimals (`docs/strategic_paths_post_v2.md:3,217-223`).
   - The “per-fix autopsy” is not an autopsy: V2 applied eight changes simultaneously (`.claude/rules/project/changelog.md:16`), and no ablation isolates any of them (`:13`).
   - The MP evidence is a code artifact (see U16-MAJ-06), and the crisis-weighting defense is dead wiring (U16-CRI-04).
   
   *Steelman.* The documents are unusually candid for a quant project: they record negative results, admit the V2 CS loss was computed batch-level and “trivially high R²” (`docs/strategic_paths_post_v2.md:170-173`), admit that gamma’s benefit is uncertain (`docs/analyse_critique_strategie.md:1028-1036`), and state a terminal principle that could kill the VAE. *Rebuttal.* Candor about uncertainty is not evidence; the same documents label mechanisms “confirmed harmful” without a controlled test (`notebooks/dashboard.ipynb:251`; `docs/strategic_paths_post_v2.md:291`), suppress the one surviving run that contradicts the conclusion, and route the decision through code that cannot express “inconclusive” or “1/N wins”. Procedural honesty without auditable evidence is still post-hoc rationalization.

4. **Real vs self-imposed constraints.**
   - *Real*: 16 GB compute; 788 stocks × 7,874 days of Tiingo data; no CRSP; non-stationary markets; finite-sample estimation error; multiple testing across many configurations.
   - *Self-imposed*: the 34-fold claim with no artifact; the three-path A/B/C decision tree with no “inconclusive” or “1/N” branch; the terminal principle that compares only to PCA while EW/inverse-vol/S&P sit in the same table; arbitrary CS-R² thresholds (3/5/10/15%) and variance-ratio bands ([0.5,2.0] vs [0.5,3.0]); retaining no artifact manifest; running the notebook with `vix_data=None`; treating a one-fold direct holdout as a deployment test; comparing a K52/N739 model to K75/N3155 runs without disclosure. These are choices, not data limits.

5. **What should be removed, merged or moved.**
   - *Remove*: V1/V2 numbers from the evidence base until reproduced; the “per-fix autopsy / confirmed harmful” language; the MP “1 eigenvalue” claim until the aspect ratio is fixed; “PCA Sharpe 0.511” and “PCA CS R² 25–35%” from the decision table (no artifact); the crisis-weighting defense of symmetric risk until VIX is wired and gamma=1 vs 3 is tested; the “86% degradation” percentage language.
   - *Merge*: `analyse_critique_strategie.md` and `literature_review_strategy.md` into one review with one recommendation (they currently contradict each other on persistence-weighted entropy: `literature_review_strategy.md:284` vs `analyse_critique_strategie.md:441`); the three decision rules (`strategic_paths` matrix, `selection.py`, `diagnostic_experiments.py` Tier 1) into one pre-registered rule with D and “inconclusive” branches.
   - *Move*: the factor-risk-parity claim from theory to a measured output (top-1 contribution, H_norm, ENB); “Adopt PCA” behind a full walk-forward with 1/N and S&P as primary hurdles; the `divergences.md` self-audit out of the assurance chain (it is stale and all-keep); the notebook from runtime to reporting only, with a single versioned config.

## Root-cause hypotheses

**R1 — The decision architecture has no “inconclusive” state and no 1/N branch.** This one design choice explains CRI-01, CRI-02, CRI-05, MAJ-09 and MAJ-15. `check_deployment_criteria` is a winner-selection machine: it skips metrics when `n<5`, treats `wins<=1` as a PCA tie, and maps A/B/C to deployment advice. It cannot say “insufficient power”, cannot say “1/N wins” (Scenario D is documented but unreachable), and cannot separate diagnosis from deployment. The documents then inherit the same binary framing (Path A/B/C). The result is that the only honest conclusion available from one real fold — “no decision is possible” — is unavailable.
- *Alternative 1*: sequential testing with a pre-registered minimum-fold gate and an explicit “inconclusive” state. Trade-off: slower and will often stop without a winner, but every output is defensible.
- *Alternative 2*: Bayesian model comparison with a posterior probability of superiority and a loss function that includes compute cost. Trade-off: requires priors and more machinery, but handles small samples and yields a graded conclusion.
- *Alternative 3*: separate “diagnosis” (facts + CIs, no recommendation) from “deployment” (human/committee decision). Trade-off: less automation, more accountability.

**R2 — The post-mortem treats a single aggregate backtest as a causal experiment.** This explains CRI-03, CRI-05, MAJ-08, MAJ-09, MAJ-17 and MAJ-18. V2 is an eight-change bundle; the “autopsy” has no ablation; the V1/V2 artifacts are lost; the surviving run differs in K, N and phi; and the benchmark numbers come from different code paths. Every causal claim (“CS loss is harmful”, “architecture mismatch”, “PCA wins”) is an inference from a single confounded observation.
- *Alternative 1*: checkpoint-replay ablation on a fixed model: vary one component at a time (CS loss, AU filter, lambda_co, VT, phi) on the same folds. Trade-off: more runs, but no retraining if replay is used.
- *Alternative 2*: Shapley / leave-one-component-out attribution over the eight V2 changes. Trade-off: 2^8 combinations, but replay makes it tractable and captures interactions.
- *Alternative 3*: pre-registered kill criteria with held-out folds and a sequential stop. Trade-off: less narrative flexibility, but credible.

**R3 — Theory and implementation disagree on what “factor risk parity” means.** The documents derive the unconstrained entropy limit `|β| ∝ 1/√λ` (`strategy_philosophy.md:102,113-116`), but the code solves `-λ w'Σw + α H(w)` with `λ=252`, w_max/w_min, a concentration penalty and an α selected on a variance-entropy frontier. The realized portfolio has top-1 factor risk 81.1% and ENB 2.70. The label “factor risk parity” describes a limiting case that the optimizer is not asked to reach. This explains MAJ-10, MAJ-11 and the “under-exposed to market” narrative in `analyse_critique_strategie.md:702-704`.
- *Alternative 1*: impose equal risk contributions as a constraint within tolerance, rather than an entropy bonus. Trade-off: may be infeasible with variance minimization, but the claim becomes testable.
- *Alternative 2*: report the achieved risk-contribution vector, H_norm and ENB as primary outputs and define success as ENB ≥ target. Trade-off: changes the objective, but aligns theory and measurement.
- *Alternative 3*: rename the strategy “entropy-regularized minimum variance” and drop the risk-parity claim. Trade-off: less appealing narrative, but honest.

**R4 — The evidence pipeline is not reproducible.** Lost runs, mixed checkpoints, config/report mismatches, dead VIX wiring, a benchmark runner broken on every fold, and a notebook that overrides config defaults create a situation where no number can be traced to a (data, config, code, checkpoint) tuple. This explains CRI-04, MAJ-09, MAJ-17, MAJ-18 and the `data_source: synthetic` vs `_diagnostic: tiingo` inconsistency (`results/diagnostic/config.json:9,129`).
- *Alternative 1*: immutable run manifests with config/data/checkpoint hashes and a results registry. Trade-off: engineering overhead, but every number becomes traceable.
- *Alternative 2*: one canonical entry point that reads one versioned config and writes one artifact bundle. Trade-off: less notebook flexibility, but eliminates drift.
- *Alternative 3*: notebooks as reports only; all parameters in versioned config files. Trade-off: less interactive, but reproducible.

**R5 — The benchmark set is self-serving.** The terminal principle compares only to PCA, while 1/N, inverse-vol and S&P are in the same table and beat the VAE in the real run. Scenario D is documented but unreachable. This explains MAJ-14, MAJ-15 and MAJ-17. The post-mortem chooses the comparison that makes the VAE look salvageable (PCA) and ignores the comparison that condemns it (1/N).
- *Alternative 1*: make 1/N the primary hurdle (as the cited DeMiguel result implies) and PCA the secondary. Trade-off: harder to pass, but aligned with the literature.
- *Alternative 2*: use the maximum Sharpe among EW/IV/PCA/S&P as the hurdle. Trade-off: conservative and subject to selection bias, but no cherry-picking.
- *Alternative 3*: report all benchmarks with confidence intervals and no “winner” claim until significance survives correction. Trade-off: less decisive, more honest.

## Verdict

This unit is not an audit; it is a sequence of advocacy documents with post-hoc rationalization. The strongest surviving evidence contradicts the headline conclusion: the one real diagnostic run has VAE Sharpe 0.382 vs PCA 0.302, CS R² 13%, and its own decision matrix selects Path A, yet the artifact prints “Adopt PCA” because a one-fold run makes every metric skip and every benchmark show 0/4 wins. The crisis-weighting defense is dead code in every entry point; the MP “one eigenvalue” evidence is an inverted-aspect-ratio bug; the factor-risk-parity claim is contradicted by an 81.1% top-factor risk contribution and ENB 2.70; the V1/V2 numbers are unreproducible; the “per-fix autopsy” has no ablation; and the benchmark story depends on a 0.511 Sharpe that exists in no artifact. The unit does contain genuine self-criticism — the admission that V2’s CS loss was batch-level, the admission that gamma’s benefit is uncertain, and the terminal principle itself — but these are procedural gestures, not evidence. The correct verdict from the surviving evidence is “inconclusive at full scale, and 1/N currently wins”, not “adopt PCA”. The unit should be rebuilt around a pre-registered, powered, reproducible decision rule with an explicit inconclusive state and 1/N as the primary hurdle; until then, its conclusions should not be used to justify an architectural redesign.

Score: 3/10
