## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent.** Turn a pure risk model into weights, with diversification measured on *principal-factor risk contributions*, and with return-seeking kept out: "The encoder produces a **pure risk model** with no return views. Return-seeking behavior is injected at the optimizer level as a separate, removable layer." `docs/DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:680`. The objective is declared a "two-level hierarchy": risk minimisation primary, factor diversification as the means `DVT:765`. Non-concavity of H is acknowledged and handled by SCA with Armijo `DVT:816-832`, cardinality by post-hoc greedy elimination `DVT:834-842`, and α by a variance-entropy frontier `DVT:814`. ISD restates this verbatim as MOD-008 `docs/ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1821-1823`.

**2. What SOTA would look like.** (i) The sub-problem should be a *genuinely* convex program solved to optimality (conic/QP), because SCA's KKT guarantee rests on the surrogate being maximised exactly — exactly Feng & Palomar's condition cited at `DVT:820-828`. (ii) Risk parity/budgeting should use the convex log-barrier formulation (Spinu 2013; Roncalli 2013) rather than non-convex direct entropy maximisation; the code itself invokes Roncalli for tilted entropy (`entropy.py:54`). (iii) Cardinality should be solved as MIQP when a MIP solver is available (Bertsimas & Shioda 2009; Chang et al. 2000) and only heuristically otherwise — the code has that ladder (`cardinality.py:761-764`) but only if MOSEK is present. (iv) The diversification target should be a stated effective-number-of-bets level with a documented, economically motivated value (Meucci 2009), not an arbitrary fraction of the eigenvalue entropy. (v) Hard constraints (turnover, caps) should be enforced by construction in *every* solver path, not by post-hoc diagnostics. (vi) Cost terms should be calibrated in return units (Almgren & Chriss 2000) or explicitly declared as dimensionless regularisers.

**3. What prevents it — and which obstacles are only apparent.** The *apparent* obstacle is non-concavity: it does not prevent a globally solvable convex sub-problem, and the doc says so correctly `DVT:820-823`. Also apparent is the α/λ scaling problem: because "Only the ratio … affects optimal weights" `DVT:771`, the economic calibration of λ `DVT:786` is a non-issue, and expending design effort on "α in return units" `DVT:1052` is wasted. The *real* obstacles are (a) n≈800 and a 5-start × 9-point frontier make exact conic solves expensive per fold, and (b) the implementation has quietly replaced the convex sub-problem by 50 PGD steps (`sca_solver.py:194-348`, default `use_fast_subproblem=True` at `config.py:643`) with a **halved risk curvature** (`sca_solver.py:220, 280-282` vs `objective_function` at `sca_solver.py:707-711`). That is a self-imposed engineering shortcut, not a mathematical necessity, and it voids the convergence claim the doc advertises.

**4. Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: long-only/full-investment, per-name cap, turnover, transaction costs, covariance estimation noise (U09). Self-imposed: μ=0 (deliberate and well argued, `DVT:776`), `λ=252`, `w_max=3-5%`, `w_min=0.1%`, `τ_max=30%`, quarterly rebalancing (`config.py:584-618`), the 5-point coarse α grid with early stop (`config.py:631-633`), the ENB target `min(n_signal/2, 0.7·ENB_spectrum)` with a floor of 2 (`frontier.py:535-537`), the two-layer entropy with fixed idio weight (`entropy.py:94`), gradient clipping at norm 10 (`entropy.py:202`), and the α-normalisation at equal weight (`sca_solver.py:1110-1120`). The last three are band-aids on unit/scale problems created by the first two; they are not market realities.

**5. What to remove, merge or move.** Remove: the soft concentration penalty as currently configured (φ=0 by default `config.py:588`; in the diagnostic `w_bar=w_max=0.03` makes P_conc identically zero — `results/diagnostic/config.json:76-79`), the λ "market-implied" calibration narrative (a no-op given `DVT:771`), the Kneedle code path (unused fallback and not Kneedle; see U11-ADV-19), the "min-variance" and "ERC (Spinu)" starts that are in fact inverse-variance and inverse-vol (`sca_solver.py:1130-1141`), and the always-on gradient clip. Merge: one objective/scaling factory used by the frontier, by post-cardinality re-optimisation and by OOS rebalancing (today α is normalised in three different ways — U11-ADV-14/15), and one constraint module whose projection enforces turnover for both the CVXPY and PGD paths. Move: cardinality enforcement inside the SCA loop (or at least recompute the frontier statistics after it), and the risk-model refresh decision out of the rebalancer into the validation protocol where it can be audited. None of these removals touches the mathematical core (principal-basis entropy, its exact gradient, the μ=0 separation, Armijo ascent).

## Core strengths to preserve
1. **Principal-factor entropy with a proven non-negative contribution vector and an exact analytic gradient** (`DVT:684-704`, `entropy.py:148-183`). This is the intellectual core; it is correct, testable, and cheap (one n×AU product per SCA iteration). Any redesign must keep the rotation, not replace it with a "sign-safe" ad-hoc measure.
2. **The μ=0 separation** (`DVT:680, 776`; `ISD:1895-1897`). Keeping return views out of the risk model is the strategy's only credible claim to being "pure risk"; the notebook honours it. Keep the layer removable and keep it removed in the declared operating configuration.
3. **Monotone-ascent outer loop with Armijo** (`DVT:824-828`, `sca_solver.py:728-766`). Even with an inexact surrogate, the line search ensures the true objective does not decrease; that property is what makes any SCA variant diagnosable. Keep the sufficient-decrease test and the recorded step-size trajectory.
4. **Deterministic multi-start with an explicit best-of rule and diverse seeds** (`sca_solver.py:1127-1159, 1198-1214`). Keep the mechanism; restore M=5 and the genuine min-var/ERC starts rather than deleting multi-start.
5. **Feasibility by projection** (`sca_solver.py:181-186, 309, 981-987`). Long-only, fully invested and capped are guaranteed at every iterate; this is what allows the non-convex search to be safe.
6. **Cost/constraint separation** — soft penalties inside the objective, hard caps as limits (`DVT:792`). Keep the architecture, but make the soft part actually operate (today it is dead, U11-ADV-17) and the hard part actually enforced (U11-ADV-12).
7. **Factor-only ENB for acceptance and eigenvalue-spectrum-aware target** (`frontier.py:391-401, 503-546`). The idea of stating the operating point in effective-number-of-bets units, independent of the idio layer, is the right acceptance language; keep it, but set the target level from an economic argument rather than `n_signal/2`.
8. **Two-phase frontier with warm starts, early stop and explicit non-monotonicity warnings** (`frontier.py:120-252, 446-498`). Good engineering with auditable honesty; keep the audit trail, remove the misnamed Kneedle path.
9. **Scheduled vs exceptional rebalancing with an entropy trigger** (`oos_rebalancing.py:437-445, 715-741`). A coherent, testable control policy; keep the trigger, align the risk-model-refresh rule with the protocol.
10. **Shared constraint parameterisation across the VAE and the six benchmarks** (`ISD:1968, 1994`; `test_benchmarks.py:703-766`). This is the precondition for any fair comparison; preserve it and make the VAE actually honour it.

## Verdict
The mathematical skeleton of U11 is sound and, in the parts that matter most, correctly executed: the principal-basis rotation is exact, the entropy gradient (including the tilted/budgeted version) is derived and implemented correctly, the μ=0 separation is a defensible architectural choice, and the outer Armijo loop plus projection keeps iterates feasible and non-decreasing. That is a real foundation. What is not sound is the layer between that mathematics and the delivered portfolios. The default fast sub-problem optimises a risk-halved surrogate while the objective uses λw'Σw, so the advertised KKT guarantee does not apply; the hard turnover cap is absent from that path and is never validated; α is normalised differently in the frontier, after cardinality and at every OOS rebalance, so a single α label denotes three objectives; the budgeted entropy that the notebook selects is not the entropy the frontier measures; the soft concentration penalty is disabled by φ=0 and by w_bar=w_max in the one real run; and the realised operating point (α at the grid floor, ENB 2.70 of 3.30 maximum, top factor 81% of risk) is not factor risk parity but a mildly diversified minimum-variance corner. These are architectural, not cosmetic: they mean the unit currently cannot demonstrate that its stated objective is the objective it optimises, nor that the portfolio it delivers is the portfolio it selected. The core should be kept; the duplicated scaling, the dead penalty and the unenforced constraint should be removed or merged, and the sub-problem should be returned to the exact convex solve that the theory already assumes.

Score: 5/10
