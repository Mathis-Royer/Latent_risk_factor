## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent of this unit (cite the spec).**
The unit decides *which data class feeds latent-risk-factor discovery*, and resolves it by fiat: "**Price data is used for latent factor discovery. Fundamental data is used only for *a posteriori* interpretation**" (`docs/DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:136-137`). The stated justification is four-fold — discovery without prior bias, capture of risk "as it actually manifests", data parsimony, later economic understanding (`DVT:139-143`; `docs/strategy_philosophy.md:62-69`). Its role in the architecture is foundational: it defines the information set that everything downstream (B, Σ_z, D, the entropy objective) can see.

**2. What a state-of-the-art version of this unit would look like for this objective (cite literature if you can).**
Modern risk models do not pose this dichotomy. They use a *hybrid*: factor *realizations* and the factor *covariance* from returns, factor *exposures* from point-in-time characteristics (Barra USE4: country + industry + style blocks, Menchero-Orr-Wang 2011; Axioma/Axioma-MSCI; Fama-French 1993/2015). The learned-factor analogue is IPCA (Kelly, Pruitt & Su, *JFE* 2019) and Instrumented PCA (Kelly-Pruitt-Su 2017), where characteristics are **instruments** for time-varying exposures while factors are estimated from returns — discovery stays data-driven, identification and interpretation become structural. Nonlinear extensions (Gu-Kelly-Xiu 2020) keep characteristics as inputs. The SOTA answer to "what data" is therefore: *returns for co-movement, characteristics for identification, both point-in-time and lagged* — the dichotomy is dissolved, not resolved. For the covariance-validation half, SOTA is shrinkage + Q-statistic testing (Ledoit-Wolf; Engle-Colacito), which the repo partly has.

**3. What prevents the current unit from reaching it (evidenced, not assumed).**
- The implemented information set is not "prices"; it is one scalar series per stock, twice transformed: F=2 = {log return, 21-day rolling std of the same log returns}, both z-scored per window (`src/config.py:134`, `src/data_pipeline/features.py:52-78`, `src/data_pipeline/windowing.py:251-260`).
- Windowing hardcodes exactly two channels (`windowing.py:279-280`); volume/high/low exist in the schema but are never fed to the encoder (`docs/implementation_decisions.md:107`; `src/data_pipeline/data_loader.py:43`).
- No characteristic data exists in the panel at all: the Tiingo merge emits `permno, date, adj_price, volume, exchange_code, share_code, market_cap, delisting_return` (`scripts/download_tiingo.py:903-912`), with `share_code = 10` and `delisting_return = np.nan` for every row (same lines).
- The interpretation half does not exist: no latent-vs-characteristic correlation anywhere in `src`, and no sector/interpretation keys in `results/diagnostic/diagnostic_data.json`; sector labels exist only in the synthetic generator (`data_loader.py:152,188`).
- Per-stock, independent encoding has no cross-sectional axis, so even a characteristic vector would be averaged away inside each stock's own window — the geometry where sectors and styles live is destroyed before any data-type question can matter (`docs/strategic_paths_post_v2.md:107-111`).
- The escape hatch (F>2) has a trigger that requires data the system does not have (`DVT:1143`) — see U02-MAJ-04.

**4. Which constraints are real and which are self-imposed?**
Real: Tiingo's free tier does not supply shares outstanding or sector, so point-in-time characteristics require a new vendor and as-of discipline; quarterly fundamentals are genuinely coarse relative to a 504-day window; 16 GB forbids large hybrid models. Self-imposed: (i) reading "no prior on the factor *basis*" as a prohibition on *any* categorized information anywhere in the system, including batch stratification (`implementation_decisions.md:287-296`); (ii) treating price-only discovery as a settled premise rather than a hypothesis with a test; (iii) choosing F=2 while the same schema lists volume and intraday range as available; (iv) reducing "prices" to close-to-close returns while the argument's own premises (flows, analyst behavior, microstructure) require volume/quote data (`DVT:108-111`); (v) declaring interpretation an optional afterthought and then counting it as a benefit.

**5. What should be removed, merged or moved?**
- **Remove** the "prices vs fundamentals" section as a *data-source* decision. It is an information-set/identification decision and belongs with the exposure model (merge into U09/U05).
- **Remove** the claim "fundamentals for interpretation" until an interpretation artifact exists; today it is rhetorical cover for the exclusion (no code, no data, no diagnostic, no roadmap step).
- **Remove** F=2 as "two features". Either delete the vol channel (it is a function of the return channel) or replace it with a genuinely independent signal (volume, high-low range). Keeping both as-is inflates the apparent information content.
- **Remove** the "80 observations are insufficient" argument (false, see MAJ-01) and the `price × avg_volume × 20` market-cap proxy (`download_tiingo.py:890-901`), which is the only "fundamental-like" quantity in the system and is fabricated.
- **Merge** discovery and interpretation into one identified model: characteristics as *instruments* for exposures, factors from returns (IPCA-shaped). Discovery remains unconstrained; the basis acquires an anchor.
- **Move** sector/industry labels from "rejected prior" to "diagnostic instrument": use them after the fact for a permutation test of factor alignment; never as an input to the latent basis.
- **Move** the dormant-risk burden out of the data choice and into the covariance estimator (structural prior / stress scenarios / robust entropy), since a variance-based risk model cannot express unobserved risk no matter how much price history it is given.

## Root-cause hypotheses

**RC1 — The information set was decided at the ingestion layer instead of the exposure layer.**
The unit asks "prices or fundamentals" as a data-source question. The operative question is "which variables identify exposures, and which estimate co-movement". Because the decision is made upstream of the model, the actual information set is never specified in the theory and is settled de facto by `windowing.py` (2 channels). This one cause produces CRI-02, MAJ-01, MAJ-05 and makes MIN-03 unresolvable. Symptoms appear as an architecture that cannot accept any new information without being rebuilt.
*Structural alternatives:* (a) **Characteristic-instrumented factor model** (IPCA-shaped): factors from returns, exposures B as a learned function of point-in-time characteristics — keeps discovery unconstrained, gives identification and interpretability for free; trade-off: needs a point-in-time vendor, and characteristic noise contaminates the basis. (b) **Two-block risk model**: keep the latent statistical block and add an explicit characteristic/industry block with a joint cross-block covariance — the practical hybrid of the industry; trade-off: reintroduces a priori blocks and risks double counting common variance. (c) **Honest price-only with a wider information set** (volume, range, short interest, implied vol), documented as such — trade-off: still no ex-ante structural information, higher dimensionality, more overfitting.

**RC2 — "No a priori bias" is an unfalsifiable commitment, not a testable hypothesis.**
The principle was extended from "don't hard-code the factor basis" to "admit no categorized information anywhere" (MOD-005, even for batching) and the one measurement instrument that could challenge it was never built. Hence CRI-01, MAJ-04, MIN-01, MIN-02: the theory cannot be contradicted by any observation the repository is capable of making.
*Structural alternatives:* (a) **Blinded validation**: labels used only post hoc — permutation test of factor-basis alignment against the sector partition, plus a synthetic bed with known latent ground truth (the generator already assigns sectors, `data_loader.py:152`); trade-off: none of substance, it costs one diagnostic. (b) **Pre-registered falsification**: declare in advance what AU/EP/CS-R² alignment would refute "prices suffice", and grade the run against it; trade-off: forces accepting that the premise may fail. (c) **Reframe the goal** from "unbiased discovery" to "identified discovery", dropping the epistemics claim entirely.

**RC3 — A variance-based risk model can only represent risk that has already varied, so the data-type choice cannot deliver the anti-cyclical objective.**
Every factor's influence flows through λ (philosophy:93-99). Unobserved heterogeneity has no sample variance; adding more of the *same* data cannot create it. This generates CRI-03 and MIN-03, and it is why the strategy's own dormant-risk story rests on an expanding window rather than on information.
*Structural alternatives:* (a) **Structural prior on Σ_z**: shrink the sample factor covariance toward a characteristic-based co-movement prior so unactivated factors inherit plausible variance; trade-off: the prior is exactly the "a priori" the unit rejects, and it must be validated OOS. (b) **Scenario/stress augmentation**: estimate Σ_z on an augmented sample that includes stressed analogues (earlier history, cross-market episodes), or on quantile/tail covariances; trade-off: scenario design becomes the new arbitrary choice. (c) **Distributionally robust factor risk parity**: maximize worst-case entropy over an ambiguity set of factor covariances rather than the point estimate; trade-off: much harder optimization, less transparency, but it is the only one of the three that directly prices estimation error of λ.

**RC4 — Per-stock temporal encoding removes the cross-sectional axis, where characteristics live.**
Even with fundamentals in hand, the current representation z-scores each stock's own window and encodes stocks independently: a characteristic would be averaged inside one stock's time series, and nothing at a given date compares two stocks. This couples U02 to U04/U05/U08 and is why "fundamentals for interpretation" is not merely unimplemented but unrepresentable in the current geometry. Evidence that the cross-sectional structure exists in the data and is missed: PCA factor RP beats the VAE on the identical panel (0.3016 vs 0.3816 Sharpe; `strategy_comparison.csv:2,7`), and the discovered basis is dominated by one direction (81.1% of risk).
*Structural alternatives:* (a) **Move discovery to the cross-section** (FactorVAE / IPCA / PCA) so exposures are estimated jointly for all stocks per date — the repo already scopes this (`strategic_paths_post_v2.md:113-138`); trade-off: major rewrite, unproven at n>3000. (b) **Per-stock encoder for residuals only**: a cross-sectional factor model for common structure plus the VAE on idiosyncratic dynamics; trade-off: two coupled models, harder validation. (c) **Explicit cross-sectional head**: train the encoder additionally on contemporaneous pairwise co-movement targets so the latent geometry is anchored to the cross-section; trade-off: another loss term, another weight to tune, and it does not fix interpretability by itself.

## Verdict

The choice of price data for co-movement estimation is defensible and standard; the *unit as an argument* is not. Section 3 of the DVT is a four-page resolution of a dichotomy that the literature dissolves rather than resolves, built on three incompatible premises (efficiency, fundamentals-see-what-prices-don't, and "prices suffice for co-movement"), decided by decree rather than by test, and closed with a compensating half ("fundamentals for interpretation") that exists nowhere in code, nowhere in the diagnostics, and cannot exist on the dataset that was actually built. Meanwhile the implemented information set is far narrower than the word "prices": one internally normalized scalar series per stock, in which the second "feature" is a rolling standard deviation of the first. The consequences are visible in the one real run — 13% cross-sectional R², one direction carrying 81% of portfolio risk, only 12 of 26 active units carrying signal — and in the repo's own post-mortem converging on an architectural cause while this unit's premise is never subjected to the test its own spec describes and cannot run. The deeper failure is not the choice of prices; it is that the unit conflates an identification problem with a data-source problem, and then protects the choice with an epistemological commitment ("no a priori bias") that the project deliberately made unfalsifiable by stripping every categorized label out of the system, including the batching strata and the validation instruments. U02 should be dissolved: its content belongs partly to the exposure-model design (U05/U09) and partly to a covariance-estimation redesign (U10/U11), and what remains — "we use returns" — is a sentence, not a chapter.

Score: 3/10
