## Five questions

1. **Purpose and original intent.** DVT §4.6 defines the composite risk profile as the aggregation of *all* local latent vectors over a stock's full history (DVT:644–646), with inference stride 1 to remove calendar alignment (DVT:648–652), default simple mean "preserving memory of all historical regimes" (DVT:656), producing a shape-only exposure matrix B that is filtered to active dimensions (DVT:672). ISD MOD-006 adds the operational definition of an active unit: `KL_k > 0.01 nats`, then `AU_max_stat = floor(sqrt(2·N_obs/r_min))` truncation keeping the highest-KL dims (ISD:1642–1650). Intent: a dense, deterministic, history-long, multiple-membership representation whose dimensionality is discovered from the data rather than chosen.

2. **What SOTA would look like.** (i) Dimensionality of a factor model should be chosen by a factor-count test on the *return panel* (Bai & Ng 2002 IC₂; Onatski 2009; Ahn & Horenstein 2013), or by out-of-sample covariance-forecast loss (QLIKE / min-variance variance / turnover), not by an encoder-capacity statistic. "Active units" is a recognized VAE diagnostic introduced by Burda, Grosse & Salakhutdinov (2015) for *capacity utilization* in generative modelling, not for counting risk factors. (ii) If a VAE is used, identification of exposures requires either a linear decode-to-return head (so latent coordinates are loadings up to rotation, resolved by an invariant criterion), or post-hoc betas from regressing returns on estimated factor returns (Connor & Korajczyk 1988; Lettau & Pelger 2020). (iii) The number of factors and the exposure subspace must be invariant to latent rotations — per-coordinate criteria are not. (iv) Aggregation should be an explicit calendar-time kernel with a stated horizon, and exposure estimation error should be propagated (shrinkage) rather than assumed away. (v) The unit should be falsified against a linear baseline on the same risk-model machinery — exactly what the PCA benchmarks exist for.

3. **What prevents it.** The AU criterion is coordinate-wise in a rotationally unidentified latent space (DVT:692 admits the indeterminacy, then declares it resolved *after* selection). In the one real run, AU is not measured at all: 47 of 52 dims exceed 0.01 nats, and AU=26 is exactly the `sqrt(N_eff)` cap, with K set to 2×that cap (`pipeline.py:307–314`), making the reported "50% utilization" tautological. The KL spectrum is flat (2.06–2.67 over the retained 26), so the ranking has no resolution. The aggregation half-life is expressed in windows but applied at stride 1, so the configured "5-year" memory is 60 days. B is then frozen for a six-year OOS.

4. **Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: 504-day windows, ~640–790 stocks, ~30 years, one 6-year OOS fold, 16 GB, and the mathematical non-identifiability of the VAE latent basis. Self-imposed: materializing all stride-1 windows (DVT:648 only counts the 5.6 GB output; the pipeline's own comment says "~32GB for stride=1", `pipeline.py:1998–2001` — streaming inference would avoid it); `K = max(2·AU_max_stat, 10)`; tying `AU_max_stat` to the *risk model's* EWMA half-life; ranking by KL; counting half-life in windows; freezing B; excluding new stocks; threshold 0.01 nats borrowed from image VAEs; `max(au_bound, 2)` and `2·k_onatski`; disabling the only external factor-count check.

5. **What should be removed, merged or moved.** Remove: per-coordinate KL selection and `AU_max_stat` truncation from inference; the duplicate `measure_active_units`; the Bai-Ng post-filter as an inference-side gate; the 50%-utilization health check; the two arbitrary constants in `post_filter_au_bai_ng`. Move: the dimensionality decision to the risk model (one authority: eigenvalue/spike test or OOS covariance loss), so U08 emits the full K-dim B and U09 decides the factor count. Merge: the KL-activity count and the BBP/`n_signal` count into a single reported statistic with the pre-cap AU always shown. Replace: window-count half-life with calendar-time decay (or remove decay per the DVT default); frozen B with per-rebalance re-encoding; the "mean" with an explicitly stated kernel and horizon. Add nothing to inference except invariance.

## Root-cause hypotheses

**RC1 — "Capacity utilization" was promoted to "number of risk factors".** U08's central quantity (AU) answers *how many latent coordinates the encoder uses*, a generative-modelling diagnostic (Burda et al. 2015). The strategy needs *how many common factors the return covariance has*. These are different objects: the real run has 47 used coordinates, 26 retained by a DOF cap, and 12 eigenvalues above the BBP noise edge. Symptoms across units: AU threshold, AU cap, K=2·AU_max, the 50%-utilization check, the Bai-Ng bolt-on, and the entropy objective targeting 26 principal directions of which 14 are noise (U10).
Alternatives: (a) *Emit full K and let the risk model decide* — one factor-count authority via the spiked/BBP test or Onatski on Σ_z, with the VAE contributing only a basis; trade-off: loses a simple "how many factors did the VAE find" story, gains internal consistency and removes three ad-hoc gates. (b) *Choose the dimension by OOS covariance-forecast loss* (QLIKE or realized min-variance variance for AU=2…K); trade-off: expensive, but it is the objective the strategy actually cares about, and it is testable. (c) *Fix K small architecturally* (K=5–15 with a linear decoder), making capacity and factor count the same object by construction; trade-off: gives up the overcomplete-dictionary hypothesis that motivates the VAE.

**RC2 — Coordinate-wise selection in a rotationally unidentified representation.** Per-coordinate KL, coordinate truncation, per-column z-scoring and clipping, and a stability metric that is rotation-invariant all operate on coordinates whose meaning is only defined up to an orthogonal transform. Symptoms: CRI-01, CRI-02, CRI-03, MAJ-11.
Alternatives: (a) *Select a subspace, not coordinates* — e.g., the top-AU left singular subspace of B, or the subspace maximizing cross-sectional explanatory power; invariant by construction; trade-off: the "factor" labels become derived, not encoder-native. (b) *Break the symmetry during training* with an anisotropic/spiked prior or ARD-style per-dimension scales so coordinates are identified up to permutation; trade-off: changes the generative model and must be retrained/validated. (c) *Do not select at all* — keep all K, rely on shrinkage to handle the noise directions; trade-off: the entropy objective then has many near-zero-eigenvalue directions and must be redefined (e.g., entropy only over signal eigenvalues, which would also fix U10).

**RC3 — No link between a univariate generative model and cross-sectional exposures.** The encoder sees one stock's own path; B is shape × total vol; the risk model regresses returns on B post hoc. There is no objective or test that B's columns span the common return variation. Symptoms: CRI-05, MAJ-06, MAJ-08, MAJ-09, the 0.13 average CS R², the 2.74 variance ratio, and the benchmark outcome (every benchmark wins 4/4, `report.json:6–30`).
Alternatives: (a) *Decouple*: let the VAE model factor *returns* (a small number of latent time series), then estimate loadings by time-series regression on the panel — this is the standard, identified route and makes B a fitted beta; trade-off: loses the "shape discovery" claim, gains identification and comparability with PCA. (b) *Make the encoder conditional on the cross-section* (input market/factor context or a cross-sectional embedding); trade-off: a different architecture and training scheme. (c) *Keep self-supervised shape codes but treat them as characteristics*, not exposures, and estimate betas from returns; trade-off: two-stage, but each stage is identifiable and falsifiable.

**RC4 — Parameters inherited across incompatible units and scales.** `half_life` in training-window units applied to stride-1 windows (21×); K=200 in the DVT vs 75 in config vs 52 in the run; 0.01 nats from image VAEs; `r_min=2`; `sqrt(2N/r)` presented as "statistical". Symptoms: CRI-02, CRI-04, MAJ-13, CRI-05's σ-scaling.
Alternatives: (a) express every temporal parameter in calendar days and assert the unit at the call site; (b) derive thresholds from a null model (permutation/bootstrap of the KL spectrum; parallel analysis) instead of imported constants; (c) eliminate the tunables by design (invariant selection, calendar-time aggregation, dimension from the risk model).

**RC5 — Self-referential validation.** The unit's tests compare the implementation to itself; the external cross-check is disabled; the stability metric is invariant to the failure mode; the reported AU is post-truncation. Symptoms: MAJ-07, MAJ-10, MAJ-11, MAJ-12, and the absence of any known-factor AU test in a unit whose spec requires one.
Alternatives: (a) pre-register falsification: does dropping the KL selection (keep all dims, or random 26 of 47) change OOS risk metrics materially? If not, the selection is decorative; (b) negative controls: shuffle stock labels in B, or permute KL rankings, and compare portfolio outcomes; (c) always report the triple (K, AU_precap, n_signal) side by side in the diagnostic, with the pre-cap value as the headline.

## Verdict

This is the most carefully engineered unit I have audited in the repository — deterministic encode-only inference, explicit train/OOS split (A11 verified), stable grouping, memory cleanup, and a clear docstring trail — and its implementation is not the problem. Its theory is. The unit answers a question the strategy does not need ("how many coordinates does the VAE use?") with a criterion that is coordinate-wise in a rotationally unidentified space, then overrides the answer with an arbitrary DOF cap that is algebraically tied to a risk-model smoothing parameter. In the one real run, 47 of 52 dims are "active", AU=26 is exactly the cap, K was defined as 2×the cap (so the 50% "utilization" is a tautology), the KL spectrum has no resolvable cut, and the configured "5-year" aggregation half-life is actually 60 days, leaving 99.7% of the weight inside a single 504-day window. The resulting B_A is a near-arbitrary 26-column subset of a stock-level shape embedding, rescaled by total volatility, frozen for a six-year OOS, excluding every stock that was not already present in training. The risk model's own diagnostics (12 signal eigenvalues of 26, average cross-sectional R² 0.13, variance ratio 2.74) and the benchmark result (all seven benchmarks win 4/4) are consistent with the claim that this exposure matrix is not carrying usable factor risk.
