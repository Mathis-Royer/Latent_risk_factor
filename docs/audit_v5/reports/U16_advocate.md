## Five questions

1. **Purpose and original intent.** This unit exists to convert results into a verdict without self-deception. Its charter is stated as a principle, not a preference: *"if after all iterative improvements the VAE strategy does not statistically outperform the PCA factor risk parity baseline across walk-forward folds, the intellectual elegance of the architecture does not justify its operational complexity"* (`docs/strategy_philosophy.md:183`). `docs/strategic_paths_post_v2.md:5` operationalises it: *"The clean run (Phase 1) must complete first. The metrics from Phase 1 determine which path to follow."* So the intended output is a **pre-registered, falsifiable decision**, not a narrative.

2. **What a state-of-the-art version would look like.** Pre-registration with a named test statistic, power calculation and minimum training budget; multiplicity control (Holm/BH) that is *reported with the corrected number*; effect sizes with confidence intervals rather than percent changes; a "value of complexity" hurdle relative to the strongest simple benchmark (DeMiguel-Garlappi-Uppal 2009; Harvey-Liu-Zhu 2016 on t>3; Bailey-López de Prado 2014 on deflated Sharpe); a claim registry where every quantitative statement carries `artifact → code path → hash/date`; and a distinction between *fault* hypotheses (scale, solver, data) and *architecture* hypotheses, tested with identifying variation (one change at a time). On the evidence of this repo, most of this is already built; what is missing is disciplined **use**.

3. **What prevents it.** (a) The evidence base collapsed: the only multi-fold artifact is a 1-epoch smoke test (`results/report.json:270-286,287`), and the only real-data artifact has **one fold and zero statistical tests** (`results/diagnostic/report.json:41,153`). (b) The automated verdict is produced by a rule that misreads missing data as a tie (`src/walk_forward/selection.py:111-136`). (c) The flagship post-mortem's three load-bearing numbers are unverifiable or arithmetically impossible (findings 08, 15, 16). (d) The causal story misdescribes the loss (finding 10). **Obstacles that are only apparent:** the repo does *not* lack a statistical protocol (Holm is implemented and applied), does *not* lack comparable benchmarks (INV-012 tests exist), and the encoder is *not* blind to cross-sectional structure (the co-movement loss is explicitly pairwise across stocks). The gap is between instruments and their use, not between ambition and machinery.

4. **Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: 30 years of daily data ⇒ at most ~34 non-overlapping-ish folds and low power; non-stationarity; compute for 5h/fold; no CRSP licence. Self-imposed: choosing **Sharpe alone** as the decision metric; accepting `E*=1` without questioning the selection criterion; reporting a single-fold outcome as a verdict; defining "success" for Path A as `Sharpe > 0`; widening the variance-ratio band from 2.0 to 3.0; maintaining two parallel decision rules; arguing cardinality for a 20,000-stock universe that no artifact contains.

5. **What should be removed, merged or moved.** *Remove:* the scenario letter and one-line recommendation from `report.json` (the per-benchmark win table is the real content; the letter is the part that inverts the evidence); the `p_corrected` field until it actually holds a corrected p; Path A's predicted CS R² ranges ("15–20%", "2–5 pp"); the "86% worse" figure; the "20,000 stocks" premise; the "1 eigenvalue above MP edge" sentence; one of the two contradictory persistence-weighting recommendations. *Merge:* the two decision rules (pre-registered matrix and automated scenario) into one gated rule that refuses to emit a verdict when `n_tests = 0`. *Move:* all numeric claims out of prose into a generated claims table keyed to artifact + git SHA; "per-fix confirmed" language into a hypothesis register until ablated. None of these removals sacrifices a strength: the honest per-metric table, the Holm machinery, the kill criterion and the divergence register all survive untouched.

## Core strengths to preserve

1. **The kill criterion.** A named comparator (PCA-FRP), a statistical predicate and an ex-ante commitment to abandon the VAE (`docs/strategy_philosophy.md:183`) is the single most valuable artifact in the repo. It is what makes the whole project falsifiable. Any redesign must keep it and add only the missing operational clauses (statistic, minimum `E*`, minimum folds, no verdict when `n_tests = 0`).
2. **Multiplicity control, correctly implemented.** Holm step-down with monotone rejection and stored `n_tests`/`alpha` (`src/integration/statistical_tests.py:116-129`, `src/integration/reporting.py:99-114`) is exactly the right instrument for a 6-benchmark × 3-metric family. Do not replace it with "p<0.05 per comparison".
3. **Metric-complete, self-incriminating reporting.** Publishing top-1 factor contribution 81.1%, ENB 2.70/12, H_norm_signal 0.399 and the benchmark table where the strategy loses to 1/N (`results/diagnostic/diagnostic_report.md:11-22,92-95,113-120`) is the behaviour a redesign must preserve. It is the reason this audit can be written at all.
4. **Pre-registered decision tree with costs and risks.** `docs/strategic_paths_post_v2.md` commits to entry conditions, expected impacts, explicit failure risks, effort estimates and a stated update trigger (`:5,11-18,96-110,190-198,311`). Keep the structure; delete the numbers that were never generated.
5. **The divergence register and the "code is the reference" rule.** `docs/divergences.md:23` resolves spec conflicts in favour of the tested implementation and lists what needs documenting. That governance instinct — documents are history, code is truth — is correct and should be extended from *spec* divergences to *claim* divergences (every number gets a producing code path).
6. **The discipline of refusing complexity.** The analysis document explicitly rejects semi-variance, persistence-weighted entropy, dropping factor entropy for MDP, and "adding ML layers to correct perceived defects" absent measurable OOS gain (`docs/analyse_critique_strategie.md:488-493`). For a 34-agent redesign this is a load-bearing prior: it forces every proposal to name its OOS test.
7. **Fix-level reversal as a habit.** Reverting one's own changes and recording what was kept and why (`docs/strategic_paths_post_v2.md:285-307`) is good scientific hygiene in intent. Keep the reversals; keep the "kept because harmless/beneficial" annotations.

## Verdict
