## Five questions

**1. Purpose and original intent (per spec).** §4.4 is framed as "the loss function determines what the latent space captures", built in layers: mean-MSE reconstruction (DVT:446-448), KL to `N(0,1)` (DVT:454), a learned scalar σ² to absorb the recon/KL balance (DVT:458-468), three exclusive modes so that β and σ² never compete (DVT:478-492), a transient Spearman↔cosine "scaffolding" with a 3-phase curriculum (DVT:494-540), crisis up-weighting γ (DVT:544-582), and a validation ELBO excluding γ and λ_co (DVT:608-622). MOD-004 restates it as "the mathematical core … any error here silently invalidates training" (ISD:1293) and elevates `D=T×F` to INV-001, "the most critical error in the entire pipeline" (ISD:1435). Code adds two terms absent from both specs: per-feature reconstruction weights and a cross-sectional R² loss (loss.py:6-11, 137-278).

**2. State of the art for this objective.** The objective the strategy actually needs is a *conditional factor model* — `r_t = B f_t + ε_t` with `f_t` shared across stocks per date — estimated with a heavy-tailed, heteroscedastic likelihood. SOTA financial VAEs use Student-t observation models with time-varying scale (Alegre et al. 2022, FactorVAE; RVRAE), temporal priors (Sicks et al. 2023, TempVAE), and factor-level disentanglement (β-TCVAE / FactorVAE) — not a Gaussian with one global scalar σ². On the recon/KL weight, the literature has a name for "learn the scalar variance instead of tuning β": auto-β (Burgess et al. 2018), and its known pathology is exactly what is here — the target level is arbitrary, so the effective regularisation is set by a nuisance parameter. A relational term should operate on the *shared* latent `f_t` (per date), not on pairwise cosine distances between per-stock codes. Any benchmark against PCA/Bai-Ng should be an explicit likelihood comparison, not an "AU" count.

**3. What prevents this unit from reaching it (evidenced).** (a) The crisis term — the only regime-aware element — is inert in every executable path (U06-CRI-01). (b) The "scaffolding" never anneals in the one recorded real-data run (CRI-09). (c) The single adaptive parameter σ², which is supposed to replace β tuning and determine factor count, is recorded nowhere, and its only runtime check is a tautology (CRI-10). (d) Two extra terms were bolted on outside the spec, one of them self-referential and now disabled (CRI-03), the other consulted in no artefact and reported "neutral" (CRI-05). (e) The loss's complexity budget `D=T·F` silently sets the number of factors (CRI-13), so "data-dependent AU" is a configuration artefact.

**4. Real vs self-imposed constraints.** *Real*: Spearman is non-differentiable (hence target-only); daily equity returns are heavy-tailed; VIX is not in the Tiingo extract; a 504×2 window on a 16 GB machine bounds batch size. *Self-imposed*: the absence of *any* crisis proxy — the spec itself authorises a realised-volatility proxy for pre-1990 data (DVT:558), so using one today is permitted and was simply never wired; the Gaussian likelihood with a scalar σ²; the three-mode taxonomy; the hidden `D/2` rescaling; the choice to encode cross-sectional structure through a pairwise loss instead of the architecture; per-window z-scoring that destroys the volatility level; and the practice of fixing every loss hyperparameter while claiming they are validated within walk-forward (CRI-16).

**5. What should be removed, merged or moved.**
- *Remove* the crisis weighting from the loss (γ), and with it the `crisis_fractions` plumbing and its four monitoring rules; if regime emphasis is wanted, apply it as a sampling weight or a risk-model conditioning variable, where it is measurable.
- *Remove* the cross-sectional R² loss entirely, together with `lambda_cs`, `cs_n_sample_dates`, its 3 tests and diagnostic plumbing. Its failure is structural, not a tuning issue, and the proposed "fix" (full-universe μ) does not remove the self-reference.
- *Merge* modes P/F/A into one loss with one KL coefficient and one explicit statement of how the coefficient is set; delete `beta_fixed`, `warmup_fraction`, `get_beta_t` and INV-006. `Mode P ≡ Mode F` with `β_eff = 2σ²` (CRI-04).
- *Remove* the per-feature weights in their current form; if channels must be balanced, make them the likelihood's own parameters (`σ²_f` per channel, estimated) instead of an undeclared tilt.
- *Move* the co-movement objective out of the loss and into the architecture (per-date shared latent), and delete the co-movement loss together with the `CurriculumBatchSampler` and the λ_co schedule — that also removes the k-means stratification dependency.
- *Move* "how many factors" out of σ² and into an explicit, pre-registered model-selection criterion shared with U05/U08/U09.

## Findings

*Steelman.* The unit is the most carefully implemented part of the repository: the `D=T×F` coefficient, the KL formula, the γ_eff formula, the curriculum boundaries and the P/F/A assemblies all match the spec to the letter, and 25+ unit tests pin them (test_loss_function.py:61-1021). Learned σ² is a genuinely defensible way to avoid a β sweep, and the doc correctly warns against combining β annealing with learned variance (DVT:480, ISD:1437) — a trap most VAE implementations fall into. *Rebuttal:* correctness of transcription is not correctness of design. Of the five mechanisms in the unit, one is provably inert in every run (γ), one is mis-scaled against its own spec by 504× (λ_co), one is self-referential by construction (L_cs), one is a re-parameterisation of another (P≡F), and the only adaptive quantity (σ²) is both unrecorded and guarded by a tautology. The spec's own numbers (bimodality, η table, "validated within walk-forward") are not what happens.

## Root-cause hypotheses

**RC1 — A conditional per-stock generative model is being asked to do a cross-sectional job, and the loss is used as the place to patch the gap.** Every add-on in this unit (L_co, L_cs, γ) exists to inject structure that the model class cannot represent: a shared per-date factor. Evidence: `N(0,I)` prior (loss.py:107-130) implies pairwise correlation 0 (CRI-14); L_cs is self-referential (CRI-03); L_co is a soft force toward a common cone (CRI-14); the risk model then needs an explicit market intercept to compensate (config `market_intercept=true`). This is the systemic version of the unit's problems and spans U05/U06/U09.
- *A1*: per-date shared latent (factor-VAE / CVAE): `z_t` common to all stocks at date t, per-stock amortised inference. Trade-off: correct generative semantics, real cross-sectional training signal; universe must be fixed per forward pass (the doc's "universe flexibility" objection, DVT:542, is answerable with permutation-invariant encoders and masking).
- *A2*: panel encoder with masked reconstruction (one pass over a date's cross-section, missingness as a mask). Trade-off: handles the 35% missing data natively, kills the need for a Spearman surrogate; cost is memory and a harder training loop.
- *A3*: keep the per-stock encoder and derive factors downstream only (PCA/ICA rotation of B), deleting L_co and L_cs. Trade-off: minimal compute, honest, but the VAE then adds nothing beyond a nonlinear feature map — which is itself the falsifiable claim the project should test.

**RC2 — The loss has no declared estimation target, so every weight becomes a free scalar and the knobs proliferate.** Nothing in the objective references the quantities the strategy needs (B, f_t, Σ_z); the recon/KL weight is set by an estimated nuisance parameter (σ²), the cross-sectional weight by a hidden `D/2`, the crisis weight by an unused label. Evidence: CRI-02, CRI-04, CRI-05, CRI-13.
- *B1*: one loss, one explicit KL coefficient, fixed by an outer criterion tied to the downstream objective (OOS CS R² / variance-target calibration), not to a likelihood artefact. Trade-off: reintroduces a hyperparameter, but a *measurable* one, validated in the same walk-forward.
- *B2*: separate the observation model per channel with estimated `σ²_f` and a Student-t (ν fixed or estimated) likelihood; delete all ad-hoc reweighting. Trade-off: more parameters, but each interpretable and testable against residuals; loss of the "no hyperparameter" story, which is a story, not a property.
- *B3*: gradient-norm balancing (GradNorm / Kendall uncertainty weighting) instead of value-matching heuristics like `D/2`. Trade-off: addresses the actual failure mode of multi-term losses; adds its own instability.

**RC3 — Inductive bias is imposed through additive penalties instead of through the data representation.** γ, feature weights, z-scoring and the cosine target all pre-judge what matters (crisis, returns over vol, shape over level, distance↔correlation). Evidence: CRI-05, CRI-06, CRI-07, CRI-14.
- *C1*: put regime information in the *input* (a regime indicator or estimated conditional volatility as a third channel, per the spec's own realised-vol proxy idea, DVT:558) and let reconstruction weight it. Trade-off: no separate knob, but the model must then be shown to use it.
- *C2*: keep the inputs pure (returns and log-vol, no per-window standardisation of level) and let a heteroscedastic likelihood handle scale. Trade-off: restores the risk-relevant level information, makes σ² meaningful; harder optimisation.
- *C3*: drop all pairwise scaffolding and evaluate cross-sectional structure only out-of-sample through the risk model's variance ratio / explanatory power. Trade-off: one clean falsification channel instead of a training-time surrogate.

**RC4 — No falsification infrastructure for loss components; components accumulate and cannot be retired.** Evidence: γ has no producer (CRI-01), L_cs disabled with a doc-only verdict and its code retained (CRI-03), feature weights "neutral" but shipped (CRI-05), effective λ unrecorded and 504× off-spec (CRI-02, CRI-17), σ² never saved (CRI-10). The unit therefore drifts by accretion of undeclared changes to a spec that still claims authority ("DO NOT modify", ISD:1340, 1435-1440).
- *D1*: make each loss term a switchable component with a mandatory ablation in every fold report (term on/off × OOS metric), and delete any term that is not ablated. Trade-off: compute cost; forces honesty.
- *D2*: freeze the loss as a single documented equation, with the effective weights (post-rescaling) logged and asserted, and a hard fail if a documented quantity differs from the implemented one. Trade-off: less freedom to tinker.
- *D3*: move all regime/relational logic out of the loss into the risk model, where it is directly measurable (conditioning, shrinkage, factor selection). Trade-off: simpler loss, more burden on U09.

## Verdict

The documented mathematics is transcribed faithfully — D factor, KL, γ_eff, curriculum boundaries, P/F/A assembly — and that is exactly what makes the unit dangerous: it looks verified while five of its mechanisms are structurally unsound. The crisis weighting, which carries the strategy's "crisis-aware" claim, has **never been active** in any run because no entry point supplies VIX (CRI-01), and even if it were, the calibration rests on a bimodality claim that is false for 504-day windows (CRI-07). The co-movement scaffolding is a geometric surrogate whose target geometry is infeasible and contradicts the model's own prior, and it was never annealed away in the one recorded run (CRI-09, CRI-14). The cross-sectional R² term is self-referential by construction and is now dead weight in the code (CRI-03). The three loss modes are one knob in three costumes, and the doc's stated reason for their exclusivity is algebraically backwards (CRI-04). Most consequentially, the single quantity that governs how many factors the strategy gets — learned σ² — is unrecorded, unguarded, and, through `D=T·F`, makes factor count a function of window length rather than of the market (CRI-10, CRI-13). None of this can be blamed on tuning: it is a design in which cross-sectional structure, regime awareness and model complexity are all injected as scalar weights on a per-stock reconstruction objective, and none of those weights can be measured, validated or retired. The required move is subtractive: one likelihood, one declared KL coefficient, no per-feature or crisis multipliers, no R² term, no pairwise scaffolding — and cross-sectional structure built into the latent model where U05/U09 can see it.

Score: 3/10
