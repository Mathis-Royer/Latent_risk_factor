## Five questions
1. **Purpose and original intent.** MOD-007 is meant to turn the VAE exposure matrix into "a complete factor risk model: dual rescaling, factor estimation via cross-sectional OLS, Σ_z estimation (Ledoit-Wolf), D_ε estimation, Σ_assets assembly" (`docs/ISD_vae_latent_risk_factors.md:1688`). Σ_z is the empirical covariance of \(\hat z_t\) "over the FULL history" with Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage (`:1750-1754`), and the reader is told "DO NOT compute Σ_z on a rolling window — use the FULL history" (`:1800`). The DVT states the same full-history/Ledoit-Wolf contract (`docs/DVT_strategie_facteurs_risque_latents_v4_1.md:752`) and adds a conditioning ridge when \(\kappa(\tilde B^T\tilde B)>10^6\) (`:759`). The intent is a stable, anti-cyclical, unconditional factor covariance that supports entropy risk parity.
2. **State of the art for this objective.** A production equity factor risk model (Barra USE4 / Axioma) estimates factor covariance with exponential weighting (the repo itself cites 252-day half-life: `src/risk_model/covariance.py:210-213`), applies serial-correlation corrections, shrinks the covariance with a documented linear/nonlinear estimator (Ledoit–Wolf 2004/2020, cited at `covariance.py:11-16`), models specific risk conditionally (EWMA + Bayesian/size shrinkage), and validates forecast accuracy out of sample with a covariance loss — the repo implements only a realized/predicted variance ratio (`src/walk_forward/metrics.py:137-155`; target [0.8,1.2] at `:143`). For risk parity, the risk model must be a forecast of *current* risk, not a long-run average: Meucci/Roncalli risk contributions are conditional on the covariance used at the rebalance.
3. **What prevents it.** The implemented estimator is not the specified one (DGJ "spiked" instead of Ledoit–Wolf); the covariance is estimated on 80% of the history, not full history; the EWMA path uses two different `gamma` values for signal detection and shrinkage; the signal slice is off by one and is applied after VT re-orders eigenvalues; VT is clamped to [0.5,2]; the conditioning ridge is a no-op; and the model fails its own calibration test (var_ratio 2.742). Details and citations below.
4. **Real vs self-imposed constraints.** Real: finite sample, non-stationarity, 788-stock Tiingo universe, 35.3% missing data (`results/diagnostic/diagnostic_report.md:30`), long-only 3% cap, 16 GB compute. Self-imposed: equal-weight full-history covariance as doctrine (`docs/sigma_z_ewma_justification.md:15`), `sigma_z_ewma_half_life=0` in config (`src/config.py:458`) but 252 in the only real run (`results/diagnostic/config.json:68`), DGJ rather than LW (`config.json:66`), 80/20 in-sample split (`pipeline.py:2243-2251`), VT clamp [0.5,2] (`config.py:465-466`), exclusion of PC1 from the entropy budget (`pipeline.py:2292-2303`), hard `n_signal` cutoff, and \(\alpha=0.001\) for the entropy term (`diagnostic_data.json:167`).
5. **What should be removed, merged or moved.** Remove DGJ and the silent ANL→DGJ fallback; remove the PC1-skip hack; remove the hard `n_signal` cutoff; remove the 80/20 in-sample VT split; remove the [0.5,2] clamp; remove the coupling of `sigma_z_ewma_half_life` to `compute_au_max_stat`/K; merge the two VT implementations into one; merge the three shrinkage configurations (doc LW, config ANL, run DGJ) into one recorded method; move risk-model validation into an independent pre-registered gate (variance ratio/QLIKE) that runs before portfolio construction; move the "market" decision into an explicit beta constraint or benchmark rather than a PCA label.

## Root-cause hypotheses

**RC1 — The unit is organized around an unfalsifiable "anti-cyclical full-history" doctrine rather than a forecast objective.** Symptoms: EWMA half-life 0 in config vs 252 in the only run; the "full history" is 80%; VT is clamped to preserve memory; `var_ratio=2.742`; the doc defends equal weights with a mean-estimation result. The doctrine makes no prediction that could fail: any poor OOS risk forecast can be attributed to "the market being cyclical" rather than to the estimator. Structural alternatives: (a) **Forecast-loss architecture** — estimate Σ_t with EWMA/DCC/regime mixture, choose the half-life by rolling out-of-sample QLIKE/variance-ratio, and report the selected value; trade-off: more parameters and less "permanent crisis memory," but falsifiable. (b) **Two-model architecture** — a strategic long-run covariance for policy risk budgeting and a tactical conditional covariance for portfolio construction, with an explicit mapping and separate validation; trade-off: complexity and possible inconsistency, but separates two objectives that are currently conflated. (c) **Long-horizon objective** — keep full-history covariance but change the evaluation and benchmark to long-horizon average risk contributions, not short-horizon OOS Sharpe; trade-off: coherent but no longer a tactical strategy.

**RC2 — Statistical signal detection is conflated with economic factor selection and risk budgeting.** Symptoms: DGJ `n_signal` used as the number of bets; PC1 skipped on an untested label; off-by-one slice; EWMA/raw `gamma` mismatch; bulk eigenvalues rescaled by VT; 81% top-factor risk with ENB 2.7. Structural alternatives: (a) **Keep all factors with an eigenvalue-proportional budget** (the tilted-entropy `budget` argument already exists in `entropy.py:47-59,117`), eliminating the hard signal/noise cutoff; trade-off: noise factors receive budget, but the budget is smooth and does not jump when `n_signal` changes. (b) **Explicit factor selection with stability/economic criteria** — Bai–Ng/Onatski plus bootstrap stability and interpretability, with the market included as a named factor; trade-off: more rigorous, but complex and may yield few factors. (c) **Portfolio-level diversification objective** — drop factor-level entropy and use ERC/diversification ratio on the full Σ_assets; trade-off: loses the "latent factor" story but the objective is well-defined without factor identification.

**RC3 — The estimator pipeline is a chain of unvalidated substitutions and patches.** Symptoms: LW→DGJ, ridge→lstsq, full→80/20, [0.01,100]→[0.5,2], in-sample→per-factor VT, signal detection decoupled from shrinkage, config default vs function default vs run config. Structural alternatives: (a) **Single estimator contract** — one covariance estimator, one calibration method, one validation gate, recorded in config and results; trade-off: less flexibility, but auditable. (b) **Method registry with pre-registered selection** — each estimator declares its assumptions and the selection rule is fixed before seeing OOS; trade-off: engineering overhead, but no silent fallbacks. (c) **Independent risk-model gate** — a separate module recomputes Σ_z, `var_ratio`, and ENB from saved artifacts and blocks the pipeline on failure; trade-off: may block the strategy, which is the point.

**RC4 — "Statistical capacity" is confused with "model capacity," and one hyperparameter controls several objects.** Symptoms: `sigma_z_ewma_half_life` → \(n_{\text{eff}}\) → AU_max → K; K=52 vs configured 75; AU=26; pretrained checkpoint mismatch; the justification doc calls this "coherence." Structural alternatives: (a) **Decouple** — estimate K/AU from VAE validation (ELBO, downstream risk-model OOS), make the covariance weighting a separate parameter; trade-off: more hyperparameters, each with a clear objective. (b) **Fixed K, data-driven AU** — fix K by architecture, select AU by incremental OOS explanatory power or portfolio usefulness; trade-off: more computation, but avoids the statistical artifact. (c) **VAE as feature extractor, risk model selects factors** — choose the number of factors by cross-validated PCA/Bai–Ng on `z_hat`, independent of K; trade-off: may discard VAE dimensions, but cleaner separation.

**RC5 — The evaluation layer is not independent of the model and uses low/inconsistent bars.** Symptoms: H_norm_signal=0.399 marked OK; top-1=81% reported next to H=0.992; ENB=2.7; "max entropy" ln(27) vs signal-only denominator; pretrained model with no training diagnostics; config/checkpoint mismatch. Structural alternatives: (a) **Pre-register hard gates** — e.g., H_norm_signal≥0.7, ENB≥0.5·n_signal, var_ratio∈[0.8,1.2]; trade-off: the strategy may fail, which is the purpose of a gate. (b) **One decomposition per metric** — label full/signal/idio and never mix denominators; trade-off: less impressive numbers, but honest. (c) **Independent replication** — a separate agent recomputes Σ_z, var_ratio, and ENB from saved artifacts without the pipeline's diagnostics; trade-off: effort, but catches definitional bugs.

## Verdict
